- GLP-1 receptor agonists (e.g. semaglutide)
	- Delay gastric emptying
	- Increase aspiration risk during sedation/anaesthesia
- Elective gastroscopy/colonoscopy
	- Recent semaglutide use
		- Postpone procedure if concern for delayed gastric emptying

	- Highest aspiration risk
- Bowel preparation
	- Does not eliminate delayed gastric emptying risk
