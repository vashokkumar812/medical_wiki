[[leukemia and lymphoma]]

Fluctuating (appearing and disappearing) [cervical lymphadenopathy](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=d9be14ba-b585-47cb-9440-dd52ae7c3774#) over months in an adult strongly favors an indolent [lymphoma](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=d9be14ba-b585-47cb-9440-dd52ae7c3774#), especially [follicular lymphoma](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=d9be14ba-b585-47cb-9440-dd52ae7c3774#).

- [Follicular lymphoma](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=d9be14ba-b585-47cb-9440-dd52ae7c3774#) classically shows waxing and waning [lymph node](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=d9be14ba-b585-47cb-9440-dd52ae7c3774#) enlargement and slow progression.
    
- [Hodgkin lymphoma](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=d9be14ba-b585-47cb-9440-dd52ae7c3774#) (HL) typically presents with progressive, contiguous [lymphadenopathy](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=d9be14ba-b585-47cb-9440-dd52ae7c3774#) (often cervical/supraclavicular) and may have [B symptoms](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=d9be14ba-b585-47cb-9440-dd52ae7c3774#) ([fever](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=d9be14ba-b585-47cb-9440-dd52ae7c3774#), night sweats, weight loss).
    
- [Chronic lymphocytic leukemia](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=d9be14ba-b585-47cb-9440-dd52ae7c3774#) ([CLL](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=d9be14ba-b585-47cb-9440-dd52ae7c3774#)/[SLL](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=d9be14ba-b585-47cb-9440-dd52ae7c3774#)) causes persistent painless [lymphadenopathy](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=d9be14ba-b585-47cb-9440-dd52ae7c3774#), often generalized, sometimes with [hepatosplenomegaly](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=d9be14ba-b585-47cb-9440-dd52ae7c3774#) and cytopenias.
    
- [Acute lymphoblastic leukemia](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=d9be14ba-b585-47cb-9440-dd52ae7c3774#) ([ALL](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=d9be14ba-b585-47cb-9440-dd52ae7c3774#)) presents acutely with cytopenias, fatigue, bleeding, infections, and sometimes [lymphadenopathy](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=d9be14ba-b585-47cb-9440-dd52ae7c3774#)—but is rapidly progressive, not intermittent.
    
- [EBV infection](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=d9be14ba-b585-47cb-9440-dd52ae7c3774#) ([infectious mononucleosis](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=d9be14ba-b585-47cb-9440-dd52ae7c3774#)) causes tender, often [self-limited](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=d9be14ba-b585-47cb-9440-dd52ae7c3774#) [cervical lymphadenopathy](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=d9be14ba-b585-47cb-9440-dd52ae7c3774#) with [fever](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=d9be14ba-b585-47cb-9440-dd52ae7c3774#), [pharyngitis](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=d9be14ba-b585-47cb-9440-dd52ae7c3774#), and [splenomegaly](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=d9be14ba-b585-47cb-9440-dd52ae7c3774#).
