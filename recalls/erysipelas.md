Q2. Kid's picture of ear. one ear was red behind. 4 hr. history of mild itching

1. Acute otitis externa.
    
2. Cellulitis
    
3. Erysipelas
    

Itching is an early symptom of otitis externa before the development of pain/discharge.

Cellulitis: tender and painful

Erysipelas: well demarcated raised, red painful plaque


| Feature | Erysipelas | Cellulitis |
|---|---|---|
| Layer | Upper dermis + lymphatics | Deep dermis + subcutaneous tissue |
| Border | **Well-demarcated, raised** | **Ill-defined, flat** |
| Onset | Sudden | Gradual |
| Appearance | Bright red, shiny | Diffuse red |
| Systemic features | More prominent (fever) | Variable |
| Common organism | GAS (*Streptococcus pyogenes*) | GAS + *Staphylococcus aureus* |
| Key AMC clue | **Raised, sharply defined edge** | **Poorly defined spreading erythema** |
