- Pregnancy Testing After IVF (In Vitro Fertilisation)

	- First-line investigation
		- Quantitative **serum β-hCG (Beta Human Chorionic Gonadotropin)**

	- Timing
		- **12–14 days after ET (Embryo Transfer)**

	- Avoid
		- **Urine Pregnancy Test (UPT)**
			- Less sensitive
			- False-negative if performed too early

	- If **serum β-hCG** positive
		- Repeat **serum β-hCG** after **48 hours**
		- Normal pregnancy
			- β-hCG approximately doubles every 48 hours during early pregnancy
		- Abnormal rise
			- Consider ectopic pregnancy
			- Consider failing pregnancy

- Confirm Pregnancy

	- First-line
		- **TVUS (Transvaginal Ultrasound)**

	- Timing
		- **6–7 weeks gestation**

	- Assess
		- Intrauterine pregnancy
		- Fetal cardiac activity
		- Number of fetuses
		- Exclude ectopic pregnancy

- Dating Pregnancy

	- First-line
		- Use **ET (Embryo Transfer) date**
		- IVF dates are more accurate than **LMP (Last Menstrual Period)**

	- Avoid
		- Redating using **LMP** if IVF dates are known

- Vaginal Bleeding or Pelvic Pain

	- First-line investigations
		- **Serum β-hCG (Beta Human Chorionic Gonadotropin)**
		- **TVUS (Transvaginal Ultrasound)**

	- Consider
		- Ectopic pregnancy
		- Miscarriage
		- Heterotopic pregnancy

- Ectopic Pregnancy

	- Higher risk after **IVF (In Vitro Fertilisation)**

	- Suspect if
		- Pelvic pain
		- Vaginal bleeding
		- Slowly rising **β-hCG**
		- No intrauterine pregnancy on **TVUS**

	- First-line investigations
		- **Serum β-hCG**
		- **TVUS**

	- Stable patient
		- Methotrexate if appropriate

	- Unstable patient
		- Emergency laparoscopy

- Heterotopic Pregnancy

	- Definition
		- Simultaneous intrauterine pregnancy + ectopic pregnancy

	- Increased after **IVF**

	- Remember
		- Seeing an intrauterine pregnancy on **TVUS** does NOT exclude ectopic pregnancy

- OHSS (Ovarian Hyperstimulation Syndrome)

	- Cause
		- Excess ovarian response during IVF stimulation

	- Risk factors
		- **PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome)**
		- Young age
		- High estradiol
		- Pregnancy after IVF

	- Symptoms
		- Abdominal pain
		- Bloating
		- Vomiting
		- Rapid weight gain
		- Dyspnoea
		- Oliguria

	- First-line investigations
		- **FBC (Full Blood Count)**
		- **U&E (Urea and Electrolytes)**
		- **LFT (Liver Function Tests)**
		- **TVUS**

	- Severe disease
		- Hospital admission
		- IV fluids
		- Thromboprophylaxis

- Ovarian Torsion

	- Increased risk after IVF

	- Symptoms
		- Sudden unilateral pelvic pain
		- Vomiting

	- First-line investigation
		- **TVUS (Transvaginal Ultrasound) with Doppler**

	- First-line management
		- Emergency laparoscopy

- Antenatal Care

	- Increased risk of
		- **GDM (Gestational Diabetes Mellitus)**
		- Pre-eclampsia
		- Preterm birth
		- Multiple pregnancy

	- Screen
		- Routine antenatal screening
		- **OGTT (Oral Glucose Tolerance Test)** for GDM
		- Blood pressure and urine protein for pre-eclampsia

- Aneuploidy Screening

	- First-line
		- **NIPT (Non-Invasive Prenatal Testing)**
		- First-trimester combined screening

	- Remember
		- IVF does NOT replace fetal aneuploidy screening

- Delivery

	- First-line
		- Vaginal delivery if obstetrically appropriate

	- Avoid
		- Caesarean section solely because pregnancy resulted from IVF

- AMC High-Yield One-Liners

	- Pregnancy test after **IVF (In Vitro Fertilisation)** → **Serum β-hCG (Beta Human Chorionic Gonadotropin)**
	- Pregnancy confirmation → **TVUS (Transvaginal Ultrasound)** at **6–7 weeks**
	- Pregnancy dating → **ET (Embryo Transfer)** date, not **LMP (Last Menstrual Period)**
	- Pelvic pain after IVF → Exclude ectopic pregnancy
	- Positive intrauterine pregnancy on **TVUS** ≠ Excludes heterotopic pregnancy
	- Most important early complication → **OHSS (Ovarian Hyperstimulation Syndrome)**
	- Sudden unilateral pelvic pain after IVF → Ovarian torsion until proven otherwise
	- IVF pregnancy alone ≠ Indication for Caesarean section
