

- Definition
	- Autoimmune symmetrical polyarticular systemic disease
	- Unknown aetiology


- Joints involved

	- Hands
		- MCP joints
		- PIP joints


	- Wrist and elbows

	- Feet
		- MTP joints
		- Tarsal joints
		- Not IP joints
		- Ankle

	- Knees
		- Common

	- Hip
		- Delayed


	- Shoulder
		- Glenohumeral joints

	- Temporomandibular joints

	- Cervical spine
		- Not lumbar spine

	- Insidious onset
		- May be acute
			- Explosive RA

- Clinical features


	- Joint symptoms

		- Pain
			- Worse on waking

		- Nocturnal pain

		- Disturbed sleep

		- Relieved with activity

		- Stiffness
			- Morning stiffness lasting hours
			- Rest stiffness

	- General

		- Malaise

		- Weakness

		- Weight loss

		- Fatigue

		- Disability
			- Depending on involvement

	- Signs
	
		- Soft swelling
			- Effusion
			- Synovial swelling
			- Especially
				- Wrist
				- MCP
				- PIP

		- Warmth
	
		- Tenderness
	
		- Limitation of movement
	
		- Muscle wasting
	
		- Late features
	
			- Deformity
	
			- Subluxation
	
			- Instability
	
			- Ankylosing

	- Characteristic deformities

		- Swan neck

		- Boutonnière

		- Z deformity

		- Ulnar deviation

	- Functional assessment

		- Power grip
			- Lifting jug

		- Precision grip
			- Key
			- Pen
			- Buttons

		- Hook grip
			- Carrying bag

- Investigations

	- ESR/CRP
		- Increased
		- Activity related

	- Anaemia
		- Normochromic
		- Normocytic

	- Rheumatoid factor



	- Anti-CCP antibodies
		- More specific


	- X-ray changes

		- Joint margin erosion

		- Loss of joint space

		- Juxta-articular osteoporosis

		- Cysts

		- Advanced
			- Subluxation
			- Ankylosing

	- MRI
		- Helpful for early diagnosis

- Diagnostic criteria
	- Features suggestive of RA

		- Family history of inflammatory arthritis

		- Symptoms >6 weeks

		- Early morning stiffness >1 hour

		- Arthritis in ≥3 regions

		- Swelling in ≥5 joints

		- Bilateral MTP compression tenderness

		- Symmetry

		- Rheumatoid nodules

		- Rheumatoid factor positivity

		- Raised ESR/CRP
			- Without infection

		- Anti-CCP positivity

		- Bony erosions on X-ray
			- Hands
			- Feet
			- Uncommon early



- Principles of management


	- Early aggressive treatment with DMARDs
		- Within first 2 years

	- Do not rely only on analgesics/NSAIDs initially

	- Monitor with
		- X-ray
		- Ultrasound
			- Hands
		- ESR
		- CRP



- General management

	- Rest and splinting
		- During acute flare

	- Regular exercise
		- Walking
		- Swimming
		- Hydrotherapy

	- Smoking cessation

	- Physiotherapy referral

	- Occupational therapy referral

	- Daily full range of joint movement


- Pharmacological management



	- NSAIDs


	- DMARDs and biological DMARDs
		- Improve long-term outcomes

	- Methotrexate
		- Backbone therapy

	- Analgesia
		- Paracetamol
		- NSAIDs

	- Avoid opioids

	- Glucocorticoids

		- For flares

		- Oral prednisolone


		- Intra-articular injections
			- Large joints

	- Biological DMARDs



		- Increased infection risk

			- TB

			- Atypical pneumonia

			- Listeria


- Supportive and preventive

	- Folic acid
		- Reduces GI toxicity
		- Reduces liver toxicity

	- Vaccination

		- Pneumococcus

		- Influenza

		- Hepatitis A

		- Hepatitis B

		- HPV
