
| Grade     | Clinical features                                                                          | Management                                                                                                                                     |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Grade I   | Bleeding only; no prolapse; painless bright red bleeding during defecation                 | Conservative (high-fibre diet, treat constipation, avoid straining/constipating drugs); injection if persistent                                |
| Grade II  | Bleeding + prolapse during defecation; reduces spontaneously                               | Rubber band ligation or sclerosant injection (office procedure); conservative measures for all                                                 |
| Grade III | Prolapse requiring manual reduction; may be painful if large                               | Surgical haemorrhoidectomy (or stapled haemorrhoidectomy/Doppler-guided haemorrhoidal artery ligation); rubber band ligation in selected cases |
| Grade IV  | Permanently prolapsed, irreducible, thrombosed; painful with bleeding and mucous discharge | Urgent hospital admission, analgesia, ice packs, urgent haemorrhoidectomy                                                                      |



- Haemorrhoids
  - Enlarged anal vascular cushions
  - Located at 3, 7 and 11 o'clock positions
  - Present with
    - Bright red rectal bleeding
    - Prolapse
    - Mucous discharge
    - Pain mainly in Grade IV (or large Grade III)

- Conservative treatment (all grades)
  - High-fibre diet
  - Treat constipation
  - Mild laxatives if required
  - Avoid straining
  - Avoid constipating drugs (eg codeine)

- Procedures
  - Sclerosant injection
    - Grade I persistent symptoms
    - Grade II
  - Rubber band ligation
    - Grade II (first choice)
    - Selected Grade III
    - Occasionally persistent Grade I
  - Surgical haemorrhoidectomy
    - Grade III
    - All Grade IV
    - Failed office procedures
