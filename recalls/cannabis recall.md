Q8. 10 weeks Pregnant patient using medical cannabis, symptoms of nausea relieved when using cannabis. what next?

1. Cease Cannabis - complete contraindication medicinal or personal use 
    
2. Gradually decrease
    
3. Continue using cannabis
    

**

**

Recommended course of action

The appropriate next step is to cease [cannabis](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=d9be14ba-b585-47cb-9440-dd52ae7c3774#) use. Continued use during [pregnancy](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=d9be14ba-b585-47cb-9440-dd52ae7c3774#) is not recommended because:

- [Cannabis](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=d9be14ba-b585-47cb-9440-dd52ae7c3774#) exposure in utero has been associated with adverse [perinatal](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=d9be14ba-b585-47cb-9440-dd52ae7c3774#) outcomes, including [low birth weight](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=d9be14ba-b585-47cb-9440-dd52ae7c3774#), [small-for-gestational-age infants](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=d9be14ba-b585-47cb-9440-dd52ae7c3774#), and potential neurocognitive and behavioral effects later in life.
