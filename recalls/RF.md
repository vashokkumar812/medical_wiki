# Acute Rheumatic Fever (ARF) (AMC MCQ High-Yield)

- **Definition**
	- Autoimmune inflammatory disease
	- Occurs **2–6 weeks after Group A Streptococcus (GAS) pharyngitis**
	- Can lead to **Rheumatic Heart Disease (RHD)**

- **Epidemiology (Australia)**
	- Mainly affects children **5–14 years**
	- High-risk groups
		- Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander children
		- Māori and Pacific Islander populations
		- Remote and socioeconomically disadvantaged communities

- **Aetiology**
	- Preceded by **Group A Streptococcus (Streptococcus pyogenes)**
		- Usually pharyngitis
	- Autoimmune molecular mimicry
		- Antibodies cross-react with
			- Heart
			- Joints
			- Brain
			- Skin

- **Clinical Features**
	- Fever
	- Migratory large joint pain
	- Lethargy
	- History of sore throat 2–6 weeks earlier (may be absent)

- **Jones Criteria (Diagnosis)**
	- **Diagnosis**
		- **2 Major**
		- OR **1 Major + 2 Minor**
		- **PLUS evidence of preceding GAS infection**
	- Exception
		- **Sydenham chorea alone can diagnose ARF** after exclusion of other causes

- **Major Criteria (Remember: J♥NES)**
	- **J**oints
		- Migratory polyarthritis
		- High-risk populations
			- Monoarthritis
			- Polyarthralgia may also count
	- **♥ Carditis**
		- Mitral regurgitation (most common)
		- Aortic regurgitation
		- Heart failure
		- Pericarditis
		- Echocardiographic valvulitis
	- **N**odules
		- Subcutaneous
		- Rare
		- Strongly associated with carditis
	- **E**rythema marginatum
		- Non-pruritic
		- Serpiginous rash
		- Spares the face
	- **S**ydenham chorea
		- Emotional lability
		- Involuntary jerky movements
		- Occurs months after infection
		- Common in adolescent girls

- **Minor Criteria**
	- Fever
	- Arthralgia
	- Raised inflammatory markers
		- ESR ≥30 mm/hr
		- CRP ≥30 mg/L
	- Prolonged PR interval on ECG

- **Evidence of Recent GAS Infection**
	- Positive throat culture
	- Rapid antigen test
	- Elevated ASO titre
	- Elevated Anti-DNase B titre

- **Investigations**
	- FBC
	- ESR
	- CRP
	- ASO titre
	- Anti-DNase B
	- ECG
		- Prolonged PR interval
	- **Echocardiogram**
		- Essential in all suspected cases
		- Detects subclinical carditis
	- Chest X-ray
		- If heart failure suspected

- **Management**
	- Hospital admission
	- Eradicate GAS
		- **IM benzathine penicillin G (single dose)** ← first-line
		- OR oral penicillin V for 10 days
	- Arthritis
		- Naproxen
		- Aspirin
	- Carditis
		- Bed rest
		- Diuretics if heart failure
		- ACE inhibitor if LV dysfunction
		- Severe cases
			- Consider corticosteroids
	- Sydenham chorea
		- Usually self-limiting
		- Severe cases
			- Valproate
			- Carbamazepine

- **Secondary Prevention (Most Tested)**
	- **IM benzathine penicillin G every 3–4 weeks**
	- Continue for **at least 10 years**
		- Longer or lifelong if severe rheumatic heart disease
	- Oral penicillin V
		- Alternative only if injections refused
		- Less effective due to poor adherence

- **Complications**
	- Rheumatic heart disease
		- Chronic mitral regurgitation
		- Mitral stenosis
		- Aortic valve disease
	- Heart failure
	- Atrial fibrillation
	- Infective endocarditis risk

- **Valve Involvement**
	- **Mitral valve = most common**
	- Aortic valve = second most common
	- Tricuspid and pulmonary valves rarely affected

- **Differential Diagnosis**
	- Septic arthritis
	- Juvenile idiopathic arthritis
	- Viral arthritis
	- Infective endocarditis
	- Systemic lupus erythematosus

- **AMC Pearls**
	- **ARF follows GAS pharyngitis—not post-streptococcal skin infection (classic teaching).**
	- **Mitral regurgitation is the commonest acute cardiac lesion.**
	- **Mitral stenosis develops years later in chronic RHD.**
	- **Migratory polyarthritis + fever + new murmur after sore throat = ARF until proven otherwise.**
	- **Echocardiography should be performed in every suspected ARF case.**
	- **Secondary prophylaxis = IM benzathine penicillin G every 3–4 weeks.**
	- **The best way to prevent RHD is adherence to long-term benzathine penicillin prophylaxis.**
	- **Sydenham chorea alone is sufficient for diagnosis after excluding other causes.**
