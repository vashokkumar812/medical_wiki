- Inverse psoriasis (flexural psoriasis)
  - Site
    - Inframammary folds
    - Axillae
    - Groin
    - Genital folds
    - Any intertriginous area
  - Presentation
    - Well-demarcated shiny erythematous plaques
    - Minimal or absent scale (moist environment)
    - Pruritic
    - Chronic
    - No response to antifungal therapy
    - Family history of psoriasis
    - Nail psoriasis may be present
- Treatment
	- <4 weeks
	  - Low-potency topical corticosteroid
	- \>4 weeks / maintenance
	  - Topical calcineurin inhibitor (tacrolimus, pimecrolimus)
	  - Vitamin D analogue
	- Avoid prolonged potent steroids in skin folds (atrophy)

- Differential diagnosis
  - Candidal intertrigo
    - Bright red plaques
    - Satellite papules/pustules
    - Collarette scaling
    - Foul odour/discharge
    - Responds to antifungals
  - Seborrhoeic dermatitis
    - Greasy yellow scales
    - Symmetrical
    - Sebaceous areas (face, chest, axillae)
  - Irritant contact dermatitis
    - History of irritant/contact exposure
    - Erythema
    - Scaling
    - Vesicles/bullae (acute)
    - Lichenification/fissures (chronic)
  - Erythrasma
    - Corynebacterium minutissimum
    - Well-defined brown-red patches
    - Fine wrinkling
    - Mild or no pruritus
    - Coral-red fluorescence on Wood lamp
