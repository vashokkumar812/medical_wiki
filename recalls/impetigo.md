- Definition
	- Superficial **bacterial skin infection**
	- Common organisms
		- *Staphylococcus aureus*
		- *Streptococcus pyogenes* (GAS)
	- Also called **school sores**
	- Common in **school-aged children**
	- Highly contagious
	- Usually does **not** resolve spontaneously
	- Responds rapidly to antibiotics

- Predisposing factors
	- Skin breaks
		- Cuts
		- Scratches
		- Insect bites
	- Underlying skin disease
		- Eczema
		- Dry/cracked skin
	- Occasionally occurs on normal skin

- Clinical features
	- Itchy vesicles/blisters
	- Vesicles rupture → ooze serous fluid
	- **Honey-coloured (golden/yellow) crusts**
	- Multiple lesions with peripheral spread
	- Common sites
		- Around mouth
		- Around nose
		- Arms
		- Legs
	- Usually **no fever or systemic illness**

- Diagnosis
	- Clinical
	- Swab for culture only if
		- Recurrent infection
		- Severe infection
		- Treatment failure

- Treatment
	- Antibiotics
		- mupirocin for localized disease
		- cephalexin for extensive disease
	- Continue until lesions healed
	- Remove crusts gently before applying topical antibiotics
	- Prevent scratching
		- Trim nails
		- Cover lesions if needed

- Infection control
	- Infectious while lesions are **weeping**


- School/sports/swimming
	- Return to school/daycare
		- **24 hours after starting antibiotics**
		- Lesions covered
	- Avoid swimming until **24 hours after antibiotics**
	- Avoid contact sports unless lesions completely covered

- Recurrent impetigo
	- Consider **nasal swab** for *S. aureus* carriage
	- May require **intranasal antibiotics**
