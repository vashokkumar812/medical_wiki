
# Pre renal Aki vs Atn

|                             | [[PRE RENAL Azotemia]] | [[ATN]]               |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------- | --------------------- |
|                             | Normal, hyaline casts  | Epithelial cell casts |
| Specific gravity            | < 1.020                | > 1.020               |
| FeNa                        | < 1%                   | > 2 %                 |
| FeUrea                      | < 35 %                 | > 50%                 |
| Urine sodium                | < 20                   | > 40                  |
| Urine osmolality            | > 500                  | < 350                 |
| Urine plasma creatine ratio | > 40                   | < 10                  |
| Plasma [[BUN]] creatine ratio   | > 20                   | < 15                  |

- Acute Kidney Injury (AKI) vs Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD)

	- AKI

		- Kidneys usually normal size

	- CKD

		- Small shrunken kidneys (except DM, amyloid, PKD)

---

- AKI Classification ⭐⭐⭐

	- Pre-renal (↓ renal perfusion) — Most common
		- Dehydration
		- Haemorrhage
		- Sepsis
		- Heart failure
		- Cirrhosis
		- Renal artery stenosis

	- Intrinsic renal
		- Acute tubular necrosis (ATN) ⭐
			- Ischaemia
			- Nephrotoxins
				- Aminoglycosides
				- Amphotericin
				- Cisplatin
				- Radiocontrast
				- Myoglobin (rhabdomyolysis)
				- Haemoglobin

		- Acute interstitial nephritis (AIN)+
			- Drugs
				- NSAIDs
				- Penicillins
				- PPIs
				- Rifampicin
			- Fever + rash + eosinophilia ⭐
		- Glomerulonephritis
			- Haematuria
			- RBC casts
			- Proteinuria
			- Hypertension

	- Post-renal
		- BPH ⭐
		- Stones
		- Pelvic tumour
		- Neurogenic bladder
		
- Urine Findings


	- ATN
		- Muddy brown granular casts ⭐

	- Glomerulonephritis
		- RBC casts ⭐

	- AIN
		- WBC casts
		- Eosinophils



- Indications for Dialysis (AEIOU) ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

	- A — Acidosis (severe)
	- E — Electrolyte disturbance
			- Hyperkalaemia refractory
	- I — Intoxication
			- Lithium
			- Methanol
			- Ethylene glycol
			- Salicylates
	- O — Overload
			- Pulmonary oedema refractory
	- U — Uraemia
			- Encephalopathy
			- Pericarditis
			- Severe bleeding


- CKD Definition ⭐⭐⭐

	- Kidney damage OR
	- eGFR <60 mL/min/1.73m²
	- Duration ≥3 months

---

- Common Causes of CKD ⭐⭐⭐

	- Diabetes mellitus ⭐
	- Hypertension ⭐
	- Glomerulonephritis
	- Polycystic kidney disease
	- Reflux nephropathy

---

- CKD Features

	- Fatigue
	- Anaemia
	- Nocturia
	- Hypertension
	- Peripheral oedema
	- Pruritus
	- Bone pain
	- Peripheral neuropathy

---

- CKD Complications ⭐⭐⭐

	- Hyperkalaemia
	- Metabolic acidosis
	- Fluid overload
	- Anaemia (↓EPO) ⭐
	- Mineral bone disease
		- ↑ PO₄
		- ↓ Ca
		- ↑ PTH
	- Cardiovascular disease

---

- CKD Management ⭐⭐⭐

	- Control BP
			- ACEI/ARB (especially proteinuria)
	- Control diabetes
	- Avoid nephrotoxins
	- Treat anaemia
			- Iron
			- Erythropoietin
	- Manage CKD-MBD
			- Phosphate restriction
			- Phosphate binders
			- Vitamin D
	- Diet
			- Salt restriction
			- Potassium restriction
			- Protein moderation
	- Dialysis/transplant if ESRD

---

- AMC High-Yield Pearls ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

	- AKI + enlarged bladder → Post-renal obstruction (catheter first)
	- Elderly man + AKI → BPH until proven otherwise
	- Septic/shocked patient → ATN
	- Fever + rash + eosinophilia after antibiotics/PPI → Acute interstitial nephritis
	- Haematuria + RBC casts → Glomerulonephritis
	- Muddy brown casts → ATN
	- Small kidneys → CKD
	- Normal kidneys do NOT exclude CKD (Diabetes, PKD, amyloidosis)
	- Most common CKD cause → Diabetes mellitus
	- Most dangerous AKI complication → Hyperkalaemia
	- Refractory hyperkalaemia → Dialysis
	- Pulmonary oedema not responding to diuretics → Dialysis
	- Uraemic pericarditis or encephalopathy → Dialysis
