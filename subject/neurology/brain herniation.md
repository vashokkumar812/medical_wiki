## definition
- displacement of part of the brain through an opening

## types
- Supratentorial
	1. Uncal transtentorial herniation  
	2. Central tentorial herniation  
	3. Subfalcine herniation  
	4. Transcalvarial herniation
1. Infratentorial
	1. Upward transtentorial herniation (“reverse coning”)  
	2. Foraminal or tonsillar herniation (“coning”)

| Type of Herniation    | Description                                                                                                          | Key Features / CT Findings                                                                                                                                             |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Uncal Transtentorial  | Uncinate process of the temporal lobe herniates into anterior part of tentorial opening                              | - Shift of brainstem & distortion of adjacent cisterns<br>- Dilatation of contralateral temporal horn<br>- PCA infarct due to compression of posterior cerebral artery |
| Central Tentorial     | Symmetrical downward movement of thalamic region through tentorial opening                                           | - Downward displacement of midline structures                                                                                                                          |
| Subfalcine            | Displacement of cingulate gyrus under the falx and across the midline                                                | - Midline shift<br>- May compress anterior cerebral artery                                                                                                             |
| Transcalvarial        | “External herniation” — brain displaced through a skull defect (fracture site or post-craniectomy)                   | - Brain tissue protruding outside skull defect                                                                                                                         |
| Upward Herniation     | “Reverse coning” after EVD insertion for hydrocephalus with posterior fossa mass → upwards transtentorial herniation | - Upward displacement of posterior fossa contents                                                                                                                      |
| Foraminal (Tonsillar) | Downward herniation of cerebellar tonsils into foramen magnum                                                        | - Compression of medulla & upper cervical cord<br>- Can cause cardiorespiratory arrest                                                                                 |
