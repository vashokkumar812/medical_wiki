* **Definition**  
  * Chronic inflammatory disease of gastrointestinal tract  
    * Unknown aetiology  

* **Types**  
  * Ulcerative colitis (UC)  
  * Crohn disease (CD)  
  * Indeterminate colitis  
    * Cannot distinguish UC vs CD  

* **Epidemiology**  
  * UC ≈ CD (equal frequency)  

* **Clinical presentation**  
  * Almost all patients  
    * Abdominal pain/discomfort  
    * Abnormal bowel habit  
      * Usually diarrhoea  
  * Important  
    * Most similar cases \= IBS  

* **Alarm features (suggest IBD)**  
  * Rectal bleeding  
  * Pallor  
  * Tachycardia  
  * Weight loss  
  * Abdominal mass  
  * Fever  
  * Nocturnal symptoms  

* **Investigations**  
  * Inflammatory markers  
    * ↑ CRP  
    * ↑ WBC  
    * ↑ Platelets  
    * ↑ ESR  
  * Stool  
    * Faecal pus cells  
    * No pathogens  

* **Definitive diagnosis**  
  * Colonoscopy / flexible sigmoidoscopy  
    * Must include  
      * Ileoscopy (if possible)  
      * Multiple biopsies  
  * Confirmatory test  
    * Histology  

* **Imaging**  
  * If small bowel disease suspected  
    * Barium follow-through  
    * Other small bowel imaging  

* **Important diagnostic rules**  
  * IBS diagnosis  
    * Always consider IBD  
  * Gastroenteritis-like symptoms \>4 days  
    * Consider IBD  
  * Chronic diarrhoea \+ rectal bleeding  
    * Colonoscopy  
  * Colonoscopy must include  
    * Ileal inspection  
    * Colonic biopsies  
  * Rectal bleeding  
    * Always endoscopy 

* **Treatment (general)**  

* **Ulcerative colitis (UC)**  
	* **Treatment goals**  
	    * Clinical remission  
	    * Histological remission 
	* **Mild–moderate UC**  
	    * Usually  
	      * Distal colon  
	      * Rectum  
	  * **Severe UC**  
	    * Features  
	      * Frequent bloody stools  
	      * Systemic illness  
	    * Management  
	      * Hospitalisation  
	      * IV steroids  

  * **[[Mesalazine]] (5-ASA)**  
	  * first line
  * **Corticosteroids**  
    * Indications  
      * Failure of 5-ASA  
      * Moderate–severe disease  
      * Need for rapid response  
    * ⚠️ No role in maintenance  
  * **Immunosuppressive therapy**  
    * Drugs  
      * Azathioprine  
      * 6-mercaptopurine  
      * Methotrexate  
    * Indications  
      * Chronically active disease  
      * Frequent relapses despite mesalazine  
  * **Surgery**  
    * Procedure  
      * Total colectomy \+ rectum removal  
      * Ileal pouch formation  
    * Indications  
      * Severe disease unresponsive to therapy  
      * Chronically active disease  
      * Dysplasia / colorectal cancer  
    * Outcomes  
      * Bowel frequency  
        * 6–8 watery stools/day  
      * Complication  
        * Pouchitis (\~25%)  
* Chrons disease  
  * **Blood markers**  
    * Hemoglobin  
    * WBC count  
    * Platelets  
    * Albumin  
    * CRP      
  * **Natural history**  
    * Chronically active disease  
    * Healing uncommon  
    * Under-reporting of symptoms  
      * Due to adaptation  
  * **Modern treatment concepts (Crohn disease)**  
    * **Mesalazine**  
      * Used in mild disease  
    * **Steroids**  
      * Only for acute crisis  
      * Reason  
        * Other drugs slow onset  
          * e.g. azathioprine (3–6 months)  
    * **Antibiotics**  
      * Metronidazole  
      * Ciprofloxacin  
      * Used for inflammation control  
    * **Smoking cessation**  
      * Very important  
    * **Early aggressive therapy**  
      * Early use of immunosuppressants  
        * Azathioprine  
    * **Biological therapy**  
      * Example  
        * Infliximab (anti-TNF)  
      * Effects  
        * Strong anti-inflammatory action  
        * Promotes healing  
      * Risks  
        * Opportunistic infections  
        * Mortality up to 1% 
	* **Surgery in Crohn disease**  
    * Indications  
      * Abscess  
      * Bowel obstruction  
      * Poorly controlled disease  
    * Principle  
      * Minimal surgery  
        * Resect minimum bowel  
* **Comprehensive management of IBD**  
  * Beyond inflammation control  
  * Includes  
    * Nutrition  
    * Education  
    * Counselling  
    * Psychological support  
* **Five essentials for IBD management**  
  * **1\. Establish responsibility**  
    * GP  
    * Gastroenterologist  
    * Surgeon  
  * **2\. Evaluate patient understanding**  
    * Use support organisations  
      * Australian Crohn’s and Colitis Association (ACCA)  
  * **3\. Encourage illness acceptance**  
    * Promote coping strategies  
  * **4\. Educate patients**  
    * Drugs  
    * Side effects  
    * Alternative therapies  
  * **5\. Ensure follow-up**  
    * Monitor  
      * Nutrition  
      * Complications  
      * Disease progression  
* **Nutrition issues in IBD**  
  * Unnecessary supplements common  
  * Focus  
    * Adequate nutrition  
    * Symptom reduction  
  * Avoid  
    * Seeds  
    * Skins  
    * Indigestible fibre (if obstruction)  
  * Avoid (if bloating/diarrhoea)  
    * Wheat  
    * Onions  
* **Iron deficiency in IBD**  
  * Cause  
    * Poor absorption during inflammation  
  * Oral iron  
    * Poor tolerance  
  * Preferred  
    * Intravenous iron  
  * Benefits  
    * ↑ energy  
    * ↑ quality of life  
* **IBD and colorectal cancer**  
  * Increased risk  
    * Colonic IBD  
  * **Surveillance (UC ≥ left colon)**  
    * Start  
      * 7–10 years after onset  
    * Procedure  
      * Colonoscopy  
      * Multiple biopsies  
    * Frequency  
      * Every 2 years  
  * **Crohn disease**  
    * Increased risk  
      * Colon cancer  
      * Small bowel cancer  
    * Surveillance  
      * Less established  
* **Multidisciplinary care**  
  * **Most important**  
    * Patient  
  * **Patient role**  
    * Understand disease  
    * Understand medications  
    * Active participation  
  * **Roles**  
    * GP  
      * Early diagnosis  
      * Support  
      * Smoking cessation  
      * General health care  
    * Gastroenterologist  
      * Specialist management  
    * Surgeon  
      * Manage complications  
  * **Team communication**  
    * Essential
