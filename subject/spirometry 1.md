# AMC MCQ Spirometry Quick Guide

| Spirometry Finding                                                                                                                  | Most Likely Diagnosis                                       |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Forced Expiratory Volume in 1 Second/Forced Vital Capacity <0.70                                                                    | Obstructive lung disease                                    |
| Forced Expiratory Volume in 1 Second/Forced Vital Capacity <0.70 + improves after bronchodilator (≥12% and ≥200 millilitres)        | Asthma                                                      |
| Forced Expiratory Volume in 1 Second/Forced Vital Capacity <0.70 + minimal reversibility                                            | Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease                       |
| Forced Vital Capacity ↓ + Forced Expiratory Volume in 1 Second/Forced Vital Capacity normal or increased                            | Restrictive lung disease (confirm with total lung capacity) |
| Forced Expiratory Volume in 1 Second ↓ + Forced Vital Capacity ↓ + Forced Expiratory Volume in 1 Second/Forced Vital Capacity <0.70 | Mixed obstructive and restrictive disease                   |
| Normal Forced Expiratory Volume in 1 Second, Forced Vital Capacity, and Forced Expiratory Volume in 1 Second/Forced Vital Capacity  | Normal spirometry                                           |

## Bronchodilator Test

| Result                                                                     | Interpretation                                    |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Increase in Forced Expiratory Volume in 1 Second ≥12% and ≥200 millilitres | Significant reversibility → Asthma likely         |
| No significant improvement                                                 | Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease more likely |

## High-Yield AMC Associations

| Clinical Scenario                  | Expected Spirometry      |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Young patient with episodic wheeze | Obstructive + reversible |
| Smoker with chronic cough          | Persistent obstruction   |
| Interstitial lung disease          | Restrictive pattern      |
| Neuromuscular disease              | Restrictive pattern      |
| Kyphoscoliosis                     | Restrictive pattern      |
| Pulmonary fibrosis                 | Restrictive pattern      |
