#mcq  #emedici 
## definition
- excessive menstrual blood loss which interferes with the woman’s physical, emotional, social and material quality of life, and which can occur alone or in combination with other symptoms

### causes
#### Hormonal Causes
| Most Likely Condition | Presentation                                        |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| PCOS                  | Young woman with hirsutism + oligomenorrhea         |
| Perimenopause         | Woman >40 years with hot flushes + irregular cycles |
| Hypothyroidism        | Weight gain + fatigue + cold intolerance            |

#### Uterine (Structural) Causes
| Condition                      | Definition                            | Symptoms / Effects                                                               | Characteristics / Risk factors                                                                                                                          | Management / Notes                                                           |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Uterine muscle dysfunction     | Poor uterine contraction              | Persistent bleeding from vessels                                                 | -                                                                                                                                                       | -                                                                            |
| Fibroids (Leiomyoma)           | Benign growths of uterine muscle      | Heavy menstrual bleeding<br>Pelvic pressure<br>Pelvic pain<br>Fertility problems | Size from microscopic → very large<br>Symptoms improve after menopause<br>Many fibroids are asymptomatic                                                | Observation<br>Medication<br>Surgical removal<br>Uterine artery embolization |
| Uterine polyps                 | Benign endometrial growths            | Heavy periods<br>Intermenstrual bleeding<br>Post-coital bleeding                 | Can develop abnormalities                                                                                                                               | Removal usually recommended                                                  |
| Adenomyosis                    | Endometrium grows into uterine muscle | Poor uterine contraction<br>Heavy bleeding<br>Painful menstruation               | -                                                                                                                                                       | -                                                                            |
| Endometrial cancer / precancer | Rare but serious cause                | -                                                                                | Age > 45 years<br>Weight > 90 kg<br>Nulliparity<br>Family history: Endometrial cancer, Ovarian cancer, Bowel cancer<br>PCOS<br>Genetic cancer syndromes | -                                                                            |

#### Blood Disorders
- Clotting disorders
- Use of anticoagulant medications (“blood thinners”)

#### Other Causes
- Liver disease
- Kidney disease
- Hormonal contraception (occasionally)

#### When No Cause Is Identified
  - Investigations may be normal
  - Thought to be:
    - Endometrial clotting dysfunction
    - No specific diagnostic test exists

- Classification

	- PALM-COEIN

		- Structural causes (PALM)
			- Polyp
			- Adenomyosis
			- Leiomyoma
				- Fibroid
			- Malignancy

		- Non-structural causes (COEIN)
			- Coagulopathy
			- Ovulatory dysfunction
			- Endometrial
			- Iatrogenic
			- Not classified

- Diagnosis

	- Clinical features
		- May or may not have
			- Blood loss >80 mL
			- Passing clots >50 cent coin size
			- Changing pad/tampon every hour
			- Waking at night to change pad/tampon
			- Need for towel/maternity pads during sleep
			- Bleeding through clothing
			- Bleeding >8 days

	- Investigations

		- Examination
			- Speculum examination
			- Cervical inspection
			- TVS
				- Fibroids
				- Polyps
				- Uterine abnormalities

		- Blood tests
			- Haemoglobin
			- Iron studies
			- Thyroid function
			- Coagulation studies

		- Ultrasound
			- Preferred
				- Transvaginal ultrasound

			- Evaluates
				- Uterus
				- Ovaries

			- Detects
				- Fibroids
				- Polyps
				- Structural abnormalities

		- Internal swabs
			- Exclude
				- Pelvic infection
				- Cervical infection

		- Endometrial biopsy
			- Sample from endometrium
			- Detects
				- Precancerous changes
				- Endometrial carcinoma

		- Hysteroscopy
			- Direct visualisation of uterine cavity
			- Allows
				- Inspection
				- Biopsy

			- Can be performed
				- Office procedure
				- Under anaesthesia

- Treatment

	- Depends on
		- Cause
		- Contraceptive need
		- Fertility wishes
		- Medical comorbidities

	- Medical treatment

		- Hormonal treatment

			- Levonorgestrel intrauterine device
				- Most effective hormonal option
				- Local progesterone release
				- ↓ menstrual blood loss
				- Provides contraception
				- Effective up to 5 years

			- Oral contraceptive pill
				- Controls bleeding
				- Cyclical or continuous use
				- Regulates cycle
				- ↓ bleeding

			- Other hormonal options
				- Vaginal ring
				- Hormonal injection
				- Transdermal patch

		- Non-hormonal treatment

			- Tranexamic acid
				- Most effective non-hormonal option
				- Stabilises clot formation
				- ↓ blood loss by ~50%
				- Does not shorten bleeding duration

			- NSAIDs
				- Example
					- Ibuprofen

				- Effects
					- ↓ menstrual flow by ~25%
					- ↓ pain/cramps

				- Limitation
					- No effect on duration

	- Surgical treatment

		- Indications
			- Failed medical therapy
			- Unsuitable for medical therapy
			- Patient preference

		- Hysteroscopy
			- Removes
				- Endometrial polyps
				- Small fibroids
			- Minimally invasive
			- Usually day procedure

		- Laparoscopy/open surgery
			- Indications
				- Large fibroids
				- Inaccessible fibroids

			- Goal
				- Myomectomy

		- Endometrial ablation
			- Heat destroys endometrium
			- ↓ or stops bleeding
			- Only if no future pregnancy desired
			- Day procedure
			- Rapid recovery

		- Hysterectomy
			- Definitive treatment
			- Permanent cure

			- Surgical routes
				- Laparoscopic
				- Vaginal
				- Abdominal

			- Indications
				- Failed alternatives
				- Contraindications to alternatives
				- Patient choice

- Treatment escalation pathway
	- Medical therapy
	- Hysteroscopic treatment
	- Fibroid surgery
	- Endometrial ablation
	- Hysterectomy

- Clinical advice
	- Heavy menstrual bleeding significantly affects quality of life
	- Early assessment allows
		- Diagnosis of cause
		- Individualised treatment
