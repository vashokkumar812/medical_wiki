# Kanavel Signs – Pyogenic Flexor Tenosynovitis

- **Diagnosis:** Pyogenic flexor tenosynovitis
- **Importance:** Surgical emergency
- **Management:**
  - Urgent hand surgery referral
  - IV antibiotics
  - Early incision, irrigation, and drainage (washout) if indicated

## Kanavel's 4 Signs

- Fusiform ("sausage-shaped") swelling of the finger
- Tenderness along the entire flexor tendon sheath
- Finger held in a slightly flexed position at rest
- Severe pain with passive extension of the finger

## Mnemonic: **STFP**

- **S** – Sausage-shaped swelling
- **T** – Tenderness along the tendon sheath
- **F** – Flexed finger
- **P** – Pain with passive extension
