## info
### normal anatomy
- umbilical cord inserts **directly into the placenta** → vessels protected by **Wharton’s jelly**.

### pathophysiology
- Placental or velamentous cord insertion anomaly → fetal vessels run unprotected within membranes over internal cervical os → membrane rupture/compression during labour → acute fetal hemorrhage → fetal hypoxia → fetal death

### Risk factors:
  - [[Placenta praevia]]  
  - Velamentous cord insertion  
  - [[Multiple pregnancy]]  

## diagnosis
### clinical features
- Small visible bleed → large fetal volume loss  

### CTG
- Fetal tachycardia  
- Deep decelerations  

## Management:
  - High suspicion  
  - Immediate Caesarean section
