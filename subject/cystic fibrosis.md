## Clinical features

- Respiratory
	- Chronic productive cough
	- Recurrent lower respiratory tract infections
	- Bronchiectasis
	- Wheeze
	- Chronic sinusitis
	- Nasal polyps
	- Clubbing
	- Haemoptysis (late)

- Gastrointestinal
	- Meconium ileus (neonates)
	- Failure to thrive
	- Steatorrhoea
	- Pancreatic insufficiency
	- Distal intestinal obstruction syndrome (DIOS)
	- Rectal prolapse
	- Fat-soluble vitamin deficiency (A, D, E, K)

- Hepatobiliary
	- Cholestasis
	- Biliary cirrhosis
	- Portal hypertension
	- Gallstones

- Endocrine
	- CF-related diabetes mellitus

- Reproductive
	- Male infertility
		- Congenital bilateral absence of vas deferens (CBAVD)
	- Reduced female fertility

- Sweat glands
	- Salty sweat
	- Recurrent dehydration
	- Hypochloraemic metabolic alkalosis

## Complications
- Bronchiectasis
- Chronic respiratory failure
- Pneumothorax
- Massive haemoptysis
- Allergic bronchopulmonary aspergillosis (ABPA)
- Pancreatic insufficiency
- Malnutrition
- CF-related diabetes
- Osteoporosis
- Liver disease
- Infertility

## investigations
- Initial investigations

	- Newborn screening
		- Performed for almost all Australian newborns
		- May miss infants lost to follow-up or whose parents decline screening

	- Sweat chloride test
		- Investigation of choice to confirm diagnosis
		- Sweat chloride
			- ≥60 mmol/L = diagnostic (with compatible clinical features)

	- Genetic testing
		- CFTR mutation analysis
		- Confirms diagnosis
		- Useful when sweat test is equivocal

	- Other investigations
		- Sputum culture
		- Chest X-ray
		- High-resolution CT chest
			- Bronchiectasis
		- Spirometry
		- Faecal elastase
			- Pancreatic insufficiency
		- Blood glucose
		- Fat-soluble vitamin levels
		- Liver function tests

## Diagnosis
- Compatible clinical features
- Plus one of
	- Positive sweat chloride test
	- Two disease-causing CFTR mutations
	- Positive newborn screening with confirmatory testing

## Management

- Respiratory
	- Airway clearance physiotherapy
	- Regular exercise
	- Nebulised hypertonic saline
	- Dornase alfa (DNase)
	- Bronchodilators if indicated
	- Prompt antibiotics for exacerbations
	- Long-term azithromycin (selected patients)
	- Inhaled antibiotics for chronic *Pseudomonas*
		- Tobramycin
		- Colistin

- CFTR modulators
	- According to genotype
		- Ivacaftor
		- Lumacaftor/Ivacaftor
		- Tezacaftor/Ivacaftor
		- Elexacaftor/Tezacaftor/Ivacaftor

- Nutrition
	- High-calorie diet
	- High-fat diet
	- Pancreatic enzyme replacement
	- Fat-soluble vitamin supplementation
	- Dietitian review

- Complication management
	- Treat diabetes
	- Treat liver disease
	- Osteoporosis prevention
	- Lung transplantation for end-stage disease

- Genetic testing

	- Newborn screening
		- Offered to almost all babies in Australia

	- Reproductive carrier screening
		- Can be offered by GPs
		- All couples planning pregnancy
		- Couples already pregnant
		- Regardless of ethnicity or family history

## Referral indications

- Genetic counselling
	- Both partners are CF carriers
	- Planning pregnancy
	- First trimester of pregnancy

- Respiratory paediatrician
	- Child with suspected CF not detected on newborn screening
	- Features include
		- Recurrent cough
		- Failure to thrive
		- Recurrent lower respiratory tract infections
		- Bronchiectasis
		- Rectal prolapse
	- For sweat testing

- Fertility specialist
	- Male infertility due to CBAVD
	- Evaluate for atypical CF

## Prognosis
- Progressive chronic disease
- Survival continues to improve with multidisciplinary care
- Respiratory failure remains the leading cause of death
