## Causes
- **Renal failure**  
- Increased renal resorption:  
  - Hypoparathyroidism  
  - Thyrotoxicosis  
- **Cellular injury with phosphate release**  
  - Tumour lysis syndrome  
  - Rhabdomyolysis  
  - Haemolysis  
  - Ischaemic gut  
- **Medications / iatrogenic**  
  - Phosphate-containing laxatives  
  - Excessive phosphate administration  
  - Bisphosphonate therapy  

---

## Clinical Features  
*(Mostly related to secondary [[Hypocalcaemia]])*  
- Precipitation of Ca²⁺ → **nephrolithiasis**  
- Interference with PTH-mediated bone resorption  
- ↓ Vitamin D levels  
- Muscle cramping  
- Tetany  
- Hyperreflexia  
- Seizures  
- **Cardiac:** prolonged QT interval  

---

## Management
- Treat the **underlying condition**  
- Limit dietary phosphate intake  
- Enhance urinary phosphate excretion:  
  - Saline  
  - Acetazolamide  
- Dialysis (if severe or refractory)  
- Oral phosphate binders:  
  - Calcium salts  
  - Aluminium salts
