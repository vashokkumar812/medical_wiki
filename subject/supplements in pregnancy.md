## antenatal supplements

| supplement        | duration                                    | dose                                             | reason / indications                                                        |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| folic acid        | 1 month before conception → 12 weeks        | standard: 500 mcg/day                            | prevents neural tube defects (NTD)                                          |
|                   |                                             | high risk: 5 mg/day                              | high-risk situations                                                        |
|                   |                                             |                                                  | monitor FBC in multiple pregnancy or haemolytic anaemia (↑ deficiency risk) |
| vitamin D (25-OH) | throughout pregnancy                        | 400 IU/day                                       | bone health (Ca/PO₄ absorption)                                             |
| iron              | as indicated (not routine)                  | ≥60 mg elemental iron/day (if anaemia)           | treat iron deficiency anaemia                                               |
| iodine            | preconception, pregnancy, breastfeeding     | 150 mcg/day                                      | prevent deficiency → avoid hypothyroidism effects                           |
| calcium           | throughout pregnancy                        | 1000 mg/day (≥19 yrs) or 1300 mg/day (14–18 yrs) | bone health; ↓ preeclampsia & preterm labour                                |
| vitamin B12       | pregnancy & lactation (if vegetarian/vegan) | 2.6 mcg/day (pregnancy); 2.8 mcg/day (lactation) | prevent neonatal neurological complications                                 |
| vitamin K         | late pregnancy (if indicated)               | not specified                                    | cholestasis → ↓ absorption                                                  |
| omega-3 (DHA/EPA) | throughout pregnancy (if low intake)        | not specified                                    | if seafood intake \<2–3 serves/week                                         |
| probiotics        | —                                           | —                                                | not recommended (insufficient evidence)                                     |
| vitamin A         | —                                           | —                                                | contraindicated in pregnancy                                                |

- folic acid
	- high risk indications
		- previous child with NTD
		- family history of NTD
		- anticonvulsant use
		- pre-existing diabetes
		- maternal BMI >30
		- malabsorption states

- vitamin D
	- deficiency
		- <50 nmol/L

	- insufficiency
		- <75 nmol/L

	- key points
		- do NOT routinely test during pregnancy
		- do NOT re-test during pregnancy
		- supplementation improves maternal vitamin D levels
		- no consistent obstetric benefit demonstrated

- iron
	- monitoring
		- check Hb
			- at booking
			- at ~28 weeks

	- supplement only if
		- anaemia
		- multiple pregnancy
		- vegetarian diet

- calcium
	- indication
		- supplement if low dietary intake
			- e.g. lactose intolerance

	- benefits
		- ↓ hypertensive disorders
		- ↓ preterm birth
