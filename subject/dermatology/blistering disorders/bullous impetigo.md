- **Cause**
	- *Staphylococcus aureus*

- **Features**
	- Fragile bullae
	- Honey-coloured crusts
	- Face common
	- Around mouth/nose

- **Investigation**
	- Skin swab + culture

- **Treatment**
	- Remove crusts
	- Topical mupirocin
	- Oral flucloxacillin/cephalexin if severe

- **Important points**
	- Highly contagious
	- Exclude from school until treated
