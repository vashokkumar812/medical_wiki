
## **Association**
- Coeliac disease
- Gluten sensitivity

## **Clinical features**
- Intensely itchy grouped vesicles
- Elbows
- Knees
- Buttocks
- Lower back

## **Diagnosis**
- Skin biopsy
- Direct IF
	- Granular IgA deposits

## **Treatment**
- Dapsone
- Lifelong gluten-free diet

## **Complication**
- Increased GI lymphoma risk
