# Bed Bugs (Cimex spp.)

- **Cause**
	- *Cimex lectularius*
	- Blood-feeding insect

- **Transmission**
	- Infestation via luggage, bedding, furniture
	- Common in hotels/shared accommodation

- **Clinical features**
	- Bites usually overnight
	- Exposed areas affected
		- Arms
		- Legs
		- Shoulders
	- Itchy erythematous macules/papules
	- Wheals/urticaria possible
	- Linear bite pattern may occur
	- Bullous reactions possible
	- Rarely anaphylaxis

- **Timing**
	- Skin reaction may be delayed
		- Up to several days

- **Complications**
	- Secondary bacterial infection
	- Psychological distress/anxiety
	- Sleep disturbance
	- Rare anaemia with heavy infestation

- **Diagnosis**
	- Clinical suspicion + environmental inspection
	- Look for
		- Bed bugs
		- Dark faecal spots on mattress

- **Differential diagnosis**
	- Scabies
	- Mosquito bites
	- Fleas
	- Drug eruption
	- Urticaria
	- Chickenpox

- **Treatment**
	- Antihistamines
	- Topical corticosteroids
	- Systemic steroids if severe
	- Treat secondary infection if present

- **Eradication**
	- Professional pest control often required
	- Bed bugs difficult to eliminate
	- Insecticide resistance common

- **Important facts**
	- Bed bugs do not transmit human infectious diseases
	- Diagnosis based on bites alone is unreliable
