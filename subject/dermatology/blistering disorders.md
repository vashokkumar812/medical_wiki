- **Classification**
	- Immunobullous
		- Pemphigus vulgaris
		- Bullous pemphigoid
		- Dermatitis herpetiformis
	- Non-immunobullous
		- Bullous impetigo
		- HSV/VZV
		- Drug eruptions
		- Porphyria cutanea tarda

- **Clinical clues**
	- Fragile blisters/erosions
		- Pemphigus vulgaris
	- Tense blisters
		- Bullous pemphigoid
	- Grouped itchy vesicles
		- Dermatitis herpetiformis
	- Honey-coloured crusts
		- Bullous impetigo

- **Diagnosis**
	- Skin biopsy
		- Histopathology
	- Direct immunofluorescence
		- Important for autoimmune blistering disease
