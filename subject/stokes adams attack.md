- Clinical features
	- Sudden onset without warning
	- Patient falls to ground
	- Collapse with loss of consciousness
	- Pallor
	- Motionless appearance
		- As if dead
	- Slow pulse
			- Or absent pulse
	- Recovery phase
		- Patient flushes as pulse increases

- Prognosis
	- Recovery
		- Occurs within seconds
		- Returns completely to normal

- Management
	- Refer for further management
		- Attacks may recur
