# Jaundice and other biliary problems

- Murtagh – table p. 622
- [[bilirubin]] metabolism  

- Pathology:  
  - Liver problems + biliary obstruction – some of the conjugated [[bilirubin]] leaks out from the hepatocytes in the blood and is excreted in the urine as [[bilirubin]]  
  - Hemolytic anemia – increased unconjugated [[bilirubin]] in the blood → increased urobilinogen in the urine  
    - High ratio of unconjugated to conjugated [[bilirubin]] in the serum
