# Libido

## Definition
> Desire for sexual activity

---

## Causes of Low Libido

### Psychosocial (most common)
- Stress
- Depression
- Anxiety
- Relationship problems
- Fatigue

### Biological
- Diabetes
- Thyroid disease
- Endocrine disorders

### Iatrogenic
Drugs:
- **SSRIs**
- **Antipsychotics**
- **Beta blockers**

### Lifestyle
- Alcohol excess

---

## Important Clinical Point
- Depression ↓ libido
- **SSRIs treating depression may further worsen libido**

---

## Management
- Address underlying cause
- Counselling
- Review medications
- Treat psychiatric illness
