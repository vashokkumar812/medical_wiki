


- Most uncomplicated cases resolve with
	- Simple measures
	- Explanation
	- Reassurance

- Avoid
	- Perfumed soaps
	- Powders

- Skin care
	- Use bland aqueous cream
	- Use mild soap substitute

- Topical corticosteroids
	- Methylprednisolone aceponate 0.1%
		- Useful initially
	- Once controlled
		- Hydrocortisone 1%

- Psychological factors
	- Stress and anxiety commonly contribute

- Obesity/intertrigo
	- Excess sweating may worsen symptoms
	- Buttocks may be strapped apart with adhesive tape

- Differential diagnoses
	- Perianal lichen simplex
	- Lichen sclerosus

- Consider these diagnoses in patients presenting with
	- “Sore bottom”
