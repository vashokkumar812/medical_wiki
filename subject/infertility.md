- Infertility
  - Failure to conceive after:
    - 12 months if female ≤35 years
    - 6 months if female >35 years
  - Refer earlier if known infertility risk factors




- Investigations
  - Female
    - TVUS (first-line imaging)

    - Mid-luteal progesterone (confirms ovulation)

  - Male
    - Semen analysis (first-line)


- Not routinely recommended
  - AMH testing
  - Antisperm antibodies
  - Tubal patency tests
    - HSG
    - HyCoSy
    - Laparoscopy with dye
    - Usually specialist investigations

- treatment

	- Lifestyle
		  - Optimise BMI
		    - Low BMI ↓ fertility
		    - High BMI ↓ fertility
		    - 5% weight loss improves fertility in overweight women
		  - Stop smoking
		  - Avoid recreational drugs
		  - Minimise alcohol
		  - Regular exercise
		  - Folate
		    - Start ≥1 month before conception
		    - Continue until 12 weeks gestation
		  - Iodine
		    - 150 mcg daily
		  - Intercourse
		    - Every 2–3 days during fertile window
		
		- Fertile window
		  - 5 days before ovulation until ovulation
		  - Ovulation occurs ~14 days before next period
		  - Prediction methods
		    - Urinary LH surge (best)
		    - Basal body temperature rises after ovulation
		    - Cervical mucus becomes slippery before ovulation
		    - Menstrual calendar
		  - Salivary testing not recommended
		  - Fertility apps alone are unreliable
		  - Before ovulation induction
		    - Exclude pregnancy
		    - Semen analysis
		    - Assess tubal patency if indicated

	- Ovulation induction
	  - First-line
	    - Letrozole
	  - Alternatives
	    - Clomifene
	    - Gonadotrophins (specialist)
	  - Confirm ovulation
	    - Mid-luteal progesterone
	  - Risks
	    - Multiple pregnancy
	    - OHSS
	
	- PCOS infertility
	  - First-line
	    - Weight loss
	    - Diet
	    - Exercise
	  - Medical treatment
	    - Letrozole
	    - Clomifene
	    - Metformin
	    - Gonadotrophins (specialist)
	
	- If Endometriosis 
	  - do Laparoscopic surgery
	
	- Male infertility
	  - ART is treatment for most abnormal semen analyses
	  - Gonadotrophins
	    - Only for hypogonadotropic hypogonadism
	  - Testosterone
	    - Avoid while attempting conception
	
	- Assisted reproductive technology
	  - IUI
	    - Mild male factor infertility
	    - Donor sperm
	    - Unexplained infertility
	  - IVF
	    - Tubal disease
	    - Severe endometriosis
	    - Failed conservative treatment
	  - ICSI
	    - Severe male factor infertility
	
	- OHSS
	  - Usually after gonadotrophins or IVF
	  - Symptoms
	    - Abdominal pain
	    - Bloating
	    - Vomiting
	    - Ascites
	    - Oliguria
	    - Dyspnoea
	    - AKI
	    - Thrombosis
	    - Pleural effusion
	    - Ovarian torsion
	    - Death
	  - Urgent referral
	    - Severe pain
	    - Vomiting
	    - Dyspnoea
	    - Reduced urine output
