- Definition
	- Renal tract pain
		- Most commonly due to **ureteric obstruction by calculi (renal colic)**


- Pain severity
	- Depends on **speed (acuity) of obstruction**
	- Not degree of obstruction

- Clinical features
	- Severe colicky pain
	- **Migratory "loin → groin" pain**
	- Pain location depends on stone site
		- Upper ureter
			- Flank pain
			- Renal angle tenderness
		- Mid ureter
			- Loin pain
		- Distal ureter
			- Groin
			- Scrotum/labia
			- Perineum
			- LUTS
				- Frequency
				- Urgency
				- Nocturia
				- Incontinence
	- Associated symptoms
		- Haematuria
		- Nausea
		- Vomiting
		- LUTS
	- **Red flag**
		- Fever → **infected obstructed kidney (urological emergency)**


- Risk factors
	- Previous/family history of stones
	- Structural abnormalities
		- Horseshoe kidney
		- Ectopic kidney
		- Medullary sponge kidney
		- ADPKD
		- Duplex ureter
		- PUJ obstruction
	- Medical
		- Gout
		- Hyperparathyroidism
		- Renal tubular acidosis
		- Recurrent Urinary tract infection
	- Drugs
		- Ciprofloxacin
		- acetazolamide
		- Sulfonamides
		- Indinavir
		- Loop diuretics
	- Lifestyle
		- Dehydration
		- High salt
		- High protein diet
- investigations
	- **Gold standard: Non-contrast CT KUB**
		- Stone size
		- Location
		- Density
		- Alternative diagnoses
	- Ultrasound
		- Preferred
			- Pregnancy
			- <50 years
			- Radiation avoidance
		- Poor for mid-ureter stones
		- CT if inconclusive
- Management
	- Analgesia
		- First-line
			- NSAIDs
			- Paracetamol
		- Second-line
			- Opioids
	- Medical expulsive therapy
		- **Tamsulosin (α-blocker)**
			- Best for
				- Distal ureter stones
				- Stones >5 mm
			- AE
				- Postural hypotension
	- Surgical indications
		- Failed conservative therapy
		- Infection + obstruction
		- Anuria
		- Bilateral obstruction
		- Solitary kidney obstruction
		- AKI
	- Procedures
		- Ureteric stent
		- Percutaneous nephrostomy
		- Definitive stone removal
			- Ureteroscopy + laser lithotripsy
			- ESWL
