- Labour definitions

	- Latent phase
		- Painful contractions
		- Cervical dilation ≤4 cm
		- Slow cervical change

		- Management
			- Discharge home OR observe in hospital
			- Advise when to return

		- Evidence
			- ↓ epidural use
			- ↓ oxytocin augmentation
			- Similar caesarean rates

	- Active phase
		- Cervical dilation ≥5 cm
		- Regular painful contractions
		- Progressive cervical dilation

		- Ends at
			- Full dilation
				- 10 cm

	- Slow labour
		- <2 cm cervical dilation in 4 hours after 5 cm dilation

		- Clinical indicators
			- Fetal descent
			- Fetal rotation
			- Contraction strength

- First stage management

	- Vaginal examination
		- Standard
			- Every 4 hours

		- More frequent if
			- Maternal concern
			- Fetal concern
			- Patient request
			- Before analgesia
			- Suspected full dilation

	- Expected duration of active labour

		- Primigravida
			- ≤18 hours

		- Multiparous
			- ≤12 hours

		- Wide variation normal

- Augmentation of labour

	- Routine amniotomy
		- Not recommended if labour progressing normally

		- Evidence
			- No improvement in outcomes
			- No reduction in C-section

	- Amniotomy + oxytocin
		- Indications
			- Slow labour
			- Labour dystocia

		- Explain to patient
			- Uncertain effect on labour duration
			- Uncertain effect on delivery mode

	- Active management of labour package
		- Early amniotomy
		- Early oxytocin

		- Effects
			- Small reduction in C-section
			- Slight reduction in labour duration

		- Not routine

- Second stage of labour

	- Definition
		- Full dilation → delivery of baby

	- Decision to expedite birth
		- Do not use time alone

		- Assess
			- Maternal condition
			- Fetal condition
			- Fetal descent
			- Labour progress
			- Patient preference

	- Escalation threshold

		- Nulliparous
			- Escalate if pushing ≥2 hours

		- Multiparous
			- Escalate if pushing ≥1 hour

		- If stable
			- Additional 1 hour may be allowed

	- Risks of prolonged second stage

		- Maternal
			- Postpartum haemorrhage
			- Chorioamnionitis
			- Endometritis
			- Postpartum fever
			- 3rd/4th degree tears

		- Fetal
			- Shoulder dystocia
			- Neonatal sepsis
			- NICU admission

	- Management of prolonged second stage
		- Maternal position change
		- Manual rotation
		- Operative vaginal delivery
		- Caesarean section

	- Maternal position

		- Preferred
			- Upright position

		- Benefits
			- ↓ assisted vaginal delivery

		- If epidural
			- Left lateral position preferred

- Cord clamping

	- Delayed cord clamping
		- ≥60 seconds
		- OR until pulsation stops

		- Benefits
			- ↑ neonatal haemoglobin
			- ↑ neonatal iron stores

		- Possible risk
			- ↑ jaundice requiring phototherapy

	- Early cord clamping
		- Indications
			- Neonatal resuscitation
			- Maternal emergency

- Third stage of labour

	- Definition
		- Birth of baby → delivery of placenta

	- Active management
		- Prophylactic oxytocin
		- Controlled cord traction

		- Benefits
			- ↓ postpartum haemorrhage
			- ↓ blood transfusion

		- PPH reduction
			- ~50%

	- Physiological management
		- No uterotonic

		- Suitable if
			- Low PPH risk
			- Informed maternal choice

		- Patient must understand
			- Higher bleeding risk

- Perineal protection bundle

	- Goal
		- Reduce obstetric anal sphincter injury

	- Components

		- Warm compress
			- During second stage
			- ↓ severe perineal trauma

		- Controlled delivery of head
			- Slow birth
			- Manual perineal support
			- Counter pressure

		- Episiotomy

			- Indications
				- Operative vaginal delivery
				- High tear risk

			- Technique
				- Mediolateral episiotomy
				- ≥60° angle
				- Perform at crowning

		- Perineal examination
			- Full genital examination
			- Rectal examination if indicated

			- Purpose
				- Detect occult sphincter injury

- Diet during labour

	- Allowed
		- Fluids
		- Light diet

	- No difference in
		- Caesarean rate
		- Operative delivery
		- Neonatal outcomes

	- Restrict only if
		- Risk of general anaesthesia

- Transfer planning

	- Limited-service birth setting
		- Discuss transfer before labour
		- Document transfer plan
		- Ensure transport pathway

	- Key principle
		- Maternal + neonatal safety priority

- AMC decision algorithm

	- Labour
		- Assess cervical dilation

	- ≤4 cm
		- Latent phase
		- Discharge home OR observe

	- ≥5 cm
		- Active labour
		- Vaginal examination every 4 hours

	- <2 cm dilation in 4 hours
		- Slow labour
		- Consider amniotomy ± oxytocin

	- Second stage
		- Full dilation

	- Escalate if pushing
		- ≥2 hours nulliparous
		- ≥1 hour multiparous

	- After birth
		- Delayed cord clamping
			- ≥60 seconds

	- Third stage
		- Active management
			- Oxytocin
			- Controlled cord traction
