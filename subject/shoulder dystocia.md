## Definition
- need for ‘additional obstetric manoevres to release the shoulders after gentle downward traction has failed’

## info
### complications
- Fetal
	- Hypoxia  
	- Death  
	- Fractures 
		- arm, clavicle  
	- Brachial plexus injury (Erb’s palsy)
- Maternal
	- PPH  
	- Perineal trauma  
	- 3rd/4th degree tears  

### pathogenesis
- Head delivers → restitution  
- Shoulders rotate to AP diameter  
- Anterior shoulder may impact under symphysis pubis  
- Rarely both or posterior shoulder impacted  

## Diagnosis
- Shoulders fail to deliver after head  
- “==Turtle sign==” → head retracts onto perineum  

## Management
### Immediate
- Call for help
- Document:
  - Time head delivered  
  - Anterior shoulder  
  - Time of each manoeuvre  
- - Need for additional manoeuvres after gentle downward traction fails (RCOG)  
- Lower bed, flatten back  
- Remove foot of bed  
- Consider episiotomy  
- Gentle traction only  
- \>5 min delay → risk cerebral damage  


### Sequence of Manoeuvres
1. MacRoberts manoeuvre
	- Flex + abduct hips (thighs to abdomen)  
	- Success ~90%  
2. Suprapubic pressure
	- Over posterior aspect of anterior shoulder  
	- Constant or rocking  
3. Internal manoeuvres
	- Rubin II → push anterior shoulder to chest  
	- Woods’ screw → rotate using posterior shoulder  
	- Reverse Woods’ screw  
	- Deliver posterior arm  
4. All fours position
	- Increases AP diameter  
	- Deliver posterior arm  
- Last resort
	- Symphysiotomy  
	- Zavanelli manoeuvre → replace head + Caesarean  
	- Intentional clavicle fracture  

### After Delivery
- Prevent PPH  
- Assess genital trauma  
- Debrief mother  
- Consider Caesarean in next pregnancy
