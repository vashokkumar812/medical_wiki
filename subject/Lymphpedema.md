## definition
- People with [[lymphoedema]] are prone to recurrent cellulitis  
  - Usually Streptococcus  

### Prevention
- Good skin care  
- Regular compression garment use  

### Treatment
- Urgent antibiotics required (minimum 14 days)

#### First line
- Phenoxymethylpenicillin 500 mg PO q6h  

#### Penicillin allergy
- Clindamycin 450 mg PO q8h  

#### If Staphylococcus suspected (e.g. folliculitis, pus)
- Flucloxacillin 500 mg PO q6h  

### Recurrent episodes
- Consider prophylaxis:
  - Phenoxymethylpenicillin 250 mg PO bid  
  - Initially 6 months  

### Supportive measures
- Elevate affected limb  
- Continue compression as tolerated  

### Clinical notes
- Lower limb cellulitis usually unilateral  
- Bilateral redness → consider:
  - Lipodermatosclerosis  
  - “Red Leg Syndrome”
