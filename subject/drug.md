# Haloperidol

- Indications
	- Marketed
		- Acute psychosis
		- Chronic psychosis
		- Acute mania
		- Gilles de la Tourette syndrome
		- Other choreas
		- Adjunct in alcoholic hallucinosis
		- Intractable nausea and vomiting
	- Accepted
		- Intractable hiccup

- Adverse Effects
	- Extrapyramidal side effects (EPSE)

- Practice Points
	- Can be used concurrently with a benzodiazepine
		- Potentiates sedation in acutely psychotic patients
	- Commonly used in elderly patients
		- Low incidence of hypotension
		- Low incidence of anticholinergic effects
		- Relatively high incidence of EPSE
