# Post-operative Complications

- **Fever causes**
	- <48 h
		- Atelectasis (most common)
	- Day 3–5
		- UTI
		- Pneumonia
	- Day 5–7
		- Wound infection
	- Any time
		- DVT/PE

- **Atelectasis**
	- Fever + low O₂ post-op
	- in smokers, upper gi surgery
	- Prevention
		- Early mobilisation
		- Incentive spirometry

- **DVT/PE**
	- Risk after surgery/immobility
	- PE
		- Sudden dyspnoea
		- Tachycardia
		- Pleuritic chest pain

- **Wound infection**
	- Redness
	- Tenderness
	- Purulent discharge
	- Usually day 5–7

- **Wound dehiscence**
	- Day 5–8
	- Sudden serosanguinous discharge
	- Risk
		- Obesity
		- Diabetes
		- Infection

- **Ileus**
	- Distension
	- Vomiting
	- Absent bowel sounds
	- Conservative management

- **Urinary retention**
	- Common after spinal anaesthesia/opioids
	- Suprapubic pain/distension

- **AMC pearls**
	- Most common early fever = atelectasis
	- Sudden post-op dyspnoea → exclude PE
	- Serosanguinous wound discharge → dehiscence
