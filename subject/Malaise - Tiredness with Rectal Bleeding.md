- Epidemiology
	- Tiredness prevalence in GP
		- 10–40%
	- Primary fatigue presentations
		- ~6%
	- Fatigue with psychological disorder
		- ~70%
	- OSA prevalence
		- 2–4% middle-aged men
	- Patients rarely attribute fatigue to psychological causes
