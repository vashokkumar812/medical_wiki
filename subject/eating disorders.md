| Feature | Anorexia nervosa ⭐⭐⭐ | Bulimia nervosa ⭐⭐⭐ |
|---------|------------------------|-----------------------|
| Weight | Underweight ⭐ | Usually normal/overweight ⭐ |
| Core feature | Restriction → low weight | Recurrent binge eating + compensatory behaviours |
| Body image | Distorted; fear of weight gain | Distorted; overconcern with weight/shape |
| Binge eating | May occur (binge-purge subtype) | Present (required) ⭐ |
| Purging | May or may not | Common ⭐ |
| Menstruation | Amenorrhoea common (not required for diagnosis) | Usually normal |
| Examination | Cachexia, lanugo, bradycardia, hypotension | Parotid enlargement, dental enamel erosion, knuckle calluses (Russell sign) ⭐ |
| Electrolytes | ↓K⁺, ↓PO₄³⁻, refeeding syndrome ⭐ | ↓K⁺, metabolic alkalosis ⭐ |
| Serious complication | Refeeding syndrome, arrhythmias | Arrhythmias from hypokalaemia |
| First-line treatment | Nutritional rehabilitation + CBT/family therapy ⭐ | CBT ⭐ |
| Medication | No drug is first-line for weight restoration | Fluoxetine ⭐ (adjunct to CBT) |
| Admission indications ⭐ | BMI <15, medical instability, electrolyte abnormality, suicidality | Severe electrolyte disturbance, dehydration, suicidality |
