- Cardiac troponins
	- Troponin I
	- Troponin T

	- Structural proteins of cardiac muscle

	- Released during myocyte damage and necrosis

	- Cornerstone for diagnosis of acute myocardial infarction (MI)

	- Modern assays
		- Extremely sensitive
		- Minor myocardial damage can elevate troponin

	- Elevated troponin also seen in
		- Pulmonary embolism
		- Septic shock
		- Pulmonary oedema
