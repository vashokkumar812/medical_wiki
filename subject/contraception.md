- Contraception – AMC First-Line + Avoid (with Expanded Abbreviations)

	- Breastfeeding (Lactation)
		- First-line
			- POP (Progestogen-Only Pill)
			- ENG Implant (Etonogestrel Implant)
			- LNG-IUD (Levonorgestrel Intrauterine Device)
			- DMPA (Depot Medroxyprogesterone Acetate)
		- Avoid
			- CHC (Combined Hormonal Contraception)
				- Especially <6 weeks postpartum

	- Immediate postpartum (<21 days)
		- First-line
			- ENG Implant
			- POP
			- LNG-IUD
			- Cu-IUD (Copper Intrauterine Device) when appropriate
		- Avoid
			- CHC

	- Heavy Menstrual Bleeding (HMB)
		- First-line
			- LNG-IUD
		- Avoid
			- Cu-IUD

	- Dysmenorrhoea
		- First-line
			- LNG-IUD
		- Avoid
			- Cu-IUD

	- Endometriosis
		- First-line
			- LNG-IUD
		- Alternative
			- Continuous COCP
		- Avoid
			- Cu-IUD if pain worsens

	- Adenomyosis
		- First-line
			- LNG-IUD
		- Avoid
			- Cu-IUD

	- PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome)
		- First-line
			- COCP
		- If estrogen contraindicated
			- POP
			- LNG-IUD
		- Avoid
			- POP alone if menstrual cycle regulation is the primary goal

	- Acne
		- First-line
			- COCP
		- Avoid
			- DMPA
			- Some POPs if acne worsens

	- Migraine with aura
		- First-line
			- POP
			- ENG Implant
			- LNG-IUD
			- Cu-IUD
		- Never
			- CHC

	- Migraine without aura
		- First-line
			- COCP (if no additional VTE risk factors)
		- Avoid
			- CHC if age ≥35 years or multiple VTE risks

	- Previous VTE (DVT/PE)
		- First-line
			- ENG Implant
			- LNG-IUD
			- Cu-IUD
		- Never
			- CHC

	- Known thrombophilia
		- First-line
			- POP
			- ENG Implant
			- LNG-IUD
			- Cu-IUD
		- Never
			- CHC

	- Smoker ≥35 years
		- First-line
			- ENG Implant
			- LNG-IUD
			- Cu-IUD
		- Avoid
			- CHC

	- Hypertension (BP ≥160/100 mmHg)
		- First-line
			- POP
			- ENG Implant
			- LNG-IUD
		- Never
			- CHC

	- Diabetes mellitus
		- Without vascular disease
			- First-line
				- LARC
		- With nephropathy, retinopathy, neuropathy or other vascular disease
			- First-line
				- ENG Implant
				- LNG-IUD
				- Cu-IUD
			- Avoid
				- CHC

	- Obesity (BMI ≥30 kg/m²)
		- First-line
			- ENG Implant
			- LNG-IUD
		- Avoid
			- CHC if multiple VTE risk factors

	- Epilepsy on enzyme-inducing AEDs
		- First-line
			- Cu-IUD
			- LNG-IUD
			- DMPA
		- Avoid relying on
			- COCP
			- POP
			- ENG Implant

	- Current breast cancer
		- First-line
			- Cu-IUD
		- Never
			- COCP
			- POP
			- ENG Implant
			- LNG-IUD
			- DMPA

	- Severe liver disease
		- First-line
			- Cu-IUD
		- Avoid
			- All estrogen-containing contraception
			- Most hormonal contraception

	- SLE (Systemic Lupus Erythematosus)
		- APS positive
			- First-line
				- POP
				- LNG-IUD
				- Cu-IUD
			- Avoid
				- CHC

	- HIV on ART
		- First-line
			- LARC
		- Avoid
			- Hormonal methods with significant ART interactions

	- High STI risk / Multiple sexual partners
		- First-line
			- Male condom
		- Plus
			- LARC if pregnancy prevention required
		- Avoid
			- Hormonal contraception alone

	- Hormone-free contraception requested
		- First-line
			- Cu-IUD
		- Alternative
			- Barrier methods

	- Wants pregnancy within the next year
		- First-line
			- ENG Implant
			- LNG-IUD
			- POP
			- COCP
		- Avoid
			- DMPA

	- Poor adherence (Frequently forgets pills)
		- First-line
			- ENG Implant
			- LNG-IUD
		- Avoid
			- COCP
			- POP

	- Emergency Contraception (EC)
		- First-line
			- Cu-IUD
		- If not suitable
			- UPA
			- LNG
		- Avoid
			- Starting hormonal contraception within 5 days after UPA

	- Current PID
		- First-line
			- Treat infection first
		- Avoid
			- Immediate IUD insertion

	- Distorted uterine cavity (e.g. fibroids)
		- First-line
			- ENG Implant
			- POP
		- Avoid
			- LNG-IUD
			- Cu-IUD

	- Unexplained vaginal bleeding
		- First-line
			- Investigate before choosing IUD
		- Avoid
			- Immediate IUD insertion

	- LAM (Lactational Amenorrhoea Method)
		- Effective only if ALL are present
			- Baby <6 months old
			- Exclusive or nearly exclusive breastfeeding
			- Amenorrhoea
		- If any criterion absent
			- Start another contraceptive method

	- AMC "Never Choose" List
		- Migraine with aura → Never CHC
		- Previous DVT/PE → Never CHC
		- Current breast cancer → Never hormonal contraception
		- Smoker ≥35 years → Avoid CHC
		- Severe hypertension → Never CHC
		- Stroke (CVA) or IHD → Never CHC
		- Heavy menstrual bleeding → Avoid Cu-IUD
		- Dysmenorrhoea → Avoid Cu-IUD
		- Enzyme-inducing AEDs → Do not rely on COCP, POP or ENG Implant
		- High STI risk → Never rely on hormonal contraception alone


- Missed Combined Oral Contraceptive Pill (COCP)

	- Missed pill
		- <48 hours since a pill should have been taken
			- Take the missed pill immediately
			- Continue remaining pills as usual
			- Even if two pills taken on same day
			- No emergency contraception (EC) required
			- No backup contraception required

		- ≥48 hours since a pill should have been taken
			- Take the most recent missed pill immediately
			- Discard earlier missed pills
			- Continue remaining pills as usual
			- Use condoms or abstain for the next 7 days

			- If missed pills occurred in Week 1
				- Unprotected sexual intercourse (UPSI) in previous 5 days
					- Consider Emergency Contraception (EC)

			- If missed pills occurred in Week 3
				- Skip hormone-free interval
				- Finish active pills
				- Start next pack immediately
				- No pill-free break

- Missed Progestogen-Only Pill (POP)

	- Traditional POP (e.g. norethisterone, levonorgestrel)
		- >3 hours late
			- Take pill immediately
			- Continue as normal
			- Use condoms for 48 hours
			- Consider EC if UPSI occurred

	- Desogestrel POP
		- >12 hours late
			- Take pill immediately
			- Continue as normal
			- Use condoms for 48 hours
			- Consider EC if UPSI occurred

	- Drospirenone POP
		- >24 hours late
			- Take missed pill immediately
			- Continue remaining pills
			- Use backup contraception for 7 days

- AMC High-Yield MCQ Points

	- <48-hour missed COCP
		- Take immediately
		- No backup required

	- ≥48-hour missed COCP
		- Take last missed pill
		- Continue pack
		- Condoms for 7 days

	- Week 1 + missed ≥48 hours + UPSI
		- Consider EC

	- Week 3 + missed ≥48 hours
		- Skip placebo/pill-free week
		- Start next pack immediately

	- POP
		- Traditional POP → >3 hours = missed
		- Desogestrel POP → >12 hours = missed
		- Drospirenone POP → >24 hours = missed

- AMC Exam Pearl

	- COCP
		- <48-hour delay → No EC, no backup
		- ≥48-hour delay → Backup 7 days
		- Week 1 + UPSI → Consider EC
		- Week 3 → Skip pill-free interval

	- POP
		- Late beyond the allowed window → Backup required
		- Consider EC if UPSI occurred after the missed pill
