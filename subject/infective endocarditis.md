- Definition
	- Inflammation of endocardium including heart valves

- Pathophysiology
	- Bacteraemia
		→ Adhesion
		→ Colonisation

- Aetiology

	- Native valve (community-acquired)
		- streptococci
			- Viridans 
			- gallolyticus (S. bovis)
		- Staphylococcus aureus
	- Healthcare-associated IE
		- Staphylococcus 
			- aureus
			- coagulase negative
		- Enterococci
	- Prosthetic valve — early (<1 year)
		- Staphylococcus 
			- aureus
			- Coagulase-negative 
		- Gram-negative bacilli
		- Fungi
	- Prosthetic valve — late (>1 year)
		- streptococci Viridans 
		- Staphylococcus aureus
		- Enterococci
	- IV drug users
		- Staphylococcus aureus
		- Streptococci
		- Pseudomonas aeruginosa
		- Candida
	- Elderly patients
		- Enterococci
		- Streptococcus gallolyticus

	- Post-dental procedure
		- Viridans streptococci

	- GU/GI source
		- Enterococci
		- Streptococcus gallolyticus

	- Culture-negative IE
		- HACEK organisms
		- Coxiella burnetii
		- Bartonella
		- Brucella

- Predisposing Factors
	- Valvular disease
	- Cardiac defects
	- Prosthetic valves
	- IV drug use

	- Following procedures
		- Dental work
			- e.g. extraction
		- Instrumentation
			- e.g. urethral dilatation
		- Surgery
			- Tonsillectomy
			- Abortion
		- Central venous catheters

- Prognosis
	- Mortality
		- 6–30%
	- ~15% previously diagnosed heart disease

- Complications
	- Emboli
		- Brain
		- Lung
		- Spleen
		- Extremities

- Diagnosis
	- Classic triad
		- Fever of unknown origin
		- Cardiac murmur
		- Embolism

- Modified Duke Criteria
	- Diagnosis made by:
		- 2 major criteria
		- OR 1 major + 3 minor
		- OR 5 minor

- Major Criteria

	- Positive blood cultures

		- Typical organisms from 2 separate cultures
			- Viridans streptococci
			- Streptococcus bovis
			- HACEK group
			- Community-acquired Staphylococcus aureus
			- Enterococci without primary focus

		- Persistently positive cultures
			- ≥2 cultures >12 hrs apart
			- OR majority of 4 cultures positive
			- First and last ≥1 hr apart

	- Evidence of endocardial involvement

		- Echocardiographic evidence
			- Oscillating intracardiac mass
			- Abscess
			- Partial prosthetic valve dehiscence

		- Valvular dysfunction
			- New valvular regurgitation
			- Murmur change alone insufficient

- Minor Criteria
	- Predisposition
		- Heart disease
		- IV drug use

	- Fever
		- ≥38°C

	- Vascular phenomena
		- Major arterial emboli
		- Septic pulmonary infarcts
		- Mycotic aneurysm
		- Intracranial haemorrhage
		- Conjunctival haemorrhage
		- Janeway lesions

	- Immunological phenomena
		- Glomerulonephritis
		- Osler nodes
		- Roth spots
		- Rheumatoid factor

	- Microbiological evidence
		- Positive culture not meeting major criteria
		- Positive serology consistent with IE

	- Echocardiographic findings
		- Suggestive but not major criteria

- Investigations
	- Blood cultures before antibiotics

		- Technique
			- 10 mL from 2 sites
			- 20 min apart

		- If insufficient sample
			- Prioritise aerobic bottle
			- Ignore anaerobic bottle

		- If pathogen identified
			- Repeat daily cultures until negative

		- Endocarditis risk patient
			- 2 blood cultures

		- Suspected IE presentation
			- 3 blood cultures

		- Avoid IV cannula cultures except
			- Suspected CA-BSI

	- Echocardiography

		- Low-risk patient
			- TTE

		- High-risk/prosthetic valve
			- TOE

		- Avoid TOE if
			- Not surgical candidate

- Management
	- Take blood cultures first

	- Start empiric IV antibiotics ASAP
		- Benzyl penicillin
		- Flucloxacillin
		- Gentamicin
