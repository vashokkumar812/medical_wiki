- Ultrasound differentiation of common adnexal masses (AMC MCQ)

	- Dermoid cyst (Mature cystic teratoma) ⭐⭐⭐
		- Young women (20–40 years)
		- Mixed echogenicity
		- Echogenic mural nodule (**Rokitansky nodule**) ⭐
		- Fat-fluid level
		- Calcifications or teeth ⭐
		- Hair → echogenic lines/dots
		- Acoustic shadowing ("tip of the iceberg sign") ⭐
		- Complication
			- Ovarian torsion ⭐

---

	- Serous cystadenoma
		- Benign epithelial ovarian tumour
		- Thin-walled
		- Unilocular ⭐
		- Anechoic simple cyst
		- Few or no septations
		- No solid components
		- No calcification

---

	- Corpus luteum cyst
		- Reproductive age
		- Thick wall
		- Internal echoes (blood)
		- Peripheral vascularity ("Ring of fire") ⭐
		- May appear haemorrhagic
		- Usually resolves spontaneously

---

	- Pedunculated fibroid
		- Arises from uterus ⭐
		- Solid hypoechoic mass
		- Whorled appearance ⭐
		- Shadowing/calcification may occur
		- Doppler shows feeding vessel from uterus ("bridging vessel sign") ⭐
		- Separate normal ovary identified

---

	- Hydrosalpinx
		- Dilated fallopian tube ⭐
		- Tubular or C-shaped cystic structure
		- Incomplete septae
		- "Cogwheel sign" (acute)
		- "Beads-on-a-string sign" (chronic) ⭐
		- Usually history of PID/infertility

---

- AMC Quick Differentiation ⭐

	- Dermoid
		- Fat + calcification + teeth + hair
		- Rokitansky nodule
		- Tip of the iceberg sign

	- Serous cystadenoma
		- Simple thin-walled unilocular cyst

	- Corpus luteum
		- Thick wall
		- Ring of fire Doppler

	- Pedunculated fibroid
		- Solid uterine mass
		- Bridging vessels

	- Hydrosalpinx
		- Tubular fluid-filled structure
		- Beads-on-a-string
		- Cogwheel sign
