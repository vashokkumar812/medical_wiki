- Malaria (AMC High-Yield)

	- Definition
		- Protozoal infection caused by *Plasmodium* species
		- Transmitted by female *Anopheles* mosquito

	- Species
		- *P. falciparum* ⭐
			- Most severe
			- Cerebral malaria
			- High mortality
			- Chloroquine resistance common
		- *P. vivax* ⭐
			- Most common outside Africa
			- Dormant liver hypnozoites
			- Relapses
		- *P. ovale*
			- Hypnozoites
			- Relapses
		- *P. malariae*
			- Chronic infection
			- Quartan fever (72-hour cycle)
		- *P. knowlesi*
			- Southeast Asia
			- Daily (24-hour) fever
			- Can be severe

	- Transmission
		- Female *Anopheles* mosquito
		- Blood transfusion
		- Organ transplantation
		- Congenital

	- Incubation
		- Usually 7–30 days
		- Longer with chemoprophylaxis

	- Clinical Features
		- Fever ⭐
		- Chills
		- Rigors
		- Sweating
		- Headache
		- Myalgia
		- Malaise
		- Nausea/vomiting
		- Abdominal pain
		- Splenomegaly
		- Hepatomegaly
		- Jaundice

	- Fever Pattern
		- *P. falciparum*
			- Irregular fever
		- *P. vivax* / *P. ovale*
			- Every 48 hours (tertian)
		- *P. malariae*
			- Every 72 hours (quartan)
		- *P. knowlesi*
			- Every 24 hours (quotidian)

	- Severe Malaria (Usually *P. falciparum*) ⭐
		- Altered consciousness
		- Cerebral malaria
		- Seizures
		- Severe anaemia
		- Acute kidney injury
		- Hypoglycaemia
		- Metabolic acidosis
		- Pulmonary oedema/ARDS
		- Shock
		- DIC
		- Haemoglobinuria ("blackwater fever")
		- Hyperparasitaemia

	- Diagnosis ⭐
		- Thick blood film
			- Most sensitive
			- Detects parasites
		- Thin blood film
			- Species identification
			- Parasite density
		- Repeat smears every 12–24 hours ×3 if initial negative
		- Rapid diagnostic antigen test
			- Useful if microscopy unavailable
		- PCR
			- Highly sensitive
			- Not routine

	- Laboratory Findings
		- Anaemia
		- Thrombocytopenia ⭐
		- Raised bilirubin
		- Raised LDH
		- Elevated transaminases
		- Hypoglycaemia
		- Raised creatinine (severe disease)

	- Treatment

		- Uncomplicated *P. falciparum*
			- Artemisinin-based combination therapy (ACT) ⭐

		- Severe malaria
			- IV artesunate ⭐
			- If unavailable → IV quinine
			- ICU supportive care

		- *P. vivax* / *P. ovale*
			- Chloroquine if sensitive
			- ACT if resistant
			- Primaquine for hypnozoites ⭐
				- Only after normal G6PD level

	- Primaquine
		- Eradicates liver hypnozoites
		- Prevents relapse
		- Required for
			- *P. vivax*
			- *P. ovale*
		- Contraindicated in
			- G6PD deficiency
			- Pregnancy

	- Prevention
		- Insecticide-treated bed nets
		- DEET insect repellent
		- Protective clothing
		- Mosquito avoidance
		- Chemoprophylaxis
			- Atovaquone-proguanil
			- Doxycycline
			- Mefloquine

	- AMC Pearls ⭐
		- Fever after travel to endemic area = Malaria until proven otherwise
		- Thick smear = Most sensitive test
		- Thin smear = Species identification
		- Repeat blood films if suspicion remains high
		- *P. falciparum* = Most severe
		- IV artesunate = Treatment of severe malaria
		- *P. vivax*/*P. ovale* = Liver hypnozoites
		- Primaquine eradicates hypnozoites
		- Check G6PD before primaquine
		- Thrombocytopenia is a common laboratory clue
		- Cerebral malaria is almost always due to *P. falciparum*
