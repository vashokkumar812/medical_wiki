

# aortic dissection

![[Pasted image 20260529062227.png]]


- definition
	-  tear occurs in the inner wall of the aorta
- risk factors
	- [[SHTN|hypertension]]
	- pre-existing [[aortic aneurysm]]
	- Marfan syndrome
	- inflammatory vasculitis
		- [[giant cell arteritis]]
		- syphilitic aortitis
		- Takayasu arteritis
	- bicuspid aortic valve
	- coarctation of aorta
	- iatrogenic trauma

- Complications
	- myocardial infarction
		- dissection proximal to coronary arteries
		- inferior [[ischemic heart disease]] + widened mediastinum suggestive
		- diagnosis:
			- transoesophageal echocardiography
		- avoid
			- thrombolysis
			- aspirin
	- aortic regurgitation
	- [[Cardiac Tamponade]]
	- pleural effusion
	- intestinal ischaemia
	- renal ischaemia
		- oliguria
		- haematuria

- Symptoms/signs
	- pain
		- sudden onset
		- severe
		- sharp/tearing
		- radiates to back
			- retrosternal
			- interscapular
			- lower back
		- migratory
		- opioid resistant
	- pulse deficits
		- unequal radial pulses
		- absent femoral pulses
	- BP discrepancy between arms
	- if aortic regurgitation develops
		- diastolic murmur

- Investigations
	- impaired renal function
		- TEE
	- otherwise
		- contrast CT scan
			- investigation of choice
	- chest X-ray
		- widened mediastinum

- Management
	- medical
		- analgesia
			- morphine
		- reduce shear stress
			- beta-blockers
				- first-line
			- calcium channel blockers
				- if beta-blockers contraindicated
		- vasodilation
			- sodium nitroprusside
				- only with beta-blocker
	- definitive
		- surgical repair
		- endovascular stent graft
