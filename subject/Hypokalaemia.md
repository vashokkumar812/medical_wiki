## Overview
- Most common electrolyte abnormality in hospitalised patients  
- Causes: mostly drugs & GI disease  
- Most abundant cation in body  
- Distribution:
  - 98% intracellular
  - 2% extracellular  
- Regulation: kidney (90% excretion), rest via GI  
- Normal range: **3.5–5.0 mmol/L**

---

## Severity
- **Mild:** 3.0–3.5 mmol/L  
- **Moderate:** 2.5–3.0 mmol/L  
- **Severe:** <2.5 mmol/L  

---

## Causes (Mnemonic: **Dr from MIT**)

### Decreased Intake
- Poor dietary intake, starvation  

### Magnesium Depletion
- Increases renal K⁺ loss (poor diet, ↑ Mg²⁺ loss)  

### Mineralocorticoid Excess
- Primary aldosteronism  
- Cushing’s syndrome  
- Accelerated hypertension  
- Renal vascular hypertension  
- Renin-producing tumour  
- Adrenogenital syndrome  
- Licorice excess  
- Bartter syndrome  
- Liddle syndrome  

### Increased Loss
- **Drugs:** diuretics, laxatives, steroids, liquorice, antibiotics (carbapenems, gentamicin, amphotericin B)  
- **Skin:** profuse sweating, extensive burns  
- **GI:** diarrhoea, vomiting, ileostomy, fistula, villous adenoma  
- **Renal:** tubular disorders, nephrogenic DI, syndromes  
- **Endocrine:** hyperaldosteronism, Cushing’s, Conn’s  
- **Dialysis:** low K⁺ dialysate, peritoneal dialysis  

### Transcellular Shift
- Insulin/glucose therapy  
- β-agonists  
- Alkalosis (respiratory/metabolic)  
- Hypokalaemic periodic paralysis  

---

## History
- **Mild:** often asymptomatic  
- Fatigue, cramps, [[weakness]], [[constipation]]  
- Severe: rhabdomyolysis, ascending paralysis, resp. failure, arrhythmias  
- Symptoms worse in IHD/CHF/LVH  
- Medications: both causative & ↑ risk (e.g., [[Sotalol]] + hypokalaemia → arrhythmias)  

---

## Examination
- **CVS:** arrhythmias, instability  
- **Neuro:** [[weakness]], sensory changes  

---

## Investigations
- Serum: K⁺, Mg²⁺, Ca²⁺, phosphate  
- **ECG (severe):**  
  - U waves  
  - T wave flattening  
  - ST [[depression]] → VT/VF  
  - Long QT, Torsades  
- [[digoxin]] level (higher risk with low K⁺)  

---

## Management
- Always replace **Mg²⁺** (facilitates K⁺ correction)  
- **Non-acute:** 10–20 mmol/hr IV  
- **Life-threatening arrhythmia:**  
  - K⁺ 20 mmol over 10 min  
  - Mg²⁺ 10 mmol over 10 min
