| ASA class | Description                                                | Simple example                                |
| --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| ASA I     | Healthy patient                                            | Healthy young adult                           |
| ASA II    | Mild systemic disease                                      | Controlled HTN, mild asthma, smoker           |
| ASA III   | Severe systemic disease with functional limitation         | Poorly controlled DM, COPD, CKD               |
| ASA IV    | Severe systemic disease that is a constant threat to life  | Unstable angina, severe heart failure, sepsis |
| ASA V     | Moribund patient not expected to survive without operation | Ruptured AAA, massive trauma                  |
| ASA VI    | Brain-dead patient for organ donation                      | Organ donor                                   |
| E suffix  | Emergency surgery                                          | ASA IIIE = severe disease + emergency surgery |
