## Primary vs Secondary Hyperaldosteronism

| Feature | Primary Hyperaldosteronism | Secondary Hyperaldosteronism |
|------|---------------------------|-----------------------------|
| Aldosterone | ↑ | ↑ |
| Renin | ↓ | ↑ |
| Common causes | Bilateral adrenal hyperplasia<br>Adrenal adenoma (Conn syndrome) | Renovascular hypertension<br>JG cell tumor (renin-producing)<br>Cirrhosis<br>Heart failure<br>Nephrotic syndrome |
| Blood pressure | Treatment-resistant hypertension | Hypertension (often volume-related) |
| Potassium | ↓ or normal | ↓ or normal |
| Acid–base status | Metabolic alkalosis | Metabolic alkalosis |
| Edema | Absent (aldosterone escape) | Present in edema states |
| Aldosterone escape | Preserved | Impaired in HF, cirrhosis, nephrotic syndrome |
