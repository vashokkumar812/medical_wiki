## Overview
- Most common of the 7 types of idiopathic interstitial [[pneumonia]] (IIP)
- Chronic, progressive, and irreversible fibrosing disease
- Median survival: 2–4 years
- Clinical syndrome associated with **Usual Interstitial [[pneumonia]] (UIP)** pattern

## Clinical Features

### History
- Age: usually >50 years
- Progressive [[dyspnea]]
- Nonproductive cough
- Smoking history is a risk factor

### Examination
- [[Clubbing]]
- Fine inspiratory crackles

## Investigations

### Chest X-ray (CXR)
- Often normal early
- Later: subpleural reticular opacities, progressing apex → base

### High-Resolution CT (HRCT)
- Low lung volumes
- Subpleural reticular opacities (intralobular and interlobular septal thickening)
- Macrocystic honeycombing
- Traction [[bronchiectasis]]
- Extent increases apex → base
- Characteristic HRCT may be diagnostic

### Lung Biopsy
- Multiple sites if imaging atypical (e.g., extensive ground-glass opacities, nodules, consolidation, peri-bronchovascular distribution)

## Management
- **ICU admission** is ethically and clinically challenging due to poor outcomes
- Exacerbations require careful decisions regarding mechanical support
- High-dose corticosteroids ± immunosuppressants: ineffective
- Supportive care and treat underlying triggers only
- Early consideration for **lung transplantation**
