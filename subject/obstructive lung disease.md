# copd
- Definition
	- Chronic airflow limitation
	- Not fully reversible

- Main risk factor
	- Cigarette smoking
	- Passive smoking
	- Occupational exposure
	- Air pollution
	- Asthma

- Symptoms
	- Progressive dyspnoea
	- Chronic cough
	- Sputum production
	- Wheeze

- Diagnosis
	- Spirometry required
	- Post-bronchodilator
		- FEV1/FVC <0.7
	- FEV1 used to grade severity

- Differentiation from asthma
	- COPD
		- Incompletely reversible airflow obstruction
	- Asthma
		- Reversible obstruction

-  treatment
	- Smoking cessation
		- Most effective intervention
		- Nicotine replacement
		- Bupropion
		- Varenicline
	- Pharmacological treatment
		- Short-acting bronchodilators
		- LABA/LAMA
		- Inhaled corticosteroids
			- Frequent exacerbations/severe disease
	- Non-pharmacological management
		- Pulmonary rehabilitation
		- Exercise training
		- Education/self-management

- Vaccination
	- Annual influenza vaccine
	- Pneumococcal vaccine

- Long-term oxygen therapy
	- >15 h/day
	- Indicated if severe chronic hypoxaemia
		- PaO2 <55 mmHg
	- Also if pulmonary hypertension/cor pulmonale

- Exacerbations
	- Worsening dyspnoea
	- Increased sputum
	- Increased sputum purulence

- Exacerbation management
	- Short-acting bronchodilators
	- Oral corticosteroids
	- Antibiotics if infective features
	- Oxygen if hypoxic

- Important points
	- COPD commonly underdiagnosed
	- Spirometry essential for diagnosis
	- Smoking cessation slows progression
