- Urethral caruncle
	- Definition
		- Benign granulomatous tumour

- Epidemiology
	- Almost exclusively affects postmenopausal women

- Clinical features
	- Haematuria
	- Very tender lesion
	- Bleeds easily

- Investigations
	- Cystoscopy
	- Biopsy

- Treatment
	- Warm salt baths
	- Oestrogen creams
	- Laser vaporisation
	- Cryotherapy
	- Cautery
	- Surgical excision
