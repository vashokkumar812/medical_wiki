	- requires investigation if unexplained

	- UPCR

- assessment
	- blood pressure
	- U&E
	- GFR
	- albumin
	- glucose
- additional investigations
	- ANA
	- dsDNA
	- C3
	- C4
	- ANCA
	- hepatitis B serology
	- hepatitis C serology
