- giant cell arteritis
	- suspect in
		- age >50 years
		- sudden visual loss

	- symptoms/signs
		- headache
		- jaw claudication
		- scalp tenderness
		- visual loss
		- temporal artery tenderness
		- polymyalgia rheumatica features

	- investigations
		- ESR
			- may be normal in 17%
		- CRP
			- most sensitive
		- platelet count
			- thrombocytosis supports diagnosis
		- FBC
		- temporal artery biopsy
			- definitive diagnosis

	- ESR upper limit normal
		- males
			- age ÷ 2
		- females
			- (age ÷ 2) + 10

	- treatment
		- start corticosteroids immediately if suspected
		- do not delay for biopsy

	- biopsy
		- still useful within 4–7 days after starting steroids

	- complications
		- permanent visual loss
		- contralateral eye involvement
		- stroke
		- bowel ischaemia
		- myocardial ischaemia
