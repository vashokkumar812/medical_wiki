| Feature      | Postpartum blues                                                    | Postpartum depression                                                                             | Postpartum psychosis                                                                       |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Onset        | 2–3 days after delivery                                             | Within weeks to 12 months after delivery                                                          | Usually within 2 weeks after delivery                                                      |
| Duration     | <2 weeks                                                            | >2 weeks                                                                                          | Acute psychiatric emergency                                                                |
| Frequency    | Very common                                                         | Common                                                                                            | Rare                                                                                       |
| Symptoms     | Tearfulness, mood swings, anxiety, irritability, emotional lability | Persistent low mood, anhedonia, fatigue, poor sleep, guilt, poor concentration, suicidal thoughts | Delusions, hallucinations, mania, severe depression, confusion, disorganised behaviour     |
| Insight      | Preserved                                                           | Usually preserved                                                                                 | Often absent                                                                               |
| Risk to baby | No                                                                  | Possible due to impaired bonding or suicide risk                                                  | High risk of suicide or infanticide                                                        |
| Treatment    | Reassurance and support                                             | Psychological therapy ± antidepressants                                                           | Immediate hospital admission, urgent psychiatric review, antipsychotics ± mood stabilisers |
| Prognosis    | Resolves spontaneously                                              | Good with treatment                                                                               | Good with early treatment but high recurrence risk                                         |
