| Personality Disorder | One-line Definition |
|---|---|
| **Paranoid PD** | **Pervasive distrust and suspicion** that others have malicious intentions. |
| **Schizoid PD** | **Detachment from social relationships** with limited emotional expression. |
| **Schizotypal PD** | **Social detachment with eccentric behaviour and odd beliefs/thinking.** |
| **Histrionic PD** | **Excessive attention-seeking** through dramatic and emotional behaviour. |
| **Borderline PD** | **Instability of relationships, emotions, self-image, and impulse control.** |
| **Antisocial PD** | **Persistent violation of others' rights** with lack of remorse. |
| **Narcissistic PD** | **Grandiose self-importance** with need for admiration and lack of empathy. |
| **Avoidant PD** | **Avoids social relationships due to fear of rejection** despite wanting them. |
| **Dependent PD** | **Excessive need to be cared for**, leading to submissive and dependent behaviour. |
| **Obsessive-Compulsive PD (OCPD)** | **Preoccupation with perfectionism, order, and control** at the expense of flexibility. |
