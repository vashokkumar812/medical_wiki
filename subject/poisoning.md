- **Pathophysiology**
	- CO binds Hb (200× > O₂)
	- ↓ O₂ delivery → tissue hypoxia

- Symptoms
	- Headache
	- Dizziness
	- Nausea
	- Confusion
	- Syncope/coma

- Clues
	- Multiple affected people
	- Normal pulse oximetry

- Diagnosis
	- ABG + co-oximetry
	- ↑ Carboxyhaemoglobin

- Management
	- 100% O₂ immediately
	- Remove exposure
	- ABCDE

	- LOC
