## Overview
- Usually associated with **sodium changes**  
- Often **inversely related to bicarbonate (HCO₃⁻)**  

---

## Causes

### 1. Drugs
- Acetazolamide  
- Aspirin overdose  
- Steroids  
- Oestrogens  
- Thiazides  
- Androgens  
- IV NaCl replacement  

---

### 2. Metabolic
- **Respiratory alkalosis:** ↓ Ca²⁺, K⁺, Mg²⁺; ↑ Cl⁻  
- **Metabolic acidosis (NAGMA):** normal anion gap metabolic acidosis  

---

### 3. Endocrine
- Hypothalamic lesion → ↑ Na⁺, ↓ thirst perception  
- Adrenocortical hyperfunction  

---

### 4. Renal
- Renal tubular acidosis (RTA)  
- Acute renal failure (ARF)  
- Diabetes insipidus (DI)  

---

### 5. Gastrointestinal
- Dehydration  
- Prolonged diarrhoea  

---

### 6. Fictitious
- **Bromide toxicity:** bromide measured as chloride in routine assays → spuriously high chloride
