### Renin
- Secreted by
	- JG cells
- Released in response to:
  - ↓ Renal perfusion pressure (afferent arteriole)
  - ↑ Renal sympathetic discharge (β1 effect)
  - ↓ NaCl delivery to macula densa

### ACE (Angiotensin-Converting Enzyme)
- Converts angiotensin I → angiotensin II
- Present in many tissues
- Major site of action: lung
- Produced by vascular endothelial cells

### Angiotensin II (AT II)
- Maintains blood volume and blood pressure
- Alters baroreceptor function
- Limits reflex bradycardia despite pressor effect

### ANP & BNP
- Released in response to ↑ volume
- Sources:
  - ANP → atria
  - BNP → ventricles
- Effects:
  - Inhibits RAAS
  - Relaxes vascular smooth muscle (via cGMP)
  - ↑ GFR
  - ↓ Renin
  - Dilates afferent arteriole
  - Promotes natriuresis

### ADH (Vasopressin)
- Regulates serum osmolality
- Also released in low blood volume
- Actions:
  - ↑ Water reabsorption in collecting ducts
  - ↑ Urea reabsorption in collecting ducts
  - Maintains corticopapillary osmotic gradient

### Aldosterone
- Regulates ECF volume and Na⁺ content
- ↑ Release in hypovolemia
- Responds to hyperkalemia:
  - ↑ K⁺ excretion
