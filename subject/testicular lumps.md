#mcq
## Epidemiology
- **Most common solid cancer in men 18–39 years**

## Risk Factors
- Family history
- **Undescended testis (cryptorchidism)**
- Congenital inguinal hernia

## Clinical Point
Most lumps are **benign**

Examples:
- Cyst
- Hydrocele

BUT

**Any testicular lump → urgent medical evaluation**

## Screening
Routine **testicular self examination NOT recommended**

Recommendation:
- Encourage **testicular awareness**
