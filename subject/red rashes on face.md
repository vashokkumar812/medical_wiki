# Red Rash on Face – Differential Diagnosis

- **Papulopustular rashes**
	- Acne vulgaris
		- Comedones present
	- Rosacea
		- Central facial erythema
		- Telangiectasia
		- No comedones
	- Periorificial dermatitis
		- Around mouth/nose/eyes
		- Spares vermillion border
		- Steroid associated
	- Malassezia folliculitis
		- Monomorphic papules/pustules
	- Pseudofolliculitis barbae
		- Shaving areas

- **Scaly rashes**
	- Seborrhoeic dermatitis
		- Greasy scale
		- Scalp/nasolabial folds/eyebrows
	- Psoriasis
		- Well-defined plaques
		- Hairline/scalp/ears
	- Atopic dermatitis
		- Pruritic
		- Dry skin
	- Contact dermatitis
		- Irritant or allergic
	- Tinea faciei
		- Annular lesion
		- Central clearing
		- Peripheral scale
	- Actinic keratosis
		- Rough scaly lesions
		- Sun-exposed skin

- **Mixed/other rashes**
	- SLE
		- Malar rash
		- Photosensitivity
	- Dermatomyositis
		- Violaceous heliotrope rash
		- Muscle weakness
	- Herpes zoster
		- Painful unilateral dermatomal rash
		- Does not cross midline
	- Sunburn
		- Erythema ± blistering

- **Key distinguishing features**
	- Rosacea
		- Triggered by alcohol/spicy food/heat
	- Acne
		- Comedones
	- Periorificial dermatitis
		- Steroid use history
	- Tinea
		- Animal/barber exposure
	- Seborrhoeic dermatitis
		- Greasy scale
	- SLE
		- Systemic symptoms

- **Important investigations**
	- Swab for bacterial culture
	- Skin scraping for fungal microscopy/culture
	- VZV PCR if shingles suspected
	- Skin biopsy if unclear diagnosis

# Red Rash on Face — AMC Differential Table

| Diagnosis | Key morphology | Distribution | Key clues | Investigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Atopic dermatitis** | Erythema, scale, xerosis, lichenification | Face, neck, eyelids | Atopy history, severe itch | Clinical |
| **Irritant contact dermatitis** | Dry erythematous scaly rash | Contact areas | New products, over-cleansing | Clinical |
| **Allergic contact dermatitis** | Erythema, vesicles, oedema | Eyelids, lips, neck | Delayed hypersensitivity, pruritus | Patch testing |
| **Seborrhoeic dermatitis** | Greasy scale, erythema | Scalp, eyebrows, nasolabial folds | Dandruff, winter flares | Clinical |
| **Psoriasis** | Well-defined scaly plaques | Hairline, scalp, ears | Family history, extensor plaques | Clinical/biopsy |
| **Actinic keratosis** | Rough scaly plaques | Sun-exposed areas | Chronic sun exposure | Biopsy if suspicious |
| **Acne vulgaris** | Papules, pustules, comedones | Face, chest, back | Comedones present | Clinical |
| **Rosacea** | Papules/pustules + erythema/telangiectasia | Central face | Flushing, triggers, no comedones | Clinical |
| **Malassezia folliculitis** | Monomorphic papules/pustules | Forehead, chin, chest/back | Oily skin, itchy | KOH/Wood lamp |
| **Pseudofolliculitis barbae** | Papules/pustules, ingrown hairs | Beard area | Shaving, curly hair | Clinical |
| **Periorificial dermatitis** | Papules/pustules | Around mouth/nose/eyes | Steroid use, vermillion spared | Clinical |
| **Tinea faciei** | Annular scaly lesion | Asymmetrical face | Central clearing, steroid worsening | Fungal scraping |
| **Sunburn** | Erythema ± blistering | Sun-exposed skin | UV exposure | Clinical |
| **Shingles (HZV)** | Vesicles/crusting | Dermatomal unilateral rash | Pain precedes rash | VZV PCR |
| **Cutaneous lupus** | Malar erythema/scaly plaques | Cheeks/nose | Photosensitivity, systemic symptoms | ANA/dsDNA/biopsy |
| **Dermatomyositis** | Violaceous heliotrope rash | Eyelids/scalp | Muscle weakness | CK, antibodies |
| **Systemic causes** | Facial plethora/erythema | Variable | Dyspnoea, oedema, systemic disease | Directed investigations |
