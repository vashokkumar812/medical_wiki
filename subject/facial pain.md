# Trigeminal Neuralgia
- AKA
	- Tic douloureux
- causes
	- primary
		- compression of trigeminal nerve by superior cerebellar artery
	- secondary
		- Cerebellopontine angle tumors
		- multiple sclerosis
- Features
	- Repeated stimulation fails to trigger attack
	- Severe lightning-like pain for 60 seconds with relapse and remitting
	- Sudden wince during attack
	- Usually maxillary (V2) or mandibular (V3) divisions
	- Pain localised to small facial area
	- may
		- mimic dental pain
		- have trigger zone
		- continuous dull ache or  asymptomatic between attacks
- Triggers
	- Eating
	- Drinking
	- Talking
	- Jaw movement
	- Wind on face
	- Light touch

- Trigeminal Neuralgia Pathophysiology
	- superior cerebellar artery ==> Compresses trigeminal nerve root
- differential diagnosis
	- Young patient
		- Consider multiple sclerosis
- Imaging
	- MRI usually normal
		- May identify
			- Tumour
			- Vascular anomaly
	- High-resolution MRI sequences
		- T2-CISS
		- MRA
	- May demonstrate vascular compression
- Treatment
	- First-line
		- Carbamazepine
			- Monitor for toxicity
			- Serum levels have limited value
		- Alternatives
			- Gabapentin
			- Pregabalin
			- Lamotrigine
	- Local treatments
		- Local anaesthetic infiltration
		- Regional nerve block
		- Bupivacaine + corticosteroid
	- Surgery
		- Microvascular decompressION
		- Long-term relapse possible
