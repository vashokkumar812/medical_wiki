# Syphilis in MSM

- **Secondary syphilis**
	- Condylomata lata
		- Flat moist perianal lesions
	- Rash
	- Lymphadenopathy

- **Differentials of perianal lesions**
	- Genital warts (HPV)
	- HSV
	- Molluscum contagiosum
	- Haemorrhoids
	- Anal SCC

- **MSM STI screening**
	- Gonorrhoea NAAT
		- Throat
		- Rectum
	- Chlamydia NAAT
		- Urine
		- Throat
		- Rectum
	- HIV serology
	- Syphilis serology
	- Hepatitis A/B serology
	- Hepatitis C if HIV+/IVDU

- **Investigations**
	- Syphilis serology
		- RPR/VDRL
	- Lesion swab NAAT
		- Can become positive before serology

- **Treatment**
	- Benzathine penicillin IM
		- Early syphilis
			- Single dose
		- Late/unknown duration
			- Weekly × 3 weeks

- **Penicillin allergy**
	- Doxycycline

- **Neurosyphilis**
	- IV penicillin required

- **Jarisch–Herxheimer reaction**
	- Flu-like illness after treatment
	- Self-limiting

- **Follow-up**
	- Repeat RPR/VDRL
		- 3 months
		- 6 months
		- 12 months if needed
	- Four-fold titre drop expected
	- Treponemal tests remain positive lifelong

- **Counselling**
	- No sex for 7 days after treatment
	- Contact tracing required
	- Condom use
	- HIV PEP/PrEP discussion
