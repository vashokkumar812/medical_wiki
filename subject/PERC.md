1. Aged <50 years
2. Pulse <100 beats per minute
3. SaO2 ≥95%
4. No haemoptysis
5. No oestrogen use
6. No surgery or trauma requiring hospitalisation within four weeks
7. No prior venous thromboembolism
8. No [[unilateral leg swelling]]

## limitations
- not work for 
	- - active cancer, thrombophilia or a strong family history of thrombophilia
- transient [[Adult medicine and surgery/evaluation/cardiology/tachycardia]] or beta-blocker use that may mask [[Adult medicine and surgery/evaluation/cardiology/tachycardia]]
- leg amputations
- morbid obesity (leg swelling not easily determined)
- baseline hypoxaemia when oximetry reading <95% is longstanding.
