
# Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD)

- Definition
	- Neurodevelopmental disorder
	- Persistent deficits in:
		- Social communication
		- Social interaction
	- Restricted, repetitive behaviours/interests
	- Symptoms present in early childhood

- Epidemiology
	- Onset before 3 years
	- Male:female ≈ 5:1

- Risk Factors / Causes
	- Mostly idiopathic
	- Fragile X syndrome
	- Tuberous sclerosis
	- PKU
	- Congenital rubella
	- Encephalitis
	- Perinatal hypoxia

- Clinical Features

	- Social
		- Poor eye contact
		- Poor peer relationships
		- Impaired non-verbal communication
		- Reduced social reciprocity

	- Communication
		- Delayed speech
		- Absent speech
		- Echolalia
		- Odd language

	- Behaviour
		- Repetitive behaviours
		- Restricted interests
		- Ritualistic behaviour
		- Resistance to change

	- Associated Features
		- Intellectual disability (~75%)
		- Seizures
		- Self-injury
		- Abnormal EEG

- Initial Assessment
	- Developmental assessment
	- Hearing assessment ⭐
	- Cognitive assessment
	- Speech and language assessment

- Management
	- Early intensive behavioural intervention ⭐
	- Family education
	- Speech therapy
	- Occupational therapy
	- Special education
	- Antipsychotics (e.g. risperidone, aripiprazole)
		- Severe aggression
		- Self-injurious behaviour

- Prognosis
	- Better with
		- Normal IQ
		- Functional speech by early childhood
	- Poor with
		- Intellectual disability
		- Absent speech


# Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD)

- Definition
	- Persistent:
		- Inattention
		- Hyperactivity
		- Impulsivity
	- Symptoms ≥6 months
	- Onset before 12 years
	- Present in ≥2 settings

- Epidemiology
	- Children: ~5%
	- Adults: ~2.5%
	- Male predominance

- Risk Factors
	- Family history
	- Mood disorders
	- Anxiety disorders
	- Substance use disorders

- Clinical Features

	- Inattention
		- Easily distracted
		- Careless mistakes
		- Poor concentration
		- Forgetfulness

	- Hyperactivity
		- Fidgeting
		- Cannot sit still
		- Excessive talking

	- Impulsivity
		- Interrupts others
		- Cannot wait turn
		- Acts without thinking

- Associated Conditions
	- Learning disorder
	- ODD
	- Conduct disorder
	- Anxiety
	- Depression
	- Low self-esteem

- Initial Assessment
	- History from parents and teachers ⭐
	- Behaviour rating scales
	- IQ assessment if indicated
	- Exclude hearing/vision problems

- Management

	- Non-pharmacological
		- Parent training
		- Behaviour therapy
		- School support
		- Structured environment

	- Pharmacological
		- First-line ⭐
			- Methylphenidate
			- Dexamfetamine
		- Alternatives
			- Atomoxetine
			- Clonidine
			- Guanfacine

- Differential Diagnosis
	- Normal behaviour
	- ASD
	- Intellectual disability
	- Mood disorders

# Conduct Disorder (CD)

- Definition
	- Persistent pattern (>12 months; ≥1 criterion in last 6 months)
	- Violates rights of others or societal rules

- Epidemiology
	- ~4%
	- Boys > girls

- Clinical Features

	- Aggression
		- Bullying
		- Fighting
		- Cruelty
		- Sexual assault

	- Property destruction
		- Fire setting
		- Vandalism

	- Deceit/Theft
		- Lying
		- Stealing
		- Robbery

	- Serious rule violations
		- Truancy
		- Running away

- Associated Conditions
	- ADHD
	- Substance abuse
	- School failure

- Management
	- Family therapy
	- Behavioural programs
	- Structured activities
	- Treat comorbid ADHD

- Outcome
	- Risk of antisocial personality disorder ⭐

- Differential Diagnosis
	- ODD
	- ADHD

#  Oppositional Defiant Disorder (ODD)

- Definition
	- Angry/irritable mood
	- Argumentative/defiant behaviour
	- Vindictiveness
	- Duration ≥6 months

- Epidemiology
	- ~3%
	- Boys > girls before puberty

- Clinical Features
	- Frequent temper outbursts
	- Argues with adults
	- Refuses rules
	- Deliberately annoys others
	- Blames others
	- Easily annoyed
	- Vindictive

- Associated Conditions
	- ADHD
	- Learning disorders
	- Low self-esteem
	- Family conflict

- Management
	- Parent management training ⭐
	- Consistent discipline
	- Positive reinforcement
	- Family therapy

- Complication
	- May progress to conduct disorder

- Differential Diagnosis
	- Conduct disorder


# Childhood Enuresis

- Definition
	- Repeated involuntary urination
	- Age ≥5 years
	- Not explained by medical illness

- Risk Factors
	- Family history
	- Psychological stress
	- UTI

- Clinical Features
	- Nocturnal
	- Diurnal
	- Both

- Initial Assessment
	- History
	- Urinalysis ⭐
	- Exclude UTI
	- Assess constipation
	- Assess psychosocial stressors

- Management

	- First-line ⭐
		- Education
		- Fluid restriction before bed
		- Timed voiding
		- Enuresis alarm (bell-pad)

	- Pharmacological
		- Desmopressin
			- Short-term control
		- Imipramine
			- Rarely used
