# Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD)

- Definition
	- Persistent:
		- Inattention
		- Hyperactivity
		- Impulsivity
	- Symptoms ≥6 months
	- Onset before 12 years
	- Present in ≥2 settings

- Epidemiology
	- Children: ~5%
	- Adults: ~2.5%
	- Male predominance

- Risk Factors
	- Family history
	- Mood disorders
	- Anxiety disorders
	- Substance use disorders

- Clinical Features

	- Inattention
		- Easily distracted
		- Careless mistakes
		- Poor concentration
		- Forgetfulness

	- Hyperactivity
		- Fidgeting
		- Cannot sit still
		- Excessive talking

	- Impulsivity
		- Interrupts others
		- Cannot wait turn
		- Acts without thinking

- Associated Conditions
	- Learning disorder
	- ODD
	- Conduct disorder
	- Anxiety
	- Depression
	- Low self-esteem

- Initial Assessment
	- History from parents and teachers ⭐
	- Behaviour rating scales
	- IQ assessment if indicated
	- Exclude hearing/vision problems

- Management

	- Non-pharmacological
		- Parent training
		- Behaviour therapy
		- School support
		- Structured environment

	- Pharmacological
		- First-line ⭐
			- Methylphenidate
			- Dexamfetamine
		- Alternatives
			- Atomoxetine
			- Clonidine
			- Guanfacine

- Differential Diagnosis
	- Normal behaviour
	- ASD
	- Intellectual disability
	- Mood disorders

# Childhood Enuresis

- Definition
	- Repeated involuntary urination
	- Age ≥5 years
	- Not explained by medical illness

- Risk Factors
	- Family history
	- Psychological stress
	- UTI

- Clinical Features
	- Nocturnal
	- Diurnal
	- Both

- Initial Assessment
	- History
	- Urinalysis ⭐
	- Exclude UTI
	- Assess constipation
	- Assess psychosocial stressors

- Management

	- First-line ⭐
		- Education
		- Fluid restriction before bed
		- Timed voiding
		- Enuresis alarm (bell-pad)

	- Pharmacological
		- Desmopressin
			- Short-term control
		- Imipramine
			- Rarely used
