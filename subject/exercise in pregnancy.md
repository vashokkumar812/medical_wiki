- Recommendations
  - If no contraindications
    - Encourage regular aerobic exercise
    - Encourage regular strength exercise
  - Exercise is recommended in uncomplicated pregnancy

- Recommended Exercise Dose
  - Moderate intensity
    - 150–300 minutes/week
  - OR
  - Vigorous intensity
    - 75–150 minutes/week
  - Muscle-strengthening activities
    - ≥2 days/week

- FITT Principle

  - Frequency
    - Most days of the week
    - Previously sedentary women
      - Start 3–4 days/week

  - Intensity
    - Moderate intensity
      - Borg scale 12–14
      - Able to maintain conversation (talk test)
    - Vigorous intensity
      - Borg scale 15–16
      - Suitable for trained women

  - Time
    - ≥30 minutes/session
    - Usually ≤60 minutes/session

  - Type
    - Walking
    - Swimming
    - Stationary cycling
    - Low-impact aerobics
    - Resistance training

  - Strength Training
    - 2 sessions/week
    - 1–2 sets
    - 12–15 repetitions per set

- Activities to Avoid
  - Activities with high fall risk
  - Activities with abdominal trauma risk
  - Activities causing excessive heat exposure

  - Examples
    - Horse riding
    - Skiing
    - Contact sports

  - Avoid after first trimester
    - Supine exercise

  - Avoid
    - Heavy weight lifting
    - Valsalva manoeuvre (breath holding)
    - Jumping/high-impact exercises

- Physiological Changes Affecting Exercise
  - Increased heart rate
  - Increased metabolic rate
  - Ligament laxity
  - Altered centre of gravity
  - Increased joint loading
  - Enlarged uterus
    - Reduced venous return

- Exercise Implications
  - Avoid sudden postural changes
  - Avoid overheating
  - Maintain adequate hydration
  - Modify exercises for balance and stability

- Warning Signs to Stop Exercise
  - Chest pain
  - Shortness of breath
  - Dizziness
  - Syncope
  - Muscle weakness
  - Calf pain
  - Calf swelling
  - Vaginal bleeding
  - Leakage of amniotic fluid
  - Decreased fetal movements
  - Uterine contractions
