- Info

	- Risk factors
			- Breast cancer
			- Colon cancer
			- Uterine cancer
			- Ovarian cancer

		- Hereditary ovarian cancer syndromes
			- BRCA mutation
			- Lynch syndrome

		- Protective factors
			- Parity
			- Breastfeeding
				- ~50% risk reduction
			- Combined OCP use

		- Menopausal status

	- Complications

		- Cyst rupture

			- Clinical features
				- Lower abdominal pain

			- Investigations
				- Ultrasound
					- Free fluid in abdomen
					- Collapsed cyst

			- Management
				- Uncomplicated
					- Outpatient analgesia
					- Usually resolves within 72 hours

				- Complicated
					- Fluid resuscitation
					- Persistent pain/hemodynamic instability
						- Laparoscopy

		- Ovarian torsion

			- Definition
				- Partial/complete rotation of ovary on supporting ligament
				- Compromises blood supply

			- Associations
				- Usually benign ovarian lesions

			- Clinical features
				- Sudden lower abdominal pain
				- Nausea/vomiting
				- Palpable adnexal mass

			- Risk factor
				- Ovarian mass >5 cm

			- Diagnosis
				- Primarily clinical
				- Ultrasound not mandatory
				- Normal USG does not exclude torsion

			- Management
				- Laparoscopy with detorsion + ovarian conservation

				- Non-viable ovary
					- Oophorectomy

- Diagnosis

	- Clinical features

		- Endometriosis

		- Malignancy clues
			- Persistent abdominal distension
			- Change in appetite
			- Pelvic pain
			- Urinary frequency

	- Risk of malignancy index (RMI)

		- Formula
			- RMI = Ultrasound findings × menopausal status × CA125

		- Ultrasound findings
			- Multilocular cyst
			- Solid area
			- Metastases
			- Ascites
			- Bilateral lesions

		- Scoring
			- 0 points
				- No features
			- 1 point
				- One feature
			- 3 points
				- 2–5 features

		- Menopausal status score
			- Premenopausal
				- 1
			- Postmenopausal
				- 2

		- CA125
			- Actual value used

	- Investigations

		- Premenopausal women
			- Measure CA125 only if
				- Ultrasound suspicious for malignancy

- Management

	- Ovarian cyst >7 cm
		- Standard of care
			- Laparoscopic cystectomy

	- Aspiration
		- Not preferred
		- High recurrence risk
