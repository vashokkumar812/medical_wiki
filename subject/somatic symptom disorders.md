- Somatic symptom and related disorders
	- Characterised by
		- Physical symptoms causing distress or functional impairment
		- Symptoms cannot be fully explained by medical disease
		- Psychological factors play a major role

---

- Somatic symptom disorder

	- Definition
		- ≥1 distressing somatic symptom
		- Excessive thoughts, feelings or behaviours related to symptoms
		- Causes significant functional impairment

	- Epidemiology / Risk factors
		- Women > men
		- Usually begins before age 30
		- More common in low socioeconomic status
		- Possible genetic predisposition
		- Family associations
			- Male relatives
				- Antisocial personality disorder
			- Female relatives
				- Histrionic personality disorder

	- Clinical features
		- Multiple physical symptoms
			- Multiple organ systems
		- Long, complicated medical history
		- Frequent doctor shopping
		- Excessive health concerns
		- Functional impairment
		- Interpersonal and psychological problems
		- Common psychiatric comorbidities
			- Major depression
			- Generalised anxiety disorder
			- Phobias
			- Personality disorders
			- Substance use disorders

	- Initial assessment
		- Exclude organic disease
		- Screen for anxiety and depression
		- Identify psychosocial stressors

	- Initial investigations
		- Directed by symptoms only
		- Avoid repeated unnecessary investigations

	- Management
		- Single primary physician
		- Regular scheduled appointments
			- Brief monthly reviews
		- Avoid unnecessary investigations
		- Psychoeducation
		- Individual psychotherapy
			- Improve coping skills
			- Increase awareness of psychological contribution

	- Differential diagnosis
		- Multiple sclerosis
		- Myasthenia gravis
		- SLE
		- HIV/AIDS
		- Thyroid disease
		- Chronic infection
		- Major depression
		- Generalised anxiety disorder
		- Schizophrenia

	- AMC pearl
		- Multiple unexplained symptoms + repeated healthcare visits + excessive health anxiety

---

- Conversion disorder (Functional neurological symptom disorder)

	- Definition
		- ≥1 neurological symptom
		- Incompatible with recognised neurological disease
		- Usually follows psychological stress

	- Epidemiology / Risk factors
		- Young women
		- Low socioeconomic status
		- Rural populations
		- Low IQ
		- Military personnel
		- Associated personality disorders
			- Histrionic
			- Dependent
			- Passive-aggressive
			- Antisocial

	- Clinical features

		- Motor symptoms
			- Weakness
			- Paralysis
			- Abnormal gait
			- Tremor
			- Jerks
			- Tics

		- Sensory symptoms
			- Blindness
			- Anaesthesia
			- Paraesthesia

		- Other
			- Mutism
			- Pseudoseizures

		- Psychological concepts
			- Primary gain
				- Internal conflict kept unconscious
			- Secondary gain
				- External benefits from illness
			- La belle indifférence
				- Surprisingly unconcerned about disability
			- Identification
				- Symptoms modelled on significant others

	- Initial assessment
		- Exclude neurological disease
		- Identify recent psychological stressor

	- Initial investigations
		- Neurological examination
		- Appropriate neurological investigations
			- MRI/CT if indicated
			- EEG if seizures suspected

	- Management
		- Reassurance
		- Psychotherapy
		- Stress management
		- Regular follow-up
		- Avoid reinforcing symptoms

	- Differential diagnosis
		- Stroke
		- Brain tumour
		- Multiple sclerosis
		- Dementia
		- Optic neuritis
		- Schizophrenia
		- Depression
		- Anxiety disorders
		- Factitious disorder
		- Malingering

	- AMC pearl
		- Neurological symptoms inconsistent with examination following psychological stress

- Illness anxiety disorder

	- Definition
		- Persistent fear of serious illness
		- Minimal or absent physical symptoms
		- Persists despite reassurance
		- Duration ≥6 months

	- Epidemiology
		- Equal in men and women
		- Usually begins at 20–30 years

	- Clinical features
		- Persistent disease preoccupation
		- Frequent medical consultations
		- Reassurance ineffective
		- Belief is not delusional
		- Functional impairment

	- Initial assessment
		- Exclude medical illness
		- Assess anxiety

	- Initial investigations
		- Limited investigations based on symptoms
		- Avoid repeated testing

	- Management
		- Regular scheduled appointments
		- Same treating doctor
		- Psychotherapy
			- CBT preferred
		- Avoid repeated reassurance and investigations

	- AMC pearl
		- Minimal symptoms + overwhelming fear of disease

- Body dysmorphic disorder

	- Definition
		- Preoccupation with an imagined or minor physical defect
		- Causes significant distress or impairment

	- Epidemiology
		- Usually 15–20 years
		- Women > men
		- Associated with
			- Depression
			- Anxiety
			- OCD
		- Possible serotonergic dysfunction

	- Clinical features
		- Facial appearance most commonly affected
		- Excessive mirror checking
		- Camouflaging behaviour
		- Social avoidance
		- Housebound
		- Functional impairment

	- Initial assessment
		- Screen for depression
		- Assess suicide risk
		- Screen for OCD

	- Initial investigations
		- None unless clinically indicated

	- Management
		- CBT
		- SSRIs first-line medication
		- TCAs or MAOIs may be used

	- Differential diagnosis
		- OCD
		- Anorexia nervosa
		- Delusional disorder
		- Schizophrenia
		- Narcissistic personality disorder
		- Brain lesions

	- AMC pearl
		- Normal appearance but convinced of severe deformity

- Factitious disorder

	- Definition
		- Intentional production or falsification of symptoms
		- Motivation is to assume the sick role
		- No obvious external reward

	- Types
		- Imposed on self
		- Imposed on another
			- Munchausen syndrome by proxy

	- Epidemiology
		- Women > men
		- Healthcare workers
		- Previous childhood abuse or repeated hospitalisation

	- Clinical features
		- Numerous admissions
		- Requests investigations
		- Negative investigations
		- Angry when challenged
		- Threatens litigation

	- Initial assessment
		- Exclude genuine disease
		- Review previous records

	- Initial investigations
		- Directed by presentation

	- Management
		- Non-confrontational approach
		- One treating team
		- Psychiatric involvement
		- Manage rather than cure

	- Differential diagnosis
		- Somatic symptom disorder
		- Malingering
		- Schizophrenia
		- Substance use
		- Personality disorders

	- AMC pearl
		- **Intentional symptoms + no external gain = Factitious disorder**

---

- Malingering

	- Definition
		- Intentional production or exaggeration of symptoms
		- Motivated by obvious external gain
			- Money
			- Drugs
			- Avoid work
			- Avoid military service
			- Housing
			- Legal benefit
		- Not a psychiatric disorder

	- Epidemiology
		- Men > women
		- More common in
			- Military
			- Prison
			- Medicolegal settings

	- Clinical features
		- Exaggerated subjective symptoms
		- Inconsistent history
		- Focus on external reward
		- Poor treatment adherence
		- Requests admission or secondary benefits

	- Initial assessment
		- Look for inconsistencies
		- Determine external incentive

	- Initial investigations
		- Directed by presentation

	- Management
		- Avoid direct confrontation
		- Allow patient to save face
		- Maintain therapeutic relationship

	- Differential diagnosis
		- Factitious disorder
		- Somatic symptom disorder

	- AMC pearl
		- **Intentional symptoms + obvious external gain = Malingering**

- High-yield differentiation

	- Somatic symptom disorder
		- Symptoms are **not intentionally produced**
		- Multiple physical complaints

	- Illness anxiety disorder
		- Minimal symptoms
		- Fear of serious illness

	- Conversion disorder
		- Neurological deficits
		- Psychological stress precedes symptoms

	- Body dysmorphic disorder
		- Preoccupation with appearance

	- Factitious disorder
		- Intentional symptoms
		- Sick role is the goal

	- Malingering
		- Intentional symptoms
		- External reward is the goal
