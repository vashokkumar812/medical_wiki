## Overview
- Important **intracellular anion**  
- Distribution:  
  - 85% in bone (as hydroxyapatite crystals)  
  - 14% in soft tissues  
  - 1% in blood  
- Intracellular concentration ≈ 100 mmol/L  
- Plasma concentration: **0.8–1.3 mmol/L**  
- **Definition:** < 0.8 mmol/L  
  - Mild: 0.65–0.8 mmol/L  
  - Moderate: 0.32–0.65 mmol/L  
  - Severe: < 0.32 mmol/L → significant clinical effects  

---

## Regulation
- **PTH**: ↑ bone release (Ca²⁺ + phosphate) but ↑ renal excretion (proximal tubule inhibition)  
- **Vitamin D**: ↑ intestinal absorption (Ca²⁺ + phosphate, esp. in jejunum)  

---

## Functions
- ATP production  
- Nucleic acids  
- Phospholipids  
- Enzymatic co-factors  
- cAMP, cGMP  
- 2,3-DPG (affects O₂-Hb dissociation)  
- Glycolytic enzymes  
- Plasma buffer (acid-base balance)  
- Immune system integrity  
- Coagulation cascade  

---

## Causes
### 1. Intake
- Malnutrition  
- Phosphate binders  
- Vitamin D deficiency  
- Malabsorption  
- Total parenteral nutrition (TPN)  

### 2. Redistribution
- Refeeding syndrome  
- Insulin in **DKA** treatment  

### 3. Output
- **Urinary losses**: diuretics, osmotic diuresis, hyperparathyroidism, Fanconi’s syndrome  
- **Non-urinary losses**:  
  - GI: vomiting, diarrhoea, fistulae  
  - Other: burns, sepsis, bleeding, excessive sweating  

---

## Clinical Features

### History
- [[SOB]], ventilator dependence  
- [[weakness]], altered mental state  
- [[heart failure]], shock  

### Examination
- **CVS:** reversible dilated cardiomyopathy, ↑ inotrope requirement  
- **Resp:** respiratory failure, ventilator dependence, left-shifted O₂-Hb curve  
- **Neuro:** [[weakness]], paraesthesia, gait disturbance, confusion  
- **Haem:** haemolysis, impaired WCC function  
- **Endo:** bone demineralisation  
- **Muskulo:** rhabdomyolysis  

---

## Investigations
- Serum phosphate, Ca²⁺, K⁺, Mg²⁺  
- ECG  
- Identify underlying cause (renal, intake, redistribution, catabolic states)  

---

## Management
- Treat underlying cause  
- **Oral phosphate**: if 0.65–0.89 mmol/L  
- **IV phosphate** (if severe):  
  - KH₂PO₄: 10 mmol phosphate + 10 mmol K in 10 mL  
  - NaKH₂PO₄: 13.4 mmol phosphate + 21.4 mmol Na⁺ + 2.6 mmol K in 20 mL  
  - Administer **1 ampoule over 1 hour**  

⚠️ Monitor for:  
- Hyperphosphataemia  
- [[Hypocalcaemia]]  
- Hypotension  
- Tetany  
- ECG changes  

❌ Use cautiously in renal failure
