- Phobic Disorders

	- Definition
		- Irrational fear of specific objects or situations
		- Leads to avoidance
		- Fear is disproportionate to actual danger

	- Types

		- Specific Phobia
			- Fear of a specific object or situation
			- Common triggers
				- Animals (e.g. spiders, dogs)
				- Natural environment (e.g. storms)
				- Blood/injections/injury
				- Heights
				- Flying
				- Darkness

		- Social Anxiety Disorder (Social Phobia)
			- Fear of embarrassment or humiliation in social situations
			- Examples
				- Public speaking
				- Performing on stage
				- Eating in public
				- Using public toilets

	- Management

		- First-line
			- Cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT)
			- Systematic desensitisation (exposure therapy)

		- Additional therapy
			- Assertiveness training (social anxiety)

		- Pharmacotherapy
			- SSRIs (first-line for social anxiety)
			- Buspirone
			- Beta-blockers
				- Situational anxiety (e.g. stage fright)
