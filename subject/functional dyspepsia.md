
- diagnosis
	- diagnosis of exclusion
	-  Exclude with:
		- Blood tests
		- Upper endoscopy
		- Upper abdominal ultrasound
- Diagnostic criteria
	- Must include ≥1:
		- Bothersome postprandial fullness
		- Early satiation
		- Epigastric pain
		- Epigastric burning
	- And:
		- No structural disease explaining symptoms
			- Including normal upper endoscopy
- Duration criteria
	- Symptoms for past 3 months
	- Symptom onset ≥6 months before diagnosis
- Postprandial distress syndrome (PDS)
	- Diagnostic criteria
		- Must include ≥1:
			- Bothersome postprandial fullness
				- After ordinary-sized meals
				- Several times/week

			- Early satiation
				- Unable to finish regular meal
				- Several times/week

	- Duration criteria
		- Symptoms for past 3 months
		- Symptom onset ≥6 months before diagnosis

	- Supportive features
		- Upper abdominal bloating
		- Postprandial nausea
		- Excessive belching
		- Epigastric pain syndrome may coexist

- Epigastric pain syndrome (EPS)
	- Diagnostic criteria
		- Must include all:
			- Epigastric pain/burning
				- At least moderate severity
				- ≥1/week

			- Intermittent pain

			- Not generalised
			- Not localised to:
				- Other abdominal regions
				- Chest

			- Not relieved by:
				- Defaecation
				- Passage of flatus

			- Does not fulfil criteria for:
				- Gallbladder disorders
				- Sphincter of Oddi disorders

	- Duration criteria
		- Symptoms for past 3 months
		- Symptom onset ≥6 months before diagnosis

	- Supportive features
		- Burning quality possible
			- Without retrosternal component

		- Pain may:
			- Increase with meals
			- Improve with meals
			- Occur during fasting

		- Postprandial distress syndrome may coexist
