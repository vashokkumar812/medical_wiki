| BMI-for-age Percentile (Australia) | Classification |
|---|---|
| **<3rd** | Underweight |
| **3rd–<85th** | Healthy weight |
| **85th–<95th** | Overweight |
| **≥95th** | Obesity |

### Height-for-age
| Percentile | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| **<3rd** | Short stature |
| **3rd–97th** | Normal |
| **>97th** | Tall stature |

### Weight-for-age *(mainly <10 years)*
| Percentile | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| **<3rd** | Underweight |
| **3rd–97th** | Normal |
| **>97th** | High weight |

### Head circumference (<2 years)
| Percentile | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| **<3rd** | Microcephaly |
| **3rd–97th** | Normal |
| **>97th** | Macrocephaly |

### AMC pearl
- **<3rd percentile** → Abnormally low
- **3rd–97th percentile** → Normal growth range (height, weight, head circumference)
- **BMI-for-age**
	- **<3rd** → Underweight
	- **85th–<95th** → Overweight
	- **≥95th** → Obesity
