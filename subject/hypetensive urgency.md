## target 
- MAP
	- 25% in 24 hours 
- BP
	- SBP <160 mm Hg or DBP <100 mm Hg over 24 hours

## management
- if hypertension diagnosed for first time
	- Tablet nifedipine R 20 mg stat and bd
	- Tablet metoprolol 50 mg stat and od
	- Tablet losartan 50 mg stat and od
	- Tablet prazosin 1 mg stat and q4h
	- Tablet hydrochlorothiazide 25 mg stat and od
- Start one drug, check BP after 4 hours. Add another drug if BP is still high
- In patients with very high BP (>200/120 mm Hg) and confirmed by rechecking the BP, two antihypertensives may be initiated at the same time
