- autoimmune destruction of lacrimal + salivary glands
  - primary
    - isolated disease
  - secondary
    - associated with
      - rheumatoid arthritis (most common)
      - SLE
      - systemic sclerosis

- features
  - dry eyes
    - gritty sensation
    - burning
    - photophobia
  - dry mouth
    - dysphagia (dry food)
    - dental caries
    - oral candidiasis
  - bilateral painless parotid enlargement
  - fatigue
  - arthralgia
  - Raynaud phenomenon
  - dry skin/vagina
  - chronic dry cough

- investigations
  - anti-SSA (Ro)
    - most sensitive
  - anti-SSB (La)
    - more specific
  - ANA+, RF+
  - Schirmer test
    - ↓ tear production
  - labial salivary gland biopsy
    - focal lymphocytic sialadenitis

- management
  - artificial tears
  - lubricating eye ointment
  - saliva substitutes
  - sugar-free gum
  - good dental care
  - pilocarpine
  - hydroxychloroquine
    - systemic symptoms

- complications
  - corneal ulcer
  - dental caries
  - oral candidiasis
  - distal RTA
  - interstitial nephritis
  - B-cell non-Hodgkin lymphoma

- AMC pearls
  - dry eyes + dry mouth → Sjögren syndrome
  - anti-SSA = most sensitive
  - Schirmer = confirms dry eyes
  - persistent parotid enlargement → lymphoma until proven otherwise
