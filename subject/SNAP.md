## Motivational Interviewing (MI) – AMC High Yiel
-  Definition
	- Patient-centred, non-confrontational counselling
	- Goal → resolve ambivalence + increase motivation for behaviour change

- Used for:
	- Smoking cessation
	- Lifestyle modification
	- Chronic disease self-management
	- Medication adherence

## Behaviour Change Factors

Consider:
- Culture
- Environment
- Literacy
- Beliefs
- Motivation
- Addiction
- Coping style
- Mood

---

## Patient Readiness (Clinical Approach)

| Readiness | Approach                       |
| --------- | ------------------------------ |
| Not ready | Explore ambivalence            |
| Unsure    | Decision balance               |
| Ready     | Action plan                    |
| Changed   | Follow-up & relapse prevention |

## Health Literacy

### Definition
Ability to **acquire, understand and use health information**

Low health literacy associated with:
- **Low income**
- **Low education**
- **High-risk behaviours**

---

## Communication for Low Health Literacy

- **Use plain language**
- **Avoid medical jargon**
- **Prioritise key messages**
- **Use visual aids**
- **Encourage questions**
- **Arrange follow-up**

---

## Teach-Back Technique

Patient **explains information in their own words**

Purpose:
- Confirm **understanding**
- Improve **patient education**
