- Definition
	- Production of paraprotein
		- Monoclonal protein (M protein)
	- Produced by non-cancerous plasma cells
	- Premalignant plasma cell disorder

- Key Features
	- Usually asymptomatic
	- No clinical features of multiple myeloma
	- May have peripheral neuropathy

- Diagnostic Concept
	- Isolated monoclonal protein
	- No end-organ damage

- Associations
	- Associated with various disorders
	- May precede plasma cell malignancy

- Differentiation from Multiple Myeloma

	- MGUS
		- No CRAB features

	- Absence of:
		- Hypercalcaemia
		- Renal failure
		- Anaemia
		- Bone lesions

	- Multiple Myeloma
		- CRAB features present

- Investigations
	- Serum protein electrophoresis
			- Detects M protein
	- Immunofixation
			- Confirms monoclonal gammopathy
	- Urine studies
			- Assess for Bence Jones protein
	- Full blood count
	- Renal function tests
	- Calcium levels
	- Skeletal imaging if clinically indicated

- Management
	- No chemotherapy required
	- Observation only
	- Regular follow-up and monitoring

- Follow-Up
	- Monitor for progression to:
			- Multiple myeloma
			- Other plasma cell disorders

- Key Exam Points
	- MGUS is asymptomatic monoclonal gammopathy
	- No CRAB features
	- No end-organ damage
	- Managed with surveillance rather than treatment
