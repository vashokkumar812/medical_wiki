- Assessment
	- Poor feeding
		- <50% normal intake in previous 24 hours
	- Reduced urine output
		- <4 wet nappies/24 hours
- Examination
    - General appearance/behaviour
        - Most important indicator of serious illness
    - Fluids/hydration
        - Dry mucous membranes
        - Reduced skin turgor
        - Sunken fontanelle
        - Reduced urine output
        - Poor weight gain
    - Glucose abnormalities
        - Apnoea
        - Jitteriness
        - Altered conscious state
- Causes of unwell neonate/young infant

    - Infective bacterial

        - UTI/pyelonephritis
            - Fever
            - Vomiting
            - Poor feeding
        - Meningitis
            - Fever
            - Tachycardia
            - Tachypnoea, dyspnea
            - Irritability
            - Neck stiffness
            - Bulging fontanelle

        - Bone/joint infection
            - Reduced limb movement
            - Swelling

        - Skin infection
            - Erythema
            - Swelling
            - Tenderness
            - Skin lesions

    - Infective viral

        - Bronchiolitis
            - Tachypnoea
            - Increased work of breathing
            - Wheeze
            - Crackles

        - Neonatal HSV
            - Vesicles
                - Absent in 1/3
            - Seizures
            - Encephalopathy
            - May be afebrile

        - Influenza/respiratory viruses
            - Fever
            - Poor feeding
            - Lethargy
            - Nasal congestion

        - Enterovirus/parechovirus
            - Fever
            - Rash
            - Poor feeding
            - Irritability
            - Seizures
            - Persistent tachycardia
                - Myocardial involvement

    - Surgical

        - Malrotation with volvulus
            - Bilious vomiting
            - Abdominal distension

        - Pyloric stenosis
            - Progressive projectile non-bilious vomiting
            - Olive-shaped mass
            - Hypochloraemic hypokalaemic metabolic alkalosis

        - Incarcerated hernia
            - Irreducible inguinal swelling

        - Hirschsprung disease/meconium ileus
            - Abdominal distension
            - Absent/infrequent stool

        - NEC
            - Abdominal distension
            - Tenderness
            - Vomiting
            - Blood in stool
            - Abdominal wall erythema/discolouration

        - Intussusception
            - Intermittent severe abdominal pain
            - Vomiting
            - Pallor
            - Lethargy
            - Red currant jelly stool
                - Late sign

    - Cardiac

        - Congenital heart disease
            - Cyanosis
            - Murmur
            - Sweating during feeds
            - Cardiac failure
                - Tachypnoea
                - Hepatomegaly
                - Hypoperfusion
            - Poor/absent pulses

        - SVT/arrhythmias
            - Persistent tachycardia
            - Pallor
            - Poor feeding

    - Respiratory

        - Meconium aspiration
            - Meconium-stained liquor
            - Tachypnoea
            - Increased work of breathing
            - Cyanosis

        - TTN/RDS
            - Tachypnoea
            - Increased work of breathing
            - Cyanosis
            - Radiological findings

        - Pneumothorax
            - Tachypnoea
            - Hyperresonance
            - Reduced breath sounds

    - Endocrine/metabolic

        - Congenital adrenal hyperplasia
            - Ambiguous genitalia
            - Hypotension
            - Dehydration
            - Hyponatraemia
            - Hyperkalaemia
            - Hypoglycaemia

        - Hypoglycaemia

        - Inborn errors of metabolism
            - Reduced consciousness
            - Hypotonia
            - Seizures
            - Jaundice
            - Organomegaly
            - Dysmorphism
            - Hypoglycaemia
            - Metabolic acidosis

    - Non-accidental injury
        - Bruising
        - Unexplained injuries
        - Intracranial injury
        - Neglect

    - Other
        - Acute bilirubin encephalopathy
            - Jaundice
        - BRUE
        - Toxins
        - Electrolyte abnormalities
            - Example: diluted formula water ingestion

- Management

    - Any unwell neonate/young infant
        - Prompt assessment
        - Senior doctor discussion

- Investigations

    - Perform
        - BGL
        - Consider VBG
        - FBE
        - UEC
        - LFT
        - CRP
        - Blood culture
        - Urine MCS

    - Lumbar puncture
        - Neonates <28 days
            - Unless contraindicated
        - Consider in young infants

    - Additional investigations according to suspected cause
        - Ammonia
        - Viral swabs
            - HSV
        - ECG
        - Imaging
            - CXR
            - Abdominal imaging
            - Neuroimaging

- Treatment

    - Involve senior clinician early
    - Early referral as indicated
        - Paediatrics
        - Surgery
        - Subspecialty teams

    - Seriously unwell infants require

        - Early empiric antibiotics
            - IV
            - IM
            - Intraosseous

        - Aciclovir if indicated

        - Adequate analgesia/sedation

        - Careful fluid management
            - Fluid resuscitation if required
            - Maintenance fluids accounting for oral intake
            - Restrict fluids to 2/3 maintenance in
                - Meningitis
                - Respiratory infection
            - Careful fluid use in cardiac disease

        - Treat underlying cause

        - Suspected bowel obstruction
            - NG tube on free drainage

        - Suspected duct-dependent congenital heart disease
            - Early cardiology/ICU discussion
            - Consider IV prostaglandin
