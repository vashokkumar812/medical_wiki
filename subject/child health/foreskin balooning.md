- Clinical features
    - Usually no consequences

    - Possible minor symptoms
        - Urine trapping
        - Spotting in underpants
        - Balanitis

- Treatment
    - Treat only if problematic

    - Topical steroid cream
        - Apply to preputial ring
            - Tightest part of foreskin
        - Betamethasone 0.05%
            - TDS for 6–12 weeks
        - Longer treatment duration than phimosis

    - If no pathological phimosis
        - Do not perform circumcision

- Prognosis
    - Success rate
        - ~90%

    - Recurrence rate
        - Up to 17%
