# Local Anaesthetic Toxicity

## Key points
- Usually rapid onset → severe neurological + cardiovascular toxicity  
- Can be delayed after oral/topical exposure  
- Management largely supportive  
- IV lipid emulsion for severe cases  
- Methaemoglobinaemia:
  - Associated with benzocaine, lignocaine, prilocaine  
  - Not dose-related  
  - More common in children (incl. topical use)  
- Allergic reactions/anaphylaxis possible → treat per anaphylaxis protocol  
- 24h advice: Victorian Poisons Information Centre 13 11 26  

---

# Background

## Causes
- Therapeutic error  
- Inadvertent IV/intra-arterial injection  
- Excess oral/topical dose  
- Fatalities reported after ingestion (lignocaine)  

## Mechanism
- Sodium channel blockade (reversible)  
- Also:
  - Potassium channel blockade  
  - Calcium channel blockade  
  - NMDA/cholinergic effects  
  - Cellular metabolic interference  

## Severity depends on
- Total dose  
- Rate  
- Route/location  
- Tourniquet use  
- Local blood flow  
- Adrenaline presence  

## Pharmacokinetics
- Hepatic metabolism  
- Half-life ~2 h  
- Bupivacaine ≥5 h  
- Bupivacaine particularly cardiotoxic  

---

# Assessment

## Assess
- Intentional overdose  
- Significant accidental overdose  
- Inadvertent IV/arterial injection  
- Any symptomatic patient  
- Developmental age inconsistent with history  

---

## History
- Intentional vs accidental  
- Dose:
  - Stated/likely dose  
  - Calculate mg/kg  
  - Co-ingestants (e.g. paracetamol)  

---

## Maximum Recommended Doses (mg/kg)

| Agent | Max dose |
|--------|-----------|
| Bupivacaine | 1–2.5 |
| Lignocaine | 4–5 |
| Mepivacaine | 4–5 |
| Prilocaine | 5–7 |
| Ropivacaine | 2.5–3 |

> Toxicity can occur below these doses if IV/intra-arterial  

---

# Examination

## Early features
- Tinnitus  
- Visual difficulty  
- Dizziness  
- Anxiety/agitation/confusion  
- Drowsiness  
- Perioral/tongue numbness  
- Metallic taste  

## Severe toxicity

### CNS
- Seizures  
- Coma  

### Cardiovascular
- Bradycardia  
- Hypotension  
- Atrial/ventricular dysrhythmias  
- Conduction blocks  
- Cardiovascular collapse  
- Asystole  

### Respiratory
- Respiratory depression  
- Apnoea  

### Methaemoglobinaemia
- Blue mucosa  
- Progressive hypoxia  
- CNS/cardiovascular failure  

- Cardiac arrest may be first sign (massive IV overdose)  

- Check Medicalert bracelet  
- Look for underlying disease signs  

---

# Investigations
- UEC  
- ABG  
- Methaemoglobin level  
- Serial ECG  
  - Prolonged PR  
  - Prolonged QRS  
  - Large terminal R in aVR  

---

# Management

## Immediate
- Stop local anaesthetic  
- Call for help  
- ABC resuscitation  

---

## Supportive care

### Airway
- Early intubation if cardiotoxicity  
- Prevent hypoxia, hypercarbia, acidosis  

### Dysrhythmias
- Sodium bicarbonate 1–2 mmol/kg IV (max 100 mmol)  
- Repeat every 1–2 min until perfusing rhythm  
- Amiodarone acceptable  
- AVOID:
  - Calcium channel blockers  
  - Beta blockers  
  - Local anaesthetics  

### Seizures
- Benzodiazepines  

### Hypotension
- Normal saline 20 mL/kg  
- Inotropes if required  
- AVOID vasopressin  

- Recovery may take >1 hour  

---

# Decontamination
- Not indicated  

# Enhanced elimination
- Not useful  

---

# Antidotes

## Sodium bicarbonate
- For ventricular dysrhythmias  

## Methylene blue
- 1–2 mg/kg IV over 5 min  
- For methaemoglobinaemia  

## IV Lipid Emulsion (20%)

### Indication
- Severe cardiovascular toxicity  
- Cardiac arrest  

### Precautions
- Egg/soya/peanut hypersensitivity  

### Adverse effects
- Allergy/anaphylaxis  
- Lab interference  
- Rare:
  - Pancreatitis  
  - VTE  
  - Pulmonary hypertension  
  - Acute lung injury  
  - Haematuria  
  - Hypertriglyceridaemia  
  - ECMO obstruction  

### Dosing
- Bolus 1.5 mL/kg over 1 min  
- Infusion 0.25 mL/kg/min  
- After 5 min:
  - Up to 2 repeat boluses (≥5 min apart)  
  - Increase infusion to 0.5 mL/kg/min  
- Continue until stable  
- Max cumulative dose 12 mL/kg  

---

# Ongoing Care
- >6 mg/kg lignocaine (teething gels/topicals) → hospital assessment  
- Monitor in HDU/ICU  

---

# Consultation
- Admit all intentional overdoses  
- Contact Poisons 13 11 26  

---

# Transfer
- Any patient with toxicity
