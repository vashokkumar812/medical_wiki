- Definition  
	- Abnormal head posture due to neck muscle contraction/spasm 
- evaluation  
	- Simple acute torticollis / wry neck
		- prognosis
			- resolves within 7–10 days  
		- Precipitating factors  
			- Awkward sleeping position  
			- Recent flight  
			- Different sleeping arrangement  

	- Infective symptoms  
		- Fever  
		- Increased drooling  
		- Sore throat  
		- Dysphagia  

	- Neurological symptoms    
		- Strabismus  
		- Diplopia  
		- Photophobia  
		- Ataxia 
		- Headache

	- Drug history  
		- Metoclopramide  

- Important point  
	- Deep pathology may have no external signs  
- Investigations  
	- Cervical spine X-ray  
		- Indications  
			- Cervical spine tenderness  
			- Severe pain  
			- Persistent symptoms ≥1 week  
			- Risk factors for atlantoaxial instability  
		- High-risk conditions  
			- Down syndrome  
			- Morquio syndrome  
			- Larsen syndrome  
			- Marfan syndrome  
	- CT neck and/or brain  
		- Indications  
			- Abnormal cervical X-ray  
			- neurological symptoms  
			- Severe pain not relieved by analgesia/muscle relaxants  
			- Suspected 
				- bone anomaly  
				- retropharyngeal abscess  

	- Specialist consultation for imaging decisions  
		- General medicine  
		- Orthopaedics  
		- ENT  
		- Ophthalmology  
		- Neurology  

- Treatment  

	- General treatment  
		- Heat pack  
		- Massage  
		- Basic analgesia  

	- Muscle spasm  
		- Diazepam  
			- Useful for sternocleidomastoid spasm  
	- Management according to cause  
		- Stabilisation  
			- May be required  
		- Infectious cause  
			- Appropriate antibiotic therapy  
		- Retropharyngeal/parapharyngeal abscess  
			- ENT referral  
		- Atlantoaxial rotatory fixation  
			- Rest  
			- Soft collar  
		- Injury/congenital bony abnormality  
			- Orthopaedic referral  
		- Acute dystonic reaction  
			- Benztropine  

- Consider paediatric consultation when  
	- Trauma-related torticollis  
	- Suspected deep neck space infection  
	- Unknown cause  
	- Prolonged symptoms
