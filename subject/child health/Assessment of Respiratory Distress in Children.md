## ⚠️ Key Points
- Children with **respiratory distress** should have **minimal handling**  
- The **severity assessment** can mostly be done **without touching** the child  
  - Parents can be asked to **uncover the chest/abdomen**  
- **Careful observation** is essential — **oxygen saturations may be misleading**

---

## 🧩 Assessment of Severity

| **Parameter**                                                                              | **Mild**                            | **Moderate**                                                                            | **Severe**                                                                                         | **Life-Threatening**                             |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **Behaviour**                                                                              | Normal<br>Able to vocalise normally | Intermittent irritability<br>Reduced activity<br>Some limitation of ability to vocalise | Increasing irritability and/or lethargy<br>Marked limitation of ability to vocalise (single words) | Drowsy or unconscious<br>Unable to vocalise      |
| **Colour**                                                                                 | Normal                              | Pallor                                                                                  | Pallor or cyanosis                                                                                 | Cyanosis                                         |
| **Respiratory Rate**<br>(At rest and not crying)                                           | Normal or mildly increased          | Increased                                                                               | Increased                                                                                          | Markedly increased or may have bradypnoea/apnoea |
| **Increased Work of Breathing***                                                           | None or minimal                     | Moderate                                                                                | Marked                                                                                             | Severe accessory muscle use or poor effort       |
| **Oxygenation**<br>(SpO₂ only of limited utility; look at the child, not just the monitor) | —                                   | —                                                                                       | **SpO₂ < 90% (room air)**                                                                          | Any O₂ requirement in **croup** = severe         |
| **Heart Rate**                                                                             | Normal or slightly increased        | Mildly increased                                                                        | Significantly increased or bradycardia                                                             | Arrhythmia or bradycardia                        |
| **Blood Pressure**<br>(Do not measure in croup)                                            | Normal                              | Increased                                                                               | Increased                                                                                          | Hypotension                                      |

---

### *Signs of Increased Work of Breathing*
- Retractions (intercostal, suprasternal, costal margin)  
- Accessory muscle use: nasal flaring, sternocleidomastoid contraction (**head bobbing**), forward posturing  
- Grunting

## 🧠 Other Considerations
- **Fever** may increase respiratory rate and heart rate  
- **Respiratory sounds** help identify the cause:
  - **Wheeze** → Asthma, Bronchiolitis  
  - **Stridor** → Croup, Acute upper airway obstruction  
  - **Reduced air entry** → important sign of severity  
- Assess **feeding and hydration** — early indicators of deterioration  
- **Effortless tachypnoea** may indicate **metabolic acidosis** (e.g. sepsis, DKA)

---

## 🩺 Management
- Refer to **specific condition guidelines** for targeted management (e.g. Asthma, Croup, Bronchiolitis)
