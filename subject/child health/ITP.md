- immune thrombocytopenia
	- definition
		- autoimmune bleeding disorder

	- diagnostic triad
		- isolated thrombocytopenia
			- platelet count <100 × 10⁹/L
		- well child without concerning clinical features
		- otherwise normal FBE and blood film

	- epidemiology
		- most common cause of symptomatic acquired thrombocytopenia in children
		- relatively uncommon overall

	- diagnosis
		- diagnosis of exclusion
		- no specific confirmatory laboratory test

	- important concepts
		- alternative causes of petechiae/purpura must be excluded
		- treatment based on bleeding severity
			- not platelet count
		- majority do not require treatment
		- intracranial haemorrhage risk very low
			- <1%

- classification
	- newly diagnosed ITP
		- commonest in children 2–6 years
		- often follows viral infection
		- sudden symptom onset
		- usually self-limiting
		- spontaneous recovery within 3–6 months

	- persistent ITP
		- thrombocytopenia lasting 3–12 months

	- chronic ITP
		- thrombocytopenia >12 months
		- more common in adolescents
			- 20–30%

- bleeding severity assessment
	- modified Buchanan bleeding score

	- grade 0
		- no bleeding
		- no new haemorrhage

	- grade 1
		- minor bleeding

		- features
			- ≤100 petechiae
			- ≤5 small bruises
				- ≤3 cm
			- no mucosal bleeding

	- grade 2
		- mild bleeding

		- features
			- >100 petechiae
			- >5 bruises
				- >3 cm

	- grade 3a
		- moderate low-risk bleeding

		- features
			- blood in nares
			- painless oral purpura
			- oral/palatal petechiae
			- buccal purpura along molars
			- epistaxis ≤5 min

	- grade 3b
		- moderate high-risk bleeding

		- features
			- epistaxis >5 min
			- haematuria
			- haematochezia
			- painful oral purpura
			- significant menorrhagia

	- grade 4
		- severe bleeding

		- features
			- mucosal bleeding requiring urgent intervention
			- suspected internal haemorrhage
				- brain
				- lung
				- joint
				- muscle

	- grade 5
		- life-threatening bleeding

		- features
			- documented intracranial haemorrhage
			- life-threatening bleeding at any site

	- initial investigations

		- must be reviewed

		- guided by differential diagnosis
		- guided by complications

- management
	- principles
		- treatment based on clinical features
			- not platelet count
		- consider
			- active bleeding
			- future bleeding risk
				- surgery
			- psychosocial factors

		- treatment goal
			- stop active bleeding
			- not normalise platelet count

	- low-risk disease
		- Buchanan score
			- 0–3a

		- features
			- petechiae
			- bruising
			- painless oral/palatal purpura
			- blood crusting in nares

		- management
			- outpatient management
			- usually no medical treatment
			- repeat FBE in 1 week
			- review in 1 week
			- family education

		- exceptions
			- psychosocial concerns
			- safety concerns

	- moderate disease
		- Buchanan score
			- 3b

		- features
			- epistaxis >5 min
			- haematuria
			- haematochezia
			- painful oral purpura
			- significant menorrhagia

		- management
			- often hospital admission

			- before treatment
				- blood film reviewed/discussed with haematologist

			- treatment goal
				- raise platelet count enough to stop bleeding

			- first-line treatment
				- oral prednisolone
					- 2–4 mg/kg
					- max 100 mg
					- 4–7 days

			- second-line treatment
				- IVIg
					- 0.8–1 g/kg

				- indications
					- poor response
					- rapid platelet rise required
						- surgery

			- adjunctive therapy
				- epistaxis
					- oral tranexamic acid
						- 25 mg/kg
						- max 1.5 g
					- ENT intervention may be required

				- heavy menstrual bleeding
					- tranexamic acid may help

			- contraindication
				- tranexamic acid contraindicated in haematuria

	- severe disease
		- Buchanan score
			- 4

		- features
			- suspected internal haemorrhage
				- brain
				- lung
				- muscle
				- joint
			- severe mucosal bleeding

		- management
			- urgent haematology consultation

			- combination therapy
				- IVIg
					- 0.8–1 g/kg
				- pulse IV methylprednisolone
					- 30 mg/kg/day
					- max 1 g/day
					- for 3 days

			- platelet transfusion
				- 20 mL/kg
				- continuous transfusion if required

			- tranexamic acid
				- IV
				- 15 mg/kg
				- max 1–1.5 g

			- life-threatening bleeding
				- thrombopoietin receptor agonists
					- romiplostim
					- eltrombopag

			- urgent surgery/referral
				- depends on bleeding site

	- life-threatening disease
		- Buchanan score
			- 5

		- features
			- documented intracranial haemorrhage
			- life-threatening bleeding
