- key points  
  - prolonged or delayed onset  
    - children require observation  
  - serum concentrations  
    - correlate with clinical effects  
    - useful in large ingestions  
  - decontamination  
    - consider in large ingestions  
    - consult toxicologist  
  - poisons advice  
    - 13 11 26  

- background  
  - onset  
    - immediate-release  
      - 1–2 hours  
    - sustained-release  
      - 4–8 hours  
  - dose-dependent toxicity  
  - monitoring  
    - drug concentrations useful  
  - clinical features  
    - drowsiness  
    - nausea  
    - vomiting  
    - anticholinergic effects  
    - seizures  
    - coma  
    - life-threatening arrhythmias  
  - large ingestion  
    - prolonged/delayed symptoms (>48 hours)  
    - mechanism  
      - slow erratic absorption  
      - anticholinergic ileus  
      - ongoing absorption  

- children requiring assessment  
  - deliberate self-poisoning  
  - significant accidental ingestion  
    - >20 mg/kg  
    - or >20 mg/kg above usual daily dose  
  - any symptomatic child  
  - unknown ingestion  
  - concern for non-accidental poisoning  
    - developmental inconsistency  

- risk assessment  

  - history  
    - intent  
      - intentional  
      - accidental  
    - dose  
      - stated/likely  
      - formulation  
        - syrup  
        - immediate-release  
        - modified-release  
      - identify  
        - drug name  
        - tablet size  
      - calculate  
        - maximum mg/kg dose  
      - toxicity threshold  
        - ≥50 mg/kg  
        - or >3 g  
    - co-ingestants  
      - e.g. paracetamol  

  - examination  
    - CNS  
      - ataxia  
      - nystagmus  
      - drowsiness  
      - coma  
      - seizures  
    - cardiovascular  
      - tachycardia  
      - hypotension  
      - arrhythmias  
        - heart block  
        - widened QRS  
        - ventricular fibrillation  
    - anticholinergic  
      - urinary retention  
      - ↓ bowel motility  
      - dry mouth  
      - sinus tachycardia  
    - always check  
      - medicalert bracelet  
      - signs of underlying disease  
        - fingerprick marks  

- investigations  
  - ECG  
    - initial  
    - repeat at 6 hours  
    - until normal  
  - paracetamol level  
    - all intentional overdoses  
  - consider co-ingestants  
    - accidental or deliberate  
- - acute management  

- children requiring treatment  
  - all symptomatic children  
  - acute ingestion  
    - unknown quantity  
  - based on ingestion  
    - not on carbamazepine  
      - >20 mg/kg  
    - on regular carbamazepine  
      - >20 mg/kg above usual daily dose  

- resuscitation  
  - supportive care  
    - maintain blood pressure  
    - maintain electrolyte balance  
  - IV fluids  
    - 20 mL/kg  
    - for hypovolaemia or hypotension  

- decontamination  
  - discuss with toxicologist  
    - poisons information centre  
      - 13 11 26  
  - activated charcoal  
    - consider if  
      - massive ingestion (>50 mg/kg)  
      - within 24 hours  
      - large controlled-release ingestion  
  - CNS toxicity  
    - only give charcoal  
      - after airway secured  
  - multidose charcoal  
    - consider in large ingestion  
    - only if bowel sounds present  
    - discuss toxicologist  

- ongoing care and monitoring  

  - mild symptoms  
    - e.g. ataxia  
    - e.g. blurred vision  
    - observe  
      - minimum 8 hours  
      - longer if controlled-release  
    - discharge  
      - once symptom-free  

  - moderate–severe or persistent symptoms (>8 hours)  
    - e.g. ↓ conscious state  
    - e.g. arrhythmias  
    - admit  
      - observation  
      - supportive care  
    - discuss toxicologist  
      - drug concentration monitoring  
    - discuss PICU  
      - if severe  

- seizure management  
  - benzodiazepines  
    - first-line  
  - avoid  
    - sodium channel blockers  
      - e.g. phenytoin  

- consultation (paediatric team)  
  - all intentional overdose  
  - persistent symptoms after 8 hours  
  - contact poisons centre  
    - 13 11 26  

- transfer indications  
  - severe symptoms  
  - potential need for ICU  
  - care beyond local capacity  

- discharge criteria  
  - normal GCS  
  - normal ECG  
  - symptom-free after 8 hours  

- psychosocial assessment  
  - assess risk  
    - further ingestion  
    - self-harm  
  - assess  
    - other drug/alcohol use  
  - if safe for discharge  
    - link to services  
      - mental health  
      - drug and alcohol  

| Carbamazepine concentration (mg/L) | Micromol/L | Effect |
|-----------------------------------|-----------|--------|
| 5–12 | 20–50 | Therapeutic range |
| 10–20 | 40–85 | Nystagmus, sedation, ataxia |
| 20–40 | 85–170 | Horizontal and vertical nystagmus, coma |
| >40 | >170 | Respiratory depression, seizures, cardiac arrhythmia |

*Children are at risk of severe features at lower concentrations compared to adults*
