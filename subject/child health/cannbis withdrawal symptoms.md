- key points  
  - most young people  
    - do not require medication  
    - use coping strategies  
    - information  
    - reassurance  
  - nicotine co-dependence  
    - common  
    - treat nicotine withdrawal concurrently  
  - withdrawal severity  
    - ↑ with psychiatric comorbidities  
    - ↑ with psychosocial vulnerabilities  
  - may not be presenting complaint  
    - can occur during any admission  
  - plan ongoing care  
    - link with post-withdrawal services  

- background  
  - cannabis use  
    - common in young people  
  - cannabis withdrawal syndrome (CWS)  
    - occurs in heavy users  
      - daily/almost daily use  
      - over months  
    - triggered by  
      - abrupt cessation  
      - significant reduction  
  - dependence  
    - ~1 in 6 users  
    - features  
      - tolerance  
      - difficulty cutting down  
      - withdrawal symptoms  
  - timeline  
    - onset  
      - 1–2 days after last use  
    - peak  
      - day 2–6  
    - resolution  
      - 2–3 weeks  
    - persistent symptoms  
      - e.g. sleep disturbance (months)  
  - presentation  
    - planned  
      - elective withdrawal  
    - unplanned  
      - during unrelated admission  
  - co-dependence  
    - nicotine common  
      - mixing cannabis with tobacco  
    - dual withdrawal  
      - more severe symptoms  
  - cannabis hyperemesis syndrome  
    - recurrent severe nausea  
    - due to use  
      - not withdrawal  
    - management  
      - IV fluids  
      - antiemetics  
      - dopamine antagonists  
      - topical capsaicin  

- assessment  

  - history  

    - common withdrawal features  
      - irritability  
      - insomnia  
      - anxiety  
      - low mood  
      - aggression  
      - decreased appetite  
      - nausea  
      - headache  

    - less common features  
      - chills  
      - sweating  
      - tremor  
      - abdominal pain  

    - pattern of use  
      - frequency  
      - quantity  
      - method  
        - joint  
        - bong  
        - e-cigarette  
        - cookies  
        - gummies  
      - last use timing  

    - high risk  
      - daily/almost daily use  
      - several months  

    - polysubstance use  
      - nicotine  
      - alcohol  
      - stimulants  
        - ice  
        - cocaine  
        - MDMA  
      - benzodiazepines  
      - opioids  
        - heroin  
        - prescription opioids  
      - inhalants  
        - nitrous oxide  
        - chroming  
      - hallucinogens  
        - LSD  

    - previous withdrawal  
      - symptoms  
      - severity  

    - physical health issues  

    - mental health issues  
      - including psychosis  

    - pregnancy  

    - psychosocial screen  
      - HEEADSSS  

    - factors for severe withdrawal  
      - previous complex withdrawal  
        - aggression  
        - suicidal ideation/attempt  
        - psychosis  
      - polysubstance dependence  
      - co-occurring disorders  
        - mental health  
        - medical  
          - e.g. T1DM, IBD  

  - examination  
    - vital signs  
    - physical exam  
      - signs of intoxication/withdrawal  
    - mental state examination  

  - assessment tool  
    - cannabis withdrawal assessment scale  
      - assists severity assessment  
      - not validated for medication decisions  

- management  

  - general  
    - collaborative approach  
    - empower young person  
      - information  
      - self-efficacy  

  - preventive care  
    - opportunistic vaccination  
      - hepatitis B  
      - HPV  
      - consider hepatitis A  

- investigations  
  - opportunistic screening  
    - sexual health  
    - pregnancy  
    - blood-borne viruses  
  - urine drug screen  
    - not routine  
    - cannabinoids persist  
      - weeks after cessation  

- treatment  
  - primarily supportive  
    - non-pharmacological  
  - no standard medication regimen  
  - use short-term PRN medications  
    - symptom-based  

- ongoing care  
  - link to voluntary services  
    - improves outcomes  
  - increase awareness  
    - available support services  

- post-withdrawal services  
  - drug and alcohol services  
    - youth  
  - family drug support services  
  - peer-support groups  
    - SMART recovery  
  - mental health services  
  - general practitioners  
  - accommodation services  
  - child protection agencies  
  - legal services  

- consultation (paediatric team)  
  - severe or complex withdrawal  
    - polysubstance use  
    - mental ill-health  
    - physical comorbidities  
    - neurodevelopmental comorbidities  
    - psychosocial comorbidities  
  - assistance with treatment setting  
    - outpatient  
    - inpatient  
    - drug and alcohol unit  
    - mental health unit  
  - assistance with medication titration  
    - sedating medications  
  - safety concerns  
    - due to substance use  

- transfer indications  
  - requires tertiary mental health care  
  - care beyond local service capacity
