- key points  
  - immediate resuscitation  
    - if hypoxia and/or shock  
    - prevent cardiorespiratory arrest  
  - effective resuscitation  
    - cohesive team  
    - knowledge + skills  
    - good communication  
    - teamwork  
  - early referral  
    - specialised paediatric / retrieval service  

- background  
  - applies to  
    - seriously unwell children  
  - does not include  
    - trauma  
    - seriously injured children  
    - neonates  
    - cardiorespiratory arrest  

- assessment and management  

  - call for help early  
    - within hospital  
    - paediatric retrieval service  

  - resuscitation approach  
    - reactive  
    - assessment + management in parallel  

  - role allocation  
    - team leader  
    - airway  
      - nurse  
      - doctor  
    - circulation  
      - nurse  
      - doctor  
    - primary survey doctor  
    - scribe + timekeeper  
      - stopwatch  
    - family support staff  

- important considerations  
  - cardiac dysfunction  
  - pericardial effusion  
    - consider echocardiogram  
  - coagulopathy  
  - non-accidental injury  

- post resuscitation care  
  - early specialist advice  
  - review history  
  - thorough examination  
  - regular reassessment  
  - maintain  
    - oxygenation  
    - ventilation  
  - targets  
    - normal systolic BP  
    - urine output  
    - glucose  
    - electrolytes  
    - temperature  
  - monitor  
    - end organ damage  
  - consider  
    - analgesia  
    - sedation  
  - family communication  
    - regular counselling  
    - offer presence during resuscitation  
    - assign support person  

- staff support  
  - resuscitation is stressful  
  - offer debriefing  
    - for all staff
