- Lymphoedema

- Definition
	- Chronic swelling of one or more body regions
	- Due to accumulation of protein-rich fluid in tissue spaces

- Pathogenesis
	- Occurs when lymphatic drainage demand exceeds lymphatic transport capacity

- Types

	- Primary lymphoedema
		- Developmental abnormality of lymphatic system
		- No triggering factor identified
		- Can present in
			- Childhood
			- Adolescence
			- Middle age

	- Secondary lymphoedema
		- Due to lymphatic vessel/node damage
		- May occur months to years after insult

- Causes of secondary lymphoedema
	- Surgery
	- Cancer-related treatment
	- Venous stripping
	- Orthopaedic surgery
	- Skin grafting
	- [[DVT]]
	- Chronic venous disease
	- Radiotherapy
	- Trauma/burns
	- Infection
		- Cellulitis
		- Filariasis
	- Prolonged dependent oedema
	- Immobility
	- Chronic neurological disease
	- Obesity-related
	- May coexist with lipoedema

- Risk factors
	- Surgery involving lymphatic system/nodes
	- Radiotherapy to lymphatic regions
	- Cellulitis/history of infection
	- Trauma to lymphatic system
	- Immobility
			- Post-[[CVA]]
			- Wheelchair-bound
	- Obesity
	- Chronic venous insufficiency
	- Family history

- Clinical features

	- Early warning signs
		- Transient swelling
		- Heaviness in affected region
		- Pain/tension
		- Tightness/bursting sensation
		- Tight clothing/jewellery

	- Important point
		- Early signs may precede swelling by >3 years
		- Early intervention reduces severity/risk

	- Bioimpedance
		- Detects early lymphatic changes
		- Useful in at-risk patients
		- Especially if baseline available

- Physical evaluation

	- Subcutaneous tissue assessment
		- Pitting vs non-pitting oedema
		- Tissue tone

	- Stemmer sign
		- Inability to pinch skin at base of 2nd toe
		- Suggestive of lymphoedema

	- Skin assessment
		- Dryness/cracking
		- Infection
		- Bruising
		- Interdigital tinea
		- Nail disease
			- Paronychia
			- Ingrown nails

	- Swelling assessment
		- Limb circumference measurement
		- Compare with
			- Opposite limb
			- Preoperative baseline

	- Anthropometrics
		- Weight
		- Height
		- BMI
		- Waist:hip ratio
		- Waist:height ratio

	- System review
		- Cardiac assessment
		- Respiratory assessment
		- Abdominal/pelvic masses
		- Lymphadenopathy
		- Arterial circulation
			- ABPI

- Investigations
	- Duplex ultrasound
		- Exclude venous insufficiency/DVT

	- Blood tests
		- If indicated

	- CT/ultrasound
		- Exclude masses/tumours

	- Chest X-ray ± echocardiogram
		- Exclude cardiac/respiratory causes

	- Bioimpedance
		- Especially useful with baseline readings

	- Lymphoscintigraphy
		- If diagnosis unclear
		- After excluding other causes

	- MR lymphangiography
		- If available

	- ICG lymphography/fluoroscopy
		- Limited availability

- General management principles
	- Reduce symptoms
	- Improve quality of life
	- Minimise cellulitis risk
	- Address emotional/practical burden
	- Encourage regular exercise

- Comprehensive lymphoedema management

	- Education
		- Limb care
		- Skin care

	- Exercise
		- Promotes lymphatic flow

	- Compression therapy
		- Individually prescribed
		- Fitted by qualified practitioner
		- Options
			- Compression bandage
			- Garment
			- Wrap
			- Pump
		- Not required for all patients

	- Manual lymphatic drainage (MLD)

	- Intermittent pneumatic compression pump

	- Other therapies
		- Photobiomodulation
			- Low-level laser therapy
		- Kinesiotaping
		- Negative pressure therapy

- Precautions
	- Diuretics
		- Ineffective in isolated lymphoedema
		- May still be needed in [[heart failure]]

	- Drugs that may worsen lymphoedema
		- Calcium channel blockers
		- Steroids
		- Pregabalin
		- Anti-inflammatory agents

	- Sudden worsening
		- Exclude
			- Tumour recurrence
			- [[DVT]]

- Skin care
	- Daily soap-free washing
	- Daily moisturiser
	- Avoid sunburn
	- Avoid tight jewellery/clothing

- Foot care
	- Check for tinea/infection
	- Keep feet clean/dry
	- Treat injuries promptly
	- Consider podiatry referral

- Exercise and movement
	- Cornerstone of treatment
	- Include
		- Flexibility exercises
		- Resistance training
		- Aerobic exercise

- Weight control
	- Essential
	- Excess weight impairs lymphatic function

- Travel advice
	- Consider prophylactic antibiotics
	- Consult lymphoedema practitioner
			- Compression advice
			- Travel advice

- Heat precautions
	- Avoid hottest time of day
	- Avoid saunas/hot water
	- Cooling rebate schemes may help

- Clinical procedure precautions
	- Prefer unaffected limb for
			- Injections
			- IV cannulas
			- BP measurement
	- Avoid immunisations in affected limb
	- Take care with
			- Skin excisions
			- Liquid nitrogen

- Surgical treatments
	- Liposuction
	- Lymphovenous anastomosis
	- Lymph node transfer
	- Conservative management required before referral

- Accessing treatment

	- Lymphoedema practitioners
		- Best practice referral
		- ALA-endorsed training
		- Public/private services available

	- Subsidies
		- Chronic Disease Management Plan
			- Up to 5 subsidised sessions

	- Compression garment subsidy schemes
		- State dependent

	- Energy cooling rebate schemes
		- May assist with cooling costs
