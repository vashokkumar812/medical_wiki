# cervical carcinoma
- Cervical screening

	- Routine screening
		- Age <25 years
			- Not recommended
		- Age 25–74 years
			- HPV test every **5 years**
			- Self-collected vaginal swab or clinician-collected sample
		- Age 70–74 years
			- If HPV test negative
				- Cease routine screening
		- Age ≥75 years
			- No previous screening or >5 years since last screen
				- May request screening
				- Self-collected or clinician-collected sample

	- Special consideration (<25 years)
		- Routine screening not recommended
		- Consider a **single HPV test (age 20–24 years)** only if
			- First sexual activity <14 years
			- Not HPV vaccinated before sexual debut
	- - Cervical screening — 2025 AMC high-yield updates

	- Routine screening
		- Age 25–74
			- HPV test every 5 years
		- Self-collected vaginal swab acceptable
		- Clinician-collected sample acceptable

	- HPV detected on self-collected sample
		- HPV 16/18
			- Refer for colposcopy
		- HPV (not 16/18)
			- Reflex LBC if clinician-collected sample available
			- Otherwise clinician review
		- Does not return for follow-up (≥9 months)
			- Repeat self-collected HPV test acceptable
			- Co-test no longer required

	- Test of Cure (ToC) after treated HSIL
		- Annual HPV test
		- Continue until
			- Two consecutive HPV-negative tests
		- Then
			- Return to routine 5-yearly screening
		- Applies retrospectively
			- Patients already meeting criteria automatically exit ToC

	- HPV 16/18 + normal evaluation
		- HPV 16/18 detected
		- LBC negative
		- Normal colposcopy
			- Repeat HPV in 12 months
		- If again
			- HPV 16/18 positive
			- LBC negative
			- May repeat HPV again in 12 months
			- Immediate repeat colposcopy not always required

	- Colposcopy updates
		- Endocervical curettage
			- Consider in selected patients
			- Only if trained and appropriate equipment available
		- Do not perform diagnostic excision of transformation zone
			- Without high-grade cytology or histology
		- LEEP
			- Preferred over cone biopsy when appropriate
			- Similar efficacy
			- Fewer postoperative complications

	- After total hysterectomy
		- No previous HSIL/AIS
			- OR completed HSIL Test of Cure
				- No further cervical screening
		- LSIL/HSIL in hysterectomy specimen
			- Annual HPV testing
			- Until two consecutive HPV-negative tests
		- Previous biopsy-proven AIS
			- Annual co-test
			- Until two consecutive negative co-tests

	- After completely excised AIS
		- Annual co-test
		- After 5 years of negative annual co-tests
			- Co-test every 3 years
		- Continue surveillance for 25 years
		- Then
			- Age <70
				- Return to routine screening
			- Age ≥70
				- Exit screening if
					- At least one co-test ≥70 years
					- HPV negative
					- Cytology negative

	- AMC pearls
		- Routine screening = HPV only every 5 years
		- Self-collection now widely accepted
		- Treated HSIL → HPV-only Test of Cure (not co-test)
		- Two consecutive negative HPV tests → Return to routine screening
		- Previous AIS requires much longer surveillance than HSIL
