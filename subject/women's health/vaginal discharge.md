	- Chronic inflammatory dermatosis
	- Likely autoimmune

	- Any age
	- Bimodal peak

	- Mean age

	- Diagnosis delay common

	- Likely autoimmune

# vulva and vagina bleeding
- Key Points

	- Usually resolves with simple measures

	- In pre-pubertal children with nocturnal vaginal or perineal pain or itch
		- Consider pinworms

	- Recurrent UTI symptoms with negative cultures
		- Assess for vulvodynia

	- Do not perform internal vaginal examinations or internal vaginal swabs in prepubertal children





- Newborn findings due to maternal oestrogen effect
	- Breast buds
	- White mucoid discharge
	- Small vaginal bleed

			- Minimal vaginal discharge is normal
	- Hymenal tags

		- Rare between 2 years and puberty
