- LAM (Lactational Amenorrhoea Method)
	- Effective only if ALL are present
		- Baby <6 months old
		- Exclusive or nearly exclusive breastfeeding
		- Amenorrhoea
	- If any criterion absent
		- Start another contraceptive method

- AMC "Never Choose" List
	- Migraine with aura → Never CHC
	- Previous DVT/PE → Never CHC
	- Current breast cancer → Never hormonal contraception
	- Smoker ≥35 years → Avoid CHC
	- Severe hypertension → Never CHC
	- Stroke (CVA) or IHD → Never CHC
	- Heavy menstrual bleeding → Avoid Cu-IUD
	- Dysmenorrhoea → Avoid Cu-IUD
	- Enzyme-inducing AEDs → Do not rely on COCP, POP or ENG Implant
	- High STI risk → Never rely on hormonal contraception alone


- Missed Combined Oral Contraceptive Pill (COCP)

	- Missed pill
		- <48 hours since a pill should have been taken
			- Take the missed pill immediately
			- Continue remaining pills as usual
			- Even if two pills taken on same day
			- No emergency contraception (EC) required
			- No backup contraception required

		- ≥48 hours since a pill should have been taken
			- Take the most recent missed pill immediately
			- Discard earlier missed pills
			- Continue remaining pills as usual
			- Use condoms or abstain for the next 7 days

			- If missed pills occurred in Week 1
				- Unprotected sexual intercourse (UPSI) in previous 5 days
					- Consider Emergency Contraception (EC)

			- If missed pills occurred in Week 3
				- Skip hormone-free interval
				- Finish active pills
				- Start next pack immediately
				- No pill-free break

- Missed Progestogen-Only Pill (POP)

	- Traditional POP (e.g. norethisterone, levonorgestrel)
		- >3 hours late
			- Take pill immediately
			- Continue as normal
			- Use condoms for 48 hours
			- Consider EC if UPSI occurred

	- Desogestrel POP
		- >12 hours late
			- Take pill immediately
			- Continue as normal
			- Use condoms for 48 hours
			- Consider EC if UPSI occurred

	- Drospirenone POP
		- >24 hours late
			- Take missed pill immediately
			- Continue remaining pills
			- Use backup contraception for 7 days

- AMC High-Yield MCQ Points

	- <48-hour missed COCP
		- Take immediately
		- No backup required

	- ≥48-hour missed COCP
		- Take last missed pill
		- Continue pack
		- Condoms for 7 days

	- Week 1 + missed ≥48 hours + UPSI
		- Consider EC

	- Week 3 + missed ≥48 hours
		- Skip placebo/pill-free week
		- Start next pack immediately

	- POP
		- Traditional POP → >3 hours = missed
		- Desogestrel POP → >12 hours = missed
		- Drospirenone POP → >24 hours = missed

- AMC Exam Pearl

	- COCP
		- <48-hour delay → No EC, no backup
		- ≥48-hour delay → Backup 7 days
		- Week 1 + UPSI → Consider EC
		- Week 3 → Skip pill-free interval

	- POP
		- Late beyond the allowed window → Backup required
		- Consider EC if UPSI occurred after the missed pill
