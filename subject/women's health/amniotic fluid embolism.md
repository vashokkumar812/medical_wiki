

- epidemiology
	- rare
	- pregnancy-specific emergency

- pathogenesis
	- amniotic fluid enters maternal circulation

- complications
	- acute cardiorespiratory collapse
	- severe DIC

- symptoms before collapse
	- breathlessness
	- chest pain
	- feeling cold
	- lightheadedness
	- restlessness
	- distress
	- panic
	- pins and needles in fingers
	- nausea
	- vomiting

- investigations
	- often diagnosed postmortem
	- fetal cells in maternal pulmonary capillaries

- management
	- ABC resuscitation
	- supportive care
	- intensive care
	- no specific therapy

- prognosis
	- 30% die within 1 hour
	- overall survival ~10%
