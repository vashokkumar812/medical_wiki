
- Bartholin Cyst
  - Obstruction of Bartholin gland duct


- Clinical Features
	- Located at posterior vaginal introitus
	    - 4 o'clock or 8 o'clock position

  - Bartholin Cyst
    - Unilateral vulval swelling
    - Painless
    - Fluctuant mass
    - Dyspareunia if large
    - Discomfort when sitting or walking

  - Bartholin Abscess
    - Acute painful vulval swelling
    - Tender
    - Erythematous
    - Fluctuant
    - Difficulty walking/sitting
    - Fever may occur

- Examination
  - Unilateral cystic vulval mass
  - Posterolateral introitus
  - Assess for abscess
  - Assess for cellulitis
- investigations
	- Initial Investigation
	
	  - Typical Bartholin cyst (<40 years)
	    - None required
	    - Clinical diagnosis
	
	- Additional Investigations
	
	  - STI risk factors
	    - Chlamydia NAAT
	    - Gonorrhoea NAAT
	
	  - Suspected infection
	    - Swab/culture of discharge or pus
	
	- Most Appropriate Investigation
	
	  - Age ≥40 years
	    - Biopsy or excision biopsy
	    - Exclude Bartholin gland carcinoma

- Management

	  - Small asymptomatic cyst
	    - Reassurance
	    - Observation
	
	- Initial Treatment
	
	  - Symptomatic Bartholin cyst
	    - Word catheter insertion
	
	- Most Appropriate Treatment
	
	  - Word catheter insertion
	    - Creates continuous drainage tract
	    - Lower recurrence than simple I&D
	
	- Recurrent Symptomatic Cyst
	
	  - Most Appropriate Treatment
	    - Marsupialisation
	
	- Bartholin Abscess
	
	  - Initial Treatment
	    - Incision and drainage + Word catheter
	
	  - Most Appropriate Treatment
	    - Word catheter placement
	
	  - Antibiotics
	    - Not routinely required
	    - Indications
	      - Cellulitis
	      - Systemic symptoms
	      - Immunocompromised patient
	      - STI suspected
	
	- Recurrent Abscess
	
	  - Most Appropriate Treatment
	    - Marsupialisation
	
	- Persistent/Recurrent Disease
	
	  - Definitive Treatment
	    - Excision of Bartholin gland
	
	- Important Age Rule
	
	  - New Bartholin mass in woman ≥40 years
	    - Bartholin gland carcinoma until proven otherwise
	
	  - Initial Investigation
	    - Biopsy
	
	  - Most Appropriate Investigation
	    - Excision biopsy

- Complications
  - Recurrence
  - Abscess formation
  - Dyspareunia
  - Cellulitis
