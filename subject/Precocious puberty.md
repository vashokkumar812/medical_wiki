# Precocious Puberty

- Definition
	- Development of secondary sexual characteristics before
		- Girls
			- <8 years
		- Boys
			- <9 years

- Classification
	- Central precocious puberty
		- Gonadotropin-dependent
		- Early activation of hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal axis
	- Peripheral precocious puberty
		- Gonadotropin-independent
		- Excess sex steroids independent of HPG axis

- Epidemiology
	- More common in girls
	- Girls
		- Usually idiopathic
	- Boys
		- More likely pathological

- Clinical features
	- Girls
		- Breast development
		- Pubic hair
		- Growth acceleration
		- Menstruation
	- Boys
		- Testicular enlargement
		- Penile enlargement
		- Pubic hair
		- Voice deepening
		- Growth acceleration
	- Both
		- Acne
		- Body odour
		- Advanced bone age
		- Behavioural changes

- Central precocious puberty
	- Causes
		- Idiopathic
		- Hypothalamic hamartoma
		- CNS tumour
		- CNS infection
		- Hydrocephalus
		- Head trauma
		- Cranial irradiation
	- Findings
		- Progressive symmetrical puberty
		- Increased growth velocity
		- Advanced bone age
		- Enlarged testes in boys

- Peripheral precocious puberty
	- Causes
		- Congenital adrenal hyperplasia
		- Adrenal tumour
		- Ovarian tumour
		- Testicular tumour
		- McCune-Albright syndrome
		- Exogenous sex steroids
		- Severe hypothyroidism
	- Findings
		- Incomplete or atypical puberty
		- Virilisation
		- Small testes despite virilisation

- Differential diagnoses
	- Premature thelarche
		- Isolated breast development
		- No pubic hair
		- Normal growth velocity
		- Normal bone age
	- Premature adrenarche
		- Isolated pubic or axillary hair
		- No breast or testicular enlargement
		- Mildly advanced bone age
	- Obesity
		- Lipomastia
		- No glandular breast tissue
	- Congenital adrenal hyperplasia
		- Virilisation
		- Ambiguous genitalia
		- Elevated 17-hydroxyprogesterone

- Investigations
	- Bone age X-ray
		- Usually advanced
	- Hormonal tests
		- LH
		- FSH
		- Estradiol
		- Testosterone
		- DHEAS
		- 17-hydroxyprogesterone
		- TSH
	- GnRH stimulation test
		- Pubertal LH response
			- Central precocious puberty
	- MRI brain
		- Boys
		- Girls <6 years
		- Neurological symptoms
	- Pelvic ultrasound
		- Ovarian pathology
		- Uterine enlargement

- Management
	- Central precocious puberty
		- GnRH analogues
			- Leuprolide
		- Treat underlying CNS lesion
	- Peripheral precocious puberty
		- Treat underlying cause
			- Tumour removal
			- Steroid therapy for CAH
	- Psychological support

- Complications
	- Reduced adult height
	- Early epiphyseal closure
	- Psychological distress
