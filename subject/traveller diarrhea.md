
- Diagnostic Clues

	- Hepatosplenomegaly
		- Consider malaria

	- Thyroid Signs
		- Consider hyperthyroidism

- Investigations

	- Stool Testing
		- First-line investigation

	- Key Principles
		- Unformed or liquid fresh stool preferred
		- Collect specimen close to laboratory delivery
		- Preserve in SAF fixative if needed
		- Store at room temperature
		- Multiple specimens improve detection

	- Recommended Stool Collection Timing
		- 1st stool
			- Day 0
		- 2nd stool
			- Day 2–3
		- 3rd stool
			- Day 5–6


	- Full Blood Count
		- Eosinophilia
	- Strongyloides Serology
		- Detect helminth infection

	- Blood Cultures
		- If fever present
- Red Flags for Specialist Referral
	- Change in bowel habit with bleeding
	- Family history of colorectal cancer
	- Abdominal mass
	- Rectal mass
	- Suspected inflammatory bowel disease
	- Suspected coeliac disease
