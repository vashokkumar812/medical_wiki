- Minor heat illnesses
	- Sunburn
		- UV exposure
		- Redness/pain after 4–6 h
		- Blistering sunburn
			- ↑ melanoma/BCC risk

		- Management
			- Symptomatic
			- Aloe vera/vitamin E creams
			- Leave blisters intact
			- Severe blistering >5% BSA
				- Consider hospital referral

		- Prevention
			- Sunscreen
			- UV-protective clothing
			- Avoid peak sun

	- Miliaria rubra
		- Sweat gland obstruction
		- Pruritic papulovesicular rash
		- Covered skin areas

		- Management
			- Keep cool/dry
			- Air exposure

	- Heat oedema
		- Dependent hand/foot swelling
		- Benign/self-limiting

	- Heat syncope
		- Vasodilation + pooling
		- Vasovagal syncope

		- Management
			- Fluids
			- Rest
			- Exclude cardiac causes/dehydration

	- Heat cramps
		- After prolonged exertion
		- Large muscles
			- Hamstrings
			- Quadriceps
			- Gastrocnemius

		- Management
			- Fluids/electrolytes
			- Stretching
			- Massage
			- Rest

- Heat exhaustion
	- Pathophysiology
		- Failure of heat homeostasis
		- Water/electrolyte depletion

	- Features
		- Tachycardia
		- Tachypnoea
		- Weakness
		- Nausea/vomiting
		- Profuse sweating
		- Syncope
		- Myalgia
		- Flushing
		- Headache
		- Fatigue

	- Key findings
		- Core temp <40°C
		- Normal mental status

	- Management
		- Cooling
		- Oral/IV fluids
		- Electrolyte replacement
		- Remove clothing
		- Cool environment
		- Avoid shivering
		- Hospital transfer if significant

- Heat stroke
	- Medical emergency
	- Core temp >40°C
	- Altered mental state
		- Confusion
		- Ataxia
		- Seizures
		- Coma

	- Pathophysiology
		- Failure of cooling mechanisms
		- SIRS
		- DIC
		- Multiorgan failure

	- Organ involvement
		- Brain
			- Cerebellar dysfunction
		- Liver
			- Elevated LFTs
		- Kidney injury
		- Bowel ischaemia
		- Cardiomyopathy

	- Types
		- Exertional
			- Athletes/workers
		- Classic
			- Elderly/comorbid/socially isolated

	- Poor prognostic signs
		- Seizures
		- Elevated creatinine
		- Coagulopathy
		- Elevated LFTs
		- Persistent altered consciousness

	- Management
		- ABCs
		- Rapid cooling
		- Oral/IV rehydration
		- Urgent hospital transfer

- Indirect heat effects
	- ↑ MI/stroke/arrhythmia
	- ↑ AKI
	- ↑ Mental health exacerbations
	- ↑ Suicide/violence
	- ↑ Domestic violence
	- ↑ Preterm birth

- Cooling strategies
	- Avoid outdoor activity during hottest hours
	- Light clothing
	- Hydration
	- Fans
		- Feel ~4°C cooler
		- Less effective >40°C
	- Air conditioning
	- Wet clothing/towels
	- Self-dousing
	- Foot immersion
	- Identify cooling refuges

| Feature | Heat exhaustion | Heat stroke |
|---|---|---|
| Core temperature | <40°C | >40°C |
| CNS status | Normal | Altered CNS status |
| Hepatocellular dysfunction | Absent | Present |
| Coagulopathy | Absent | May be present |
