
| Feature                    | **Dissociative Amnesia**                                   | **Dissociative Identity Disorder (DID)**                 | **Depersonalization/Derealization Disorder (DPDR)**                                      |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Core defect**            | Inability to recall important autobiographical memories    | ≥2 distinct identities repeatedly control behaviour      | Feeling detached from self and/or surroundings                                           |
| **Memory**                 | Amnesia (selective or generalized)                         | Amnesia between identity states                          | Memory intact                                                                            |
| **Identity**               | Intact                                                     | Multiple identities                                      | Identity intact                                                                          |
| **Reality testing**        | Intact                                                     | Usually intact                                           | **Intact (key feature)**                                                                 |
| **Consciousness**          | Normal                                                     | Normal                                                   | Normal                                                                                   |
| **Trigger**                | Psychological trauma/stress                                | Severe childhood trauma (especially sexual abuse)        | Stress, anxiety                                                                          |
| **Typical onset**          | After traumatic event                                      | Childhood (diagnosed years later)                        | Adolescence/early adulthood                                                              |
| **Characteristic symptom** | Memory gaps                                                | Alternate personalities ("alters")                       | **Out-of-body experience** (depersonalization); surroundings feel unreal (derealization) |
| **Special feature**        | Fugue → purposeful travel + amnesia                        | Different identities have different behaviours/memories  | Déjà vu, jamais vu may occur                                                             |
| **Associated disorders**   | Mood disorders, conversion disorder, personality disorders | PTSD, BPD, depression, substance abuse, eating disorders | Anxiety symptoms common                                                                  |
| **Course**                 | May resolve, recur, or become chronic                      | Fluctuating or chronic                                   | Episodic or persistent                                                                   |
| **Treatment**              | Psychotherapy + remove stressor ± hypnosis/relaxation      | Long-term psychotherapy                                  | Psychotherapy (reduce anxiety)                                                           |
| **Major rule-outs**        | Head injury, seizure, stroke, alcohol/drugs                | BPD, bipolar disorder, malingering, factitious disorder  | Substance use, panic disorder, PTSD                                                      |

## Rapid Recognition (AMC)

| Presentation | Diagnosis |
|--------------|-----------|
| Cannot remember traumatic event after severe stress | **Dissociative amnesia** |
| Memory loss + unexplained travel/wandering | **Dissociative amnesia with fugue** |
| Multiple identities with memory gaps | **Dissociative identity disorder** |
| "I feel outside my body." | **Depersonalization** |
| "The world feels unreal or dream-like." | **Derealization** |
| Detached feeling but knows it is not real | **Depersonalization/Derealization disorder** |
