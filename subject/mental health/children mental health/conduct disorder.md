
- Definition
	- At least 3 of 15 specified behaviours occurring during the preceding 12 months, with at least one occurring during the preceding 6 months, causing clinically significant impairment in social, academic or occupational functioning
	- Violates rights of others or societal rules

- Epidemiology
	- ~4%
	- Boys > girls

- Clinical Features

	- Aggression
		- Bullying
		- Fighting
		- Cruelty
		- Sexual assault

	- Property destruction
		- Fire setting
		- Vandalism

	- Deceit/Theft
		- Lying
		- Stealing
		- Robbery

	- Serious rule violations
		- Truancy
		- Running away

- Associated Conditions
	- ADHD
	- Substance abuse
	- School failure

- Management
	- Family therapy
	- Behavioural programs
	- Structured activities
	- Treat comorbid ADHD

- Outcome
	- Risk of antisocial personality disorder ⭐

- Differential Diagnosis
	- ODD
	- ADHD
