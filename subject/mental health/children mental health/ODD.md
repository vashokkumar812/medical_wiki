## definition
- Persistent pattern of angry/irritable mood, defiant behaviour or vindictiveness.

## Diagnostic criteria
- Duration: 
	- ≥ 6 months.
- Symptoms: ≥ 4 of the following 8 symptoms, from at least one category
	- Angry/irritable mood
		- Loses temper.
		- Easily annoyed or touchy.
		- Angry and resentful.
	- Argumentative/defiant behaviour
		- Argues with authority figures.
	- Defies or refuses to comply with rules/requests.
		- Deliberately annoys others.
	- Blames others for mistakes or misbehaviour.
		- Vindictiveness


## Epidemiology
- ~3%
- Boys > girls before puberty



## Associated Conditions
- ADHD
- Learning disorders
- Low self-esteem
- Family conflict

## Management
- Parent management training ⭐
- Consistent discipline
- Positive reinforcement
- Family therapy

## Complication
- May progress to conduct disorder

## Differential Diagnosis
- Conduct disorder
