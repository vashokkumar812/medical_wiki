# Anxiety Disorders (AMC MCQ Notes)

- Anxiety
	- Definition
		- Excessive fear and worry with psychological and autonomic symptoms

	- Psychological Symptoms
		- Excessive worry
		- Restlessness
		- Hypervigilance
		- Poor concentration
		- Irritability
		- Sleep disturbance

	- Physical Symptoms
		- Palpitations
		- Tremor
		- Sweating
		- Hyperventilation
		- Dry mouth
		- Muscle tension

---

# Panic Disorder

- Definition
	- Recurrent unexpected panic attacks
	- Persistent concern about future attacks or behavioural change

- Epidemiology
	- Women > men
	- Usual onset: 20–30 years

- Panic Attack
	- Abrupt onset
	- Peaks within minutes
	- Usually lasts 10–30 minutes

- Symptoms
	- Palpitations
	- Chest pain
	- Dyspnoea
	- Tremor
	- Sweating
	- Dizziness
	- Nausea
	- Paresthesia
	- Fear of dying
	- Fear of losing control

- Associated Condition
	- Agoraphobia ⭐
		 - Fear of situations where escape may be difficult

- Initial Assessment
	- ECG ⭐
	- Blood glucose
	- Exclude ACS, arrhythmia, hyperthyroidism, PE, substance use

- Management

	- Acute attack
		- Reassurance
		- Calm environment
		- Short-term benzodiazepine if severe

	- Long-term
		- CBT ⭐
		- SSRI (first-line) ⭐
			- Sertraline
			- Escitalopram
		- SNRI alternative

---

# Generalised Anxiety Disorder (GAD)

- Definition
	- Excessive anxiety and worry
	- ≥6 months ⭐
	- Difficult to control

- Clinical Features
	- Excessive worry
	- Restlessness
	- Fatigue
	- Poor concentration
	- Irritability
	- Muscle tension
	- Sleep disturbance

- Initial Assessment
	- Exclude medical causes
		- Hyperthyroidism
		- Arrhythmia
		- Substance use

- Management
	- CBT ⭐
	- SSRI (first-line) ⭐
	- SNRI alternative
	- Buspirone
	- Benzodiazepines
		- Short-term only

---

# Specific Phobia

- Definition
	- Excessive fear of a specific object or situation
	- Immediate anxiety on exposure
	- Avoidance behaviour

- Common Types
	- Animals
	- Heights
	- Flying
	- Blood/injections
	- Storms

- Management
	- Exposure therapy ⭐
	- CBT

---

# Social Anxiety Disorder

- Definition
	- Fear of embarrassment or negative evaluation in social situations

- Clinical Features
	- Public speaking anxiety
	- Eating in public
	- Social avoidance
	- Performance anxiety

- Management
	- CBT ⭐
	- SSRI (first-line)
	- Beta-blocker (e.g. propranolol)
		- Performance anxiety only ⭐

---

# Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder (OCD)

- Definition
	- Obsessions
		 - Intrusive unwanted thoughts
	- Compulsions
		 - Repetitive behaviours to reduce anxiety

- Common Obsessions
	- Contamination
	- Harm
	- Doubt
	- Sexual thoughts
	- Religious thoughts

- Common Compulsions
	- Hand washing
	- Checking
	- Counting
	- Cleaning
	- Ordering

- Clinical Features
	- Insight usually preserved
	- Time consuming (>1 hour/day)

- Initial Assessment
	- Screen for depression
	- Suicide risk
	- Functional impairment

- Management
	- Exposure and response prevention (ERP) ⭐
	- CBT
	- SSRI (high dose) ⭐
		 - Fluoxetine
		 - Sertraline
		 - Escitalopram
	- Clomipramine
		 - Second-line

---

# Acute Stress Disorder (ASD)

- Definition
	- Trauma-related disorder
	- Symptoms:
		- ≥3 days
		- <1 month ⭐
	- Begins within 1 month of trauma

- Symptoms
	- Intrusive memories
	- Flashbacks
	- Nightmares
	- Avoidance
	- Hyperarousal
	- Dissociation

- Management
	- Trauma-focused CBT ⭐
	- Supportive care
	- Short-term medication if required

---

# Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD)

- Definition
	- Symptoms >1 month following trauma ⭐

- Diagnostic Criteria

	- Exposure to traumatic event

	- Intrusion
		- Flashbacks
		- Nightmares
		- Intrusive memories

	- Avoidance
		- Thoughts
		- People
		- Places

	- Negative mood/cognition
		- Guilt
		- Emotional numbing
		- Detachment

	- Hyperarousal
		- Hypervigilance
		- Exaggerated startle
		- Irritability
		- Poor sleep

- Management

	- First-line
		- Trauma-focused CBT ⭐

	- Pharmacological
		- SSRI ⭐
			- Sertraline
			- Paroxetine
			- Fluoxetine

		- Prazosin
			- Trauma-related nightmares ⭐

		- Benzodiazepines
			- Avoid routine use ⭐

# Quick Comparison

| Disorder | Key Diagnostic Feature | First-line Treatment |
|-----------|------------------------|----------------------|
| **Panic Disorder** | Recurrent unexpected panic attacks | CBT + SSRI |
| **GAD** | Excessive worry ≥6 months | CBT + SSRI |
| **Specific Phobia** | Fear of specific object/situation | Exposure therapy |
| **Social Anxiety** | Fear of embarrassment | CBT + SSRI |
| **OCD** | Obsessions + compulsions | ERP + SSRI |
| **ASD** | Trauma symptoms 3 days–1 month | Trauma-focused CBT |
| **PTSD** | Trauma symptoms >1 month | Trauma-focused CBT + SSRI |

---

# AMC Pearls ⭐

- Panic attack lasts **minutes**, not hours.
- Panic disorder commonly coexists with **agoraphobia**.
- **CBT + SSRI** are first-line for most anxiety disorders.
- **ERP** is the treatment of choice for OCD.
- **Exposure therapy** is first-line for specific phobia.
- **Propranolol** is useful only for **performance anxiety**.
- PTSD requires symptoms **>1 month**.
- ASD lasts **3 days–1 month**.
- **Prazosin** reduces PTSD-related nightmares.
- Avoid long-term benzodiazepines due to dependence.

# Generalised Anxiety Disorder (GAD)

- Definition
	- Excessive, persistent anxiety and worry
	- Difficult to control
	- Present for **≥6 months** ⭐
	- Causes functional impairment

- Epidemiology
	- Lifetime prevalence: ~5%
	- Women > men
	- Usually begins in childhood or early adulthood
	- Chronic course with exacerbations during stress

- Risk Factors
	- Family history
	- Genetic predisposition
	- Chronic stress
	- Personality traits (high neuroticism)

- Clinical Features

	- Psychological
		- Excessive worry
		- Feeling "on edge"
		- Poor concentration
		- Irritability

	- Physical
		- Muscle tension ⭐
		- Fatigue
		- Sleep disturbance
		- Restlessness

	- Associated Disorders
		- Depression
		- Somatic symptom disorders
		- Substance misuse

- Initial Assessment
	- Screen for:
		- Depression
		- Suicide risk
		- Substance use
	- Exclude medical causes
		- Hyperthyroidism
		- Arrhythmia
		- Phaeochromocytoma
		- Medication/substance-induced anxiety

- Management

	- Non-pharmacological (First-line)
		- CBT ⭐
		- Relaxation therapy
		- Stress management
		- Sleep hygiene

	- Pharmacological
		- First-line ⭐
			- SSRIs
				- Sertraline
				- Escitalopram
				- Fluoxetine
		- Alternatives
			- SNRI (Venlafaxine, Duloxetine)
			- Buspirone
		- Short-term only
			- Benzodiazepines
				- Severe acute anxiety
				- Avoid long-term use

- Prognosis
	- Often chronic
	- Good response with CBT + SSRI

---

## AMC Pearls ⭐

- **Duration ≥6 months** distinguishes GAD from normal anxiety.
- **Muscle tension + excessive worry** are classic features.
- **CBT + SSRI** are first-line treatment.
- **Buspirone** is useful for chronic GAD (non-sedating, no dependence).
- **Avoid long-term benzodiazepines** due to tolerance and dependence.
