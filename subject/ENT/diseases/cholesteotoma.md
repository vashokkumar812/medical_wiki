cholesteatoma
- definition
	- abnormal growth of keratinizing squamous epithelium into:
		- middle ear
		- mastoid air cells

- epidemiology
	- most common destructive ear disease
	- congenital
		- around 5 years
		- male predominance
	- acquired
		- most common type in adults




- risk factors
	- chronic Eustachian tube dysfunction
	- childhood otitis media
	- repeated ventilation tube insertion
	- cleft palate
	- Down syndrome

- clinical features
	- onset
		- insidious
		- slowly progressive
	- symptoms
		- conductive hearing loss
			- early symptom
			- may be minimal despite large lesion
			- sensorineural hearing loss → inner ear involvement
		- foul-smelling persistent otorrhoea
			- early symptom
			- often after water exposure
		- chronic otitis media refractory to antibiotics
		- otalgia
			- uncommon
			- suggests complications if present
		- vertigo
			- labyrinthine involvement
		- facial weakness/twitching
			- facial nerve involvement

	- examination
		- otoscopy (most important)
			- retraction pocket in
				- pars flaccida (attic)
				- posterosuperior quadrant
			- keratin debris
			- white pearly mass
			- granulation tissue
			- marginal perforation
			- bone erosion
		- granulation polyp
			- cholesteatoma until proven otherwise
			- do not remove blindly
				- risk of damaging
					- ossicles
					- facial nerve
		- fistula test
			- positive pressure applied to ear canal
			- positive
				- vertigo
				- nystagmus
			- suggests labyrinthine fistula

- unsafe tympanic membrane perforation
	- superior perforation
	- posterior perforation
	- marginal perforation
	- granulation tissue
	- white middle ear mass
	- bone erosion



- investigations
	- audiogram
		- conductive hearing loss
		- sensorineural hearing loss → inner ear involvement
	- CT temporal bone (investigation of choice)
		- soft tissue mass with bone erosion
		- may show erosion of
			- ossicles
			- mastoid septae
			- facial nerve canal
			- inner ear structures
	- MRI
		- if intracranial extension suspected

- management
	- all cholesteatomas require treatment
	- obstructing granulation polyp
		- antibiotic-steroid ear drops for 1 week
	- definitive
		- mastoidectomy + tympanoplasty ± ossicular reconstruction
		- may require
			- ossiculoplasty
			- meatoplasty
		- intraoperative facial nerve monitoring
	- conservative
		- regular microscopic keratin debridement
		- indications
			- elderly
			- high surgical risk
			- only hearing ear
		- lifelong follow-up
