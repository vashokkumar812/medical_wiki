- Definition

	- Obesity in pregnancy
		- BMI ≥30 kg/m²

	- WHO BMI classification

		- Underweight
			- <18.5

		- Normal
			- 18.5–24.9

		- Overweight
			- 25–29.9

		- Obese class I
			- 30–34.9

		- Obese class II
			- 35–39.9

		- Obese class III
			- ≥40

- Complications

	- Antenatal risks
		- Miscarriage
		- Congenital anomalies
			- Neural tube defects
		- Gestational diabetes
		- [[hypertensive disorders in pregnancy#1. Preeclampsia|Pre-eclampsia]]
		- Stillbirth
		- Thromboembolism
		- Preterm birth
		- Obstructive sleep apnoea

	- Intrapartum risks
		- Prolonged labour
		- Failed induction
		- Instrumental delivery
		- Shoulder dystocia
		- Caesarean section
		- Postpartum haemorrhage

	- Postpartum risks
		- Wound infection
		- Thromboembolism
		- Breastfeeding difficulties
		- Postnatal depression

	- Risks increase with higher BMI class

- Gestational weight gain recommendations

	- Normal BMI
		- 11.5–16 kg

	- Overweight
		- 6.8–11.3 kg

	- Obese
		- 5–9 kg

- Diagnosis

	- BMI measurement
		- At first antenatal visit
		- Before 10–12 weeks

- Management

	- Non-pharmacological

		- Explain
			- Pregnancy risks
			- Risk reduction strategies

		- Nutritional supplementation
			- Folic acid 5 mg
			- Iodine 150 µg daily
			- Due to increased neural tube defect risk

	- Screening

		- Early gestational diabetes screening

		- Repeat screening at 28 weeks
			- If early test normal

		- NIPT counselling
			- Higher no-result rate
			- Due to low fetal fraction

	- Vaccinations
		- Influenza vaccine
		- COVID-19 vaccine
		- Due to increased maternal morbidity risk

	- [[hypertensive disorders in pregnancy#1. Preeclampsia|Pre-eclampsia]] prevention
		- Calcium 1500–2000 mg daily
		- Low-dose [[aspirin]]
			- 100–150 mg daily
		- Start at 12–16 weeks
		- Stop around 36 weeks

	- Fetal surveillance
		- Serial third-trimester growth ultrasounds

	- Anaesthetic review
		- Recommended antenatally
		- Especially if BMI >40

	- Labour and delivery considerations
		- Labour often prolonged
			- Especially latent phase
		- Emergency CS risk increases with BMI
		- Higher failed induction rates
		- Prepare for
			- Shoulder dystocia
			- Postpartum haemorrhage

	- Timing of delivery
		- BMI ≥50
			- Offer delivery before due date

- AMC exam pearls
	- BMI ≥30 = obesity in pregnancy
	- Folic acid 5 mg recommended
	- Early GDM screening required
	- Serial growth scans recommended
	- Aspirin 100–150 mg for pre-eclampsia prevention
	- BMI ≥50
		- Consider delivery before due date
