			- Short segment Barrett’s (<3 cm), no dysplasia

			- Long segment Barrett’s (≥3 cm), no dysplasia

	- Diagnosis
		- Eosinophilic oesophagitis

	- MCQ/high-yield points
		- Diagnostic criterion

		- Common associations
