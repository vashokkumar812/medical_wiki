  * Onset  
    * When did you first notice the hearing loss?  
    * Was it sudden or gradual?  
    * Did it develop over minutes, hours, days, weeks or months?  
    * Was it present since childhood?  
  * Duration and progression  
    * How long have you had this problem?  
    * Is it getting worse, improving or remaining stable?  
    * Is it constant or intermittent?  
  * Laterality  
    * Is the right ear, left ear or both ears affected?  
    * If both ears are affected, did it start on one side or both sides?  
  * Severity and functional impact  
    * Can you hear normal conversation?  
    * Do you struggle to understand speech in noisy environments?  
    * Do you frequently ask people to repeat themselves?  
    * Does it affect your work, family or social activities?  
  * Associated symptoms  
    * Ear pain  
    * Ear discharge  
    * Tinnitus  
    * Vertigo or imbalance  
    * Ear blockage  
    * Facial weakness or numbness  
  * Previous management  
    * Previous audiometry or hearing assessment  
    * Hearing aids or ear surgery  
    * Previous treatment and response  
    * Sign language or communication support
