

  * Onset, duration and progression  
    * When did the pain begin?  
    * Was it sudden or gradual?  
    * Is it constant or intermittent?  
  * Site and character  
    * Which ear is affected?  
    * Is the pain superficial or deep?  
    * Is it sharp, dull, throbbing or burning?  
  * Severity and radiation  
    * Severity on a scale of 0–10  
    * Radiation to the jaw, teeth, throat or neck  
  * Aggravating and relieving factors  
    * Does pulling the pinna worsen the pain?  
    * Does chewing or swallowing aggravate it?  
    * Does water entering the ear worsen it?  
    * What relieves the pain?  
  * Associated symptoms  
    * Fever  
    * Hearing loss  
    * Discharge  
    * Itching  
    * Tinnitus or vertigo  
    * Swelling around the ear  
  * Risk factors  
    * Swimming or water exposure  
    * Cotton bud use or ear instrumentation  
    * Earbud or hearing aid use  
    * Foreign body insertion  
    * Diabetes or immunosuppression
