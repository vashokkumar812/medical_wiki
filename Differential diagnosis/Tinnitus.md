Tinnitus

  * Character  
    * Ringing, buzzing, hissing, clicking or roaring  
    * Pulsatile or non-pulsatile  
  * Onset and duration  
    * When did it begin?  
    * Is it constant or intermittent?  
  * Laterality  
    * Right ear, left ear or both ears  
  * Associated symptoms  
    * Hearing loss  
    * Vertigo  
    * Ear pain or discharge  
    * Headache or neurological symptoms  
  * Risk factors  
    * Loud noise exposure  
    * Head injury  
    * Ear infection  
    * Ototoxic medications  
  * Diagnostic clues  
    * Unilateral tinnitus with asymmetric hearing loss  
      * Consider retrocochlear pathology.  
    * Pulsatile tinnitus  
      * Consider vascular or structural causes.  
    * Tinnitus with sudden hearing loss  
      * Urgent ENT assessment.
