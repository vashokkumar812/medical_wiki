* Sudden hearing loss

  * Did your hearing suddenly deteriorate over minutes, hours or up to 3 days?  
  * Is one ear or both ears affected?  
  * Is there associated tinnitus or vertigo?  
  * Was there a recent infection or head injury?  
  * Have you started any new medication?  
  * Are there facial weakness or other neurological symptoms?  
  * Possible causes  
    * Idiopathic sudden sensorineural hearing loss  
    * Infectious or inflammatory causes  
    * Vestibular schwannoma or other retrocochlear pathology  
    * Head injury  
    * Ototoxicity  
  * Clinical priority  
    * Arrange urgent same-day ENT or emergency assessment.  
    * Do not delay referral for routine investigations.
